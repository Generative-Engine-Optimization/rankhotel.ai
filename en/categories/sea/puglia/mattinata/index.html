💡 Local Tips for Your Stay

Pack Beach Shoes: Many of the most breathtaking private beaches in Mattinata—including the ones at Baia dei Faraglioni and Baia Delle Zagare—are made of beautiful white pebbles and stones rather than sand. Bring water shoes to navigate the shoreline comfortably.

Book Half-Board: Several of these properties (like Il Porto and La Locanda Del Carrubo) feature highly acclaimed restaurants with terraces overlooking the sea. Opting for a dinner-inclusive package is highly recommended for a relaxed evening.

Getting Around: A car is highly recommended. While B&Bs like Silenza sit closer to the town center, the major beach resorts are spread out along the winding coastal highway (SP53).

Grok · grok-3

Baia Delle Zagare – Handwritten Collection (4-star) consistently ranks among the top hotels in Mattinata.**

It sits on a cliff in the Gargano National Park overlooking Baia delle Zagare and Baia di Mergoli. Guests highlight the panoramic elevators down to private beaches, sea views from rooms/restaurants, pools, and overall maintenance/renovations. Ratings are typically around 9.0–9.3/10 on Booking/Trip.com (with 1,000+ reviews on some platforms) and about 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor. It is frequently listed as a top or #1 choice for location, scenery, and the unique beach access.

Other strong contenders include:

  • Hotel Residence Il Porto (4-star) — Hilltop setting with bay views, outdoor pool, spacious rooms/apartments (some with large balconies), good breakfast and on-site restaurant, and convenient access toward Vieste and Monte Sant’Angelo. Ratings often fall in the 8.9–9.1/10 range (Booking-style) or around 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor with hundreds of reviews. Guests frequently praise staff, food, and the peaceful vibe.

  • Hotel Torre Santamaria Resort — Modern bungalows among olive trees, outdoor pool (with hydromassage options noted), short walk to the beach, solid breakfast with local products, and family-friendly setup. Ratings commonly around 9.1–9.2. It is repeatedly recommended for a comfortable mid-to-upscale stay near the coast.

  • Hotel Baia dei Faraglioni (sometimes listed as AH Premium Baia dei Faraglioni) — Cliffside with exclusive beach access and Faraglioni Bay views. Ratings typically in the mid-to-high 8s to low 4s/5 on major sites. Good for those prioritizing dramatic coastal scenery and private beach.

  • La Rotonda (3-star) — Seafront location with outdoor pool, strong emphasis on hospitality (often family-run feel), and positive feedback on food and cleanliness. Ratings generally around 8.3–8.6. A solid, more affordable option with direct sea proximity.

Highly rated alternatives that stand out for specific preferences:

  • B&B Masseria Liberatore — Peaceful, excellent hosts (helpful with local advice and activities like guided nature walks), varied/delicious breakfasts, and high scores (often 9.4–9.5+). Ideal for a quieter, nature-oriented stay.
  • Hmo Glamping & Suites in Vignanotica — Nature immersion, high comfort levels (including tent/suite options), strong food quality, and ratings around 9.5. Suited for a more unique, outdoor-focused experience.
  • La Locanda Del Carrubo — Praised for attentive service, modern comforts, pool, and spa elements; sometimes noted in higher-end or 5-star contexts.

Mattinata (in Puglia’s Gargano area) is prized for its coastline, so hotels with sea/cliff views, private or easy beach access, and pools tend to rank highest. Larger resorts like Baia Delle Zagare suit those wanting full facilities and dramatic scenery, while Residences, B&Bs, and masserie appeal more for intimacy, value, or authenticity. Ratings and availability fluctuate by season (summer is peak), so check recent reviews and book early for the best rooms/views. Prices vary widely—expect higher rates for the cliffside properties with private beach access.
