Gallipoli, Italy (in Puglia) is the main tourist destination matching this query—a historic coastal town known for its old town, beaches (like Spiaggia della Purità), and Ionian Sea setting. (There is also a Gallipoli/Gelibolu in Turkey linked to WWI battlefields, with fewer high-end hotel options focused more on practical stays near the sites.)

Here are some of the consistently top-rated and recommended hotels in Gallipoli, Italy, based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and travel guides (ratings and popularity as of 2025–2026 data; always verify current scores and availability, as they fluctuate seasonally).

Luxury / Spa & Modern Highlights

  • Biancodonda Greenblu Hotel & Spa — Frequently ranked among the very best (often ~4.9/5 on Tripadvisor, ~9.1–9.4 on Booking/others). Modern design, rooftop pool with sea views, spa facilities (sauna, hammam, etc.), fitness center, strong staff praise, and a convenient location near the historic center/old town bridge. Ideal for a splurge with comfort and amenities.
  • Grand Hotel Costa Brada — Highly rated (~4.7/5), beachfront with private beach access, good for sea views and relaxation. Praised for service, food, and facilities; a strong choice if you prioritize direct beach access.
  • Torre Assunta Hotel — Upscale option with outdoor pool, spa elements, and solid guest feedback for comfort and value among higher-end properties.

Stylish Boutique & Historic Charm

  • Palazzo Colombo — Chic restored palazzo in/near the old town with sea views, atmospheric design, and high ratings for style and location (close to key sights and beaches).
  • Pascaraymondo Suite Palace — Exceptional guest scores (often near 9.9), stylish suites, terrace, and proximity to Spiaggia della Purità.
  • POPULA - The Lifestyle Hotel — Modern and trendy with strong ratings (~9.5+ from many reviews), good location, quality rooms, breakfast, and overall experience.
  • Relais Corte Palmieri & Il Chiostro — Historic residence with character, pool options in some reports, and positive feedback on service and location near restaurants/shopping.

Strong Value / Charming B&Bs

  • B&B Corte Casole — Frequently praised as a top-value pick in the historic center: clean, comfortable rooms (some with outdoor space/terrace), personalized service, and excellent location. High traveler ratings.
  • Faro Bianco Gallipoli - Suites & Apartments — Top or near-top on many Tripadvisor lists (~4.8/5), clean suites, rooftop terrace/breakfast, and central location with easy access to beaches and the old town.

Tips:
Prices vary widely by season (summer peak is more expensive; shoulder seasons offer better value). Many properties are in or near the historic center (great for walking) or slightly outside for beach/pool focus. Free or nearby parking can be limited in the old town, so check that. Book via major platforms for current rates, photos, and cancellation policies.
