Quick Tips

  • Location focus: Torre dell’Orso is a popular beach town in Salento (Puglia) known for its sandy beach, clear water, and the 16th-century tower. Hotels right by the water (like Belvedere) or a short walk/drive away score highest for convenience. Nearby attractions include Baia dei Turchi, Grotta della Poesia, and Roca Vecchia.
  • Prices: Expect variation by season (higher in summer peak). Mid-range options often start around €100–200+ per night for a double room; luxury or high-season stays cost more. Always check current rates on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotel sites, as they fluctuate.
  • Other strong mentions: Camere le Vie di Omero (very close to the beach, solid ratings ~9.0), Lumina Rooms (beachfront, high scores but fewer reviews), and various smaller B&Bs or masserie for a more intimate experience.
  • What guests praise most: Location and sea views (especially Belvedere), helpful staff, cleanliness, pools/gardens, and local food. Common notes include that the main beach can get crowded in high season.
