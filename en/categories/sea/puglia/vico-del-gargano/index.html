Park Hotel Villa Maria (in San Menaio, part of the Vico del Gargano municipality) consistently ranks among the top options.

It is a 3-star Art Nouveau-style villa (early 1900s) set in a pine forest/garden area, about 250 meters from the beach (with private beach access or shuttle in some accounts). Guests frequently praise the elegant setting with sea/garden views, excellent restaurant (especially fresh fish and Apulian specialties), helpful staff, cleanliness, spacious rooms, and overall atmosphere. Ratings typically fall in the 9.1–9.3/10 range on Booking.com (hundreds of reviews) and around 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor. Amenities include free parking, Wi-Fi, restaurant, bar, and sometimes pool or beach facilities. It is a strong choice for couples or those seeking a refined, nature-oriented stay near the sea.

Verde Matematico is another highly rated pick (often 9.1–9.2/10 on Booking.com with ~180+ reviews). This B&B/guesthouse sits in a peaceful countryside setting amid olive groves/hills, roughly 1.5 km from the historic center and a few kilometers from Calenella beach. It offers comfortable, stylish rooms (some with sea/garden views or terraces), free private parking, garden/terrace, Wi-Fi, and breakfast. Guests highlight the idyllic location, kind staff, value, and connection to the agricultural estate (they produce high-quality extra-virgin olive oil). Ideal for a quiet, nature-focused stay.

Elda Hotel (in the Foresta Umbra area of the municipality) is a 4-star property, a restored historic refuge (from the 1940s, renovated more recently). It sits immersed in the UNESCO-listed Foresta Umbra / Gargano National Park, making it excellent for hiking and nature. Ratings are solid (around 8.5–8.9/10 on Booking.com). Features include a restaurant (Mediterranean/Gargano cuisine, including vegetarian/vegan options), garden, bar, free parking, Wi-Fi, and some rooms/chalets with forest views. Guests appreciate the peaceful forest setting, food, and location as a base for exploring the park.

Other strong options:

  • GarganoMareBorghi ospitalità diffusa (or similar albergo diffuso / diffused hotel concepts) — High ratings (often 9+ where reviewed) for its charming setup in the historic center with panoramic views; good for an authentic village experience.
  • Hotel Marechiaro (in San Menaio) — Beachfront with private beach/Beach Club, restaurant, and family-friendly amenities. Ratings vary (around 4.0/5 on Tripadvisor with hundreds of reviews; more mixed on some recent Booking scores). Convenient for direct sea access and relaxed seaside stays.
  • Smaller highly rated B&Bs/apartments such as Gárgara Bed and Breakfast, Case Vacanza Al Borgo Antico, B&B il Castello, Holiday B&B, or Aria di Vico Aparthotel frequently score 9.0+ and suit those wanting a more intimate, central, or budget-friendly experience in or near the historic center (“Village of Love”).

Vico del Gargano (a Borghi più belli d’Italia village) offers a mix of historic hilltop charm, proximity to beaches (San Menaio, Calenella), and access to the Foresta Umbra and Gargano National Park. Options range from beachfront and elegant villas to countryside B&Bs and forest hotels. Prices often start around €60–150+ per night depending on season, dates, and type (check current availability on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Kayak, as rates fluctuate). Many places emphasize free parking, Wi-Fi, and local cuisine.

For the absolute “best,” prioritize based on your priorities: sea proximity (Park Hotel Villa Maria or Marechiaro), historic center immersion (diffused hotels or central B&Bs), or nature/forest immersion (Elda or Verde Matematico). Always verify recent reviews and availability for your dates, as the area is seasonal.
