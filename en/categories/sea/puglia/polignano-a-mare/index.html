Top hotels in Polignano a Mare (Puglia, Italy) consistently praised across recent reviews and rankings include a mix of iconic cliffside stays, boutique options in the historic center, and countryside resorts/spas. Rankings draw from sources like Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and specialized hotel lists (updated into 2026). Ratings and availability fluctuate by season—summer is peak and more expensive—so check current prices and reviews.

Iconic / Luxury Cliffside & Boutique

  • Hotel Grotta Palazzese (often listed as Grotta Palazzese Beach Hotel or similar) — The most famous option. Rooms and terraces overlook the Adriatic; the signature restaurant is set inside a dramatic sea cave (accessible by stairs carved into the rock). Guests highlight the unique views, location right on the cliffs near the old town, and atmospheric dining. It is high-end and pricey; some reviews note it can feel overrated relative to cost or mention service variability, but the setting is unmatched.

  • Antico Mondo Rooms & Suites — Frequently tops “best of” lists with exceptional guest scores (around 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor and ~9.9/10 on Booking). Elegant rooms/suites in the historic center (some with stone walls, wooden beams, or sea views), very close to Lama Monachile beach and the old town. Praised for location, cleanliness, breakfast, and helpful staff. Strong mid-to-upscale choice.

  • The Duchess of Puglia — Highly rated boutique-style stay in the heart of the old town (Piazza Vittorio Emanuele II). Guests describe spacious, meticulously designed rooms with high-quality finishes, a calm atmosphere amid the bustle, and excellent staff. Feels like a refined, private-club experience.

Resorts & Countryside / Spa Experiences

  • Borgobianco Resort & Spa Polignano - MGallery — 5-star countryside resort (a few km outside town) with olive-grove setting, pools, spa, and good dining. Strong overall ratings for relaxation, staff, and facilities; free or shuttle service into Polignano is often available. Some note the location requires transport and occasional service inconsistencies, but many call it a peaceful base for exploring Puglia.

  • Pietrablu Resort & Spa — Popular seaside 4-star resort with direct sea access, natural rock pools, multiple swimming pools, spa, and family-friendly amenities. Frequently ranks high for value and facilities; good for a more resort-style stay slightly outside the historic center.

  • Masseria Montenapoleone — Standout 18th-century (or earlier roots) masseria inland with pool amid olive/almond trees, wellness offerings, kitchen garden, and romantic trullo suites. Often ranked among the very top for atmosphere, food, and rural luxury. Note it is a bit farther afield (near Fasano/Pezze di Greco area), so better if you have a car or want a countryside base.

Other Strong Contenders

  • San Lorenzo Boutique Hotel & SPA — Well-reviewed for style, spa, and comfort (often high on Tripadvisor lists).
  • Cala Ponte, a Tribute Portfolio Hotel — Modern luxury option with pool and spa elements; newer and praised in recent rankings.
  • Palazzo Viceconte, B&B Corte Altavilla, Covo dei Saraceni, Quintessenza Luxury Domus, and Aeris Suite & Relax — Frequently mentioned for character, old-town location, views, or high guest scores (many in the 9+ range).

Quick tips:
For the classic Polignano experience (cliff views, old town, Lama Monachile beach), prioritize Grotta Palazzese, Antico Mondo, or The Duchess of Puglia. For pools, spa, and quieter surroundings, choose Borgobianco, Pietrablu, or a masseria. Book early for summer; many places offer sea-view rooms or suites that are worth the premium. Always verify current ratings and policies, as small boutique properties can vary in consistency.
