Hotel Don Ferrante (Dimore di Charme), Il Melograno (Masseria Il Melograno), La Peschiera, Masseria Torrepietra, and Torre Cintola Greenblu Sea Emotions stand out as some of the strongest options in Monopoli, Puglia, based on traveler reviews, ratings from TripAdvisor/Booking.com/Trip.com, and specialist guides (as of recent 2025–2026 data).

Monopoli is a charming Adriatic coastal town with a lively historic center, a working port, and nearby beaches/coves. Hotels range from intimate seafront boutiques in the old town to countryside masserie (fortified farmhouses) and beach resorts. Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer); expect roughly €150–400+ per night for top properties, with luxury options higher.

Top Luxury / Boutique Picks

  • Hotel Don Ferrante (Dimore di Charme) — Frequently ranked among the very best and most romantic. This former seaside fortress/palazzo sits right on the cliffs in the historic center with Adriatic views, vaulted stone ceilings, a rooftop plunge pool, terraces, and a restaurant. Intimate (around 10–17 rooms). Guests praise location, service, sea views, and atmosphere (TripAdvisor ~4.5/5 from hundreds of reviews; Booking ~9.1). Ideal for couples.
  • Il Melograno (Masseria Il Melograno) — Classic 16th-century masseria (Puglia’s first of its kind turned luxury hotel), family-run, set in the countryside amid olive groves a short drive from town. Moorish courtyards, pools, tennis, spa, and a private beach club/shuttle. Floral-tiled rooms and a relaxed yet high-end feel. Strong for relaxation and authentic Puglian character (high marks for grounds, service, and breakfast; some note it shows a bit of age).
  • La Peschiera — Exclusive 5-star beachfront property (Small Luxury Hotels of the World) on a former fish hatchery/lagoon north of town. Just 13 rooms/suites, private beach, multiple (often 6–7) pools with natural spring water elements, spa/sauna, and excellent seafood dining with sea views. Very intimate and scenic; adults-oriented feel in some descriptions. Highly rated for exclusivity and setting.

Strong Mid-to-Upper Range / Resort Options

  • Masseria Torrepietra — Attractive 19th-century masseria/boutique hotel in the countryside (a few km from the center and coast) with outdoor pool, terrace, gardens/olive groves, and strong guest feedback (Booking ~9.2–9.3 from hundreds of reviews). Good value relative to pure luxury, solid service and breakfast.
  • Torre Cintola Greenblu Sea Emotions — Popular 4-star seafront resort in the Capitolo area with pools (fresh and sea water), private/cliffside beach access (lido), dome-style rooms, and family-friendly amenities. High volume of positive reviews for cleanliness, location, and facilities (often tops TripAdvisor “best value” or traveler rankings with 4.4/5 from ~1,800+ reviews). More resort-style than boutique.

Other Highly Rated Mentions

  • Palazzo Murat B&B, Albergo Diffuso Monopoli, Le Contrade Vico Alba, Palazzo Indelli, and various well-reviewed central B&Bs/suites (e.g., Santa Maria 24 or similar) for those wanting character-filled stays right in or near the historic center with high scores (often 9+ on Booking/Trip.com).
  • Playa del Mar (adults-only boutique option near the coast) also appears frequently in mid-range recommendations.

Quick Tips

  • Location choice: Historic center (Don Ferrante and many B&Bs) for walkability, restaurants, and atmosphere. Countryside masserie for peace, pools, and space. Capitolo/coastal resorts for direct beach access.
  • Best for: Romantics/couples → Don Ferrante or La Peschiera. Relaxation/authenticity → Il Melograno or Masseria Torrepietra. Families/beach → Torre Cintola.
  • Check current availability, exact rates, and reviews on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotels’ sites, as seasonal demand (especially summer) is high and properties can book up. A car is useful for masserie and exploring the broader area (Polignano a Mare, Alberobello trulli, beaches).

These consistently rank at the top across major platforms and specialist lists for quality, location, and guest satisfaction. Enjoy Monopoli!
