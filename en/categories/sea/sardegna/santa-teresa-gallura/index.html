The best hotels in Santa Teresa Gallura range from immersive, multi-pool luxury resorts nestled into the dramatic Sardinian coastline to charming, ultra-clean boutique options right in the town center.

Here are the top accommodations in and around town, categorized by the type of experience they offer:

Luxury & Destination Resorts

Resort Valle dell’Erica Thalasso & SPA: A premier 5-star resort located about 20 minutes outside of town. It features stunning saltwater pools, a massive spa, multiple private beach coves, and an acclaimed kids' club. Note: While the property is gorgeous, some guests prefer driving into town for more varied and reasonably priced dining options.

Mangia's Santa Teresa Sardinia, Curio Collection by Hilton: A refined 5-star cliffside retreat overlooking the bay. It offers a peaceful atmosphere with excellent service, beautiful modern design, and a dedicated shuttle to drop you directly at the beach or the center of town.

Colonna Grand Hotel Capo Testa: This beachfront 5-star property sits on the dramatic Capo Testa peninsula. It features panoramic sea views and multiple cascading pools, though the room interiors incline toward a traditional, classic Sardinian style.

Prime Beach Access & Town Center Convenience

Hotel Corallaro: A highly rated 4-star marine-themed hotel with a stellar location. It sits just a 2-minute walk from Rena Bianca , one of Sardinia's most beautiful white-sand beaches. It perfectly bridges the gap between beach access and town life, letting you walk to local boutiques and restaurants seamlessly.

Luma Relais de Charme: A stellar 4-star boutique hotel tucked into the town center. It is highly praised by travelers for immaculate cleanliness, attentive service, and sharp contemporary design, all while being a short walk from both the central piazza and the beach.

Charming Bed & Breakfasts & Value Stays

Hotel da Cecco: A bright, comfortable 3-star hotel providing incredible value. It features private balconies, spacious rooms, and a highly rated breakfast spread. The staff is incredibly welcoming, making it a reliable base for exploring the region.

Villa Bianca Luxury B&B Experience: A premium B&B offering sweeping views of Rena Bianca. It feels like a private paradise with spacious balconies, though breakfast is served on an intimate shared terrace right outside the rooms.

Here are some of the consistently highest-rated and most recommended hotels in Santa Teresa Gallura (northern Sardinia), based on recent Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and other traveler reviews.

Luxury / Resort Options

These are ideal if you want full amenities, spa facilities, pools, and a more secluded or beach-focused stay (some are a short drive from the town center).

  • Resort Valle Dell’Erica Thalasso & Spa (5-star) — Frequently ranks at or near the top on Tripadvisor (around 4.6/5 from 2,000+ reviews). Spread-out resort with beautiful natural grounds, sea views toward the Maddalena archipelago and Corsica, multiple restaurants, thalasso spa with saltwater pools, and access to beaches/coves. Excellent for a relaxing, high-end stay.
  • Mangia’s Santa Teresa Sardinia, Curio Collection by Hilton (5-star) — Modern resort with strong ratings (often 4.1–4.2/5 on Tripadvisor or ~8.7+ on other sites), private beach area, pools, and good facilities. Popular for all-inclusive or full-service stays.
  • Colonna Grand Hotel Capo Testa (5-star) — Located near Capo Testa with Mediterranean-style pools (including waterfalls), beach access, and solid reviews (typically around 8.0–8.1/10 or 3.8+/5). Good for a classic luxury beach hotel experience.
  • Felix Hotels – Hotel La Coluccia (5-star boutique) — Highly regarded for its design, pool, spa elements, and scenic setting. Often praised as one of the more beautiful boutique options in the area.

Strong Mid-Range / Central Hotels

These are excellent for walking to Rena Bianca beach, the town center, restaurants, and the ferry to Corsica, with high guest scores for location, staff, and value.

  • Hotel Da Cecco (3-star) — One of the most consistently top-rated (Booking.com often ~9.3–9.4/10; strong Tripadvisor scores). Just a short walk from Rena Bianca beach and the center, with excellent staff, good breakfast, and some rooms offering views toward Corsica/Bonifacio. Frequently listed among the most popular.
  • Hotel Canne al Vento (3-star) — Excellent guest scores (Booking.com ~8.7–9.0; Tripadvisor often 4.7/5). Central location, outstanding homemade breakfast buffet, friendly family-run atmosphere, and convenient for exploring on foot.
  • Hotel Corallaro (4-star, sometimes listed as Grand Hotel Corallaro) — Very good ratings (Booking.com ~8.8/10; Tripadvisor ~4.3/5). Right by Rena Bianca beach in the historical center, with a pool, restaurant, and convenient access. Strong on location and staff.
  • LH Hotel La Funtana (4-star) — Often ranks highly for value (Tripadvisor around 4.4/5). Features a pool, good breakfasts, spacious rooms, and a convenient location near the center.

Quick Tips

  • Best for beach + walkability: Hotel Da Cecco, Hotel Corallaro, or Hotel Canne al Vento (close to Rena Bianca).
  • Best for full resort/spa experience: Valle Dell’Erica or the Mangia’s/Hilton properties.
  • Capo Testa area: Colonna Grand Hotel Capo Testa or nearby options.
  • Ratings and rankings can shift slightly by season and platform. Peak summer (July–August) is the most expensive and busiest; shoulder seasons often offer better value.
  • Check current availability and prices on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites, as rates vary widely. Many properties include breakfast.
