Chia (in Domus de Maria, southern Sardinia, Italy) is the main destination matching this query—a popular beach area known for turquoise waters, dunes, and spots like Su Giudeu, Cala Cipolla, and Sa Colonia beaches (near Cagliari, about a 50–60 minute drive from the airport).

Chía (near Bogotá, Colombia) has standard hotels but is far less prominent for “best hotels” searches focused on resorts or luxury beach stays.

Here are some of the consistently top-rated and recommended hotels in Chia, Sardinia, based on traveler reviews from sources like TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and travel guides (ratings as of recent data; they can fluctuate):

Luxury & Unique Stays

  • Faro Capo Spartivento — Often singled out as one of the most exclusive and memorable options. This is a small boutique hotel in a historic (still operational) 19th-century lighthouse perched on cliffs with dramatic sea views. Expect elegant suites, infinity pool(s), excellent service, and a romantic, secluded feel near Cala Cipolla Beach. Ratings typically 9.3–9.5/10 (Booking) or ~4.8/5 (TripAdvisor). It’s pricey and the access road can be rough, but guests praise the uniqueness, food, and setting. Ideal for couples or a special splurge.

  • Conrad Chia Laguna Sardinia — A 5-star property within the large Chia Laguna Nature Resort complex. It offers modern design with Sardinian touches, multiple pools, an excellent spa, beach clubs (shuttle access to private areas on award-winning beaches), restaurants/bars, and strong family or couple facilities. Ratings around 4.4/5 (TripAdvisor) and ~8.5 (other platforms). Highly regarded for service, breakfast, and overall resort amenities; guests share access to the wider complex’s facilities.

Highly Rated Upscale / Boutique Options

  • Hotel Aquadulci — Frequently ranks among the top in Chia for its peaceful garden setting, pool, proximity to Su Giudeu Beach (short walk), clean/comfortable rooms, and attentive staff. Ratings often ~9.3/10 (Booking) or 4.4/5 (TripAdvisor, with Travellers’ Choice recognition). Good balance of quality, location, and relative value among nicer properties; popular for relaxation.

  • Baia di Chia Resort Sardinia, Curio Collection by Hilton — Another property in the Chia Laguna complex, leaning more adult/romantic with sea/lagoon views, a semi-Olympic pool, and direct-ish beach access. Ratings around 4.1/5 (TripAdvisor) with praise for the setting, renovations, and quieter vibe.

Family-Friendly / Resort-Style

  • Chia Laguna – Hotel Village (or The Village Chia Laguna Sardinia) — The more family-oriented option in the same large resort complex. Cottage-style rooms, kids’ clubs/activities, pools, beach access via shuttle, and shared amenities across the property. Solid mid-to-upper ratings (often 8+ / 4+ range) for facilities and location.

Other notables sometimes recommended include Veridia Resort Sardinia, Spartivento Hotel, Su Giudeu Hotel, and various high-rated B&Bs or villas for more intimate stays.

Tips:

  • Peak season (especially July–August) sees higher prices and crowds; shoulder seasons (May–June, September–October) are often better for value and weather.
  • Many properties are spread out—renting a car is useful for exploring beaches and sites like Nora.
  • Check current availability, exact ratings, and packages (half-board, etc.) on major booking sites, as they change.
