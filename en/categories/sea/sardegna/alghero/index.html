Villa Las Tronas Hotel & Spa, El Faro Hotel & Spa, Hotel Domomea, Hotel Corte Rosada (Adults Only), Hotel Angedras, Hotel Villa Piras, Hotel Calabona, Hotel Catalunya, and Smy Carlos V Alghero consistently rank among the top options in Alghero (Sardinia) based on recent TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Expedia, and other review sites.

Here’s a breakdown of standout choices:

Luxury / Boutique Highlights

  • Villa Las Tronas Hotel & Spa (5-star) — Frequently named one of the best in Alghero and Sardinia overall. Historic 19th-century villa on a promontory with panoramic sea views, elegant gardens, outdoor and indoor pools, spa, and a short walk to the historic center. Guests praise the aristocratic charm, staff, location, and atmosphere (ratings typically 9.1–9.4/10). Ideal for a refined, secluded yet convenient stay.
  • El Faro Hotel & Spa (4-star) — Scenic peninsula setting in Porto Conte (about 15–20 minutes from the center) with sea views, outdoor pools (including seawater), spa/thalasso, restaurant, and rocky shoreline good for snorkeling. Strong ratings (around 9.0) for the peaceful natural park location and facilities; best with a car.
  • Smy Carlos V Alghero (5-star) — Seafront position with spa, pools, and views; solid luxury option near the promenade.

Strong Mid-to-Upper Range / Popular Favorites

  • Hotel Domomea (4-star boutique) — Excellent central location (short walk to historic center, harbor, and beach), rooftop pool with hydromassage, garden, and good breakfast. High guest scores (often 9.0–9.2) for cleanliness, staff, and value in a quiet residential area.
  • Hotel Corte Rosada – Adults Only (affiliated with Meliá) — Frequently tops TripAdvisor “best value” or beach hotel lists. Private beach area, pool, spa, and all-inclusive options in a peaceful coastal setting near Porto Conte.
  • Hotel Angedras — Highly rated (around 4.5/5 on TripAdvisor) for service, cleanliness, and location near the center; reliable year-round option with parking and shuttle.
  • Hotel Villa Piras — Often ranks high on traveler lists for location (walkable to center), friendly staff, and value.
  • Hotel Calabona — Beach-oriented with sea views, pool, and private beach access; solid mid-range choice a short distance from town.
  • Hotel Catalunya — Central, modern, good views (including rooftop dining options), and convenient for exploring the marina and old town.

Other Notables

  • Hotel Alma di Alghero, Hotel Dei Pini, Hotel Sa Cheya Relais & Spa, and Hotel Florida (seafront on Lido di San Giovanni with pools) also receive frequent positive mentions for location, facilities, or value depending on needs (beach access, spa, or proximity to the historic center).

Tips:
Alghero’s historic center (Catalan-influenced old town) is compact and walkable, so central hotels suit sightseeing and dining best. For quieter beach/nature stays, look toward Porto Conte or Lido areas (car recommended). Ratings and rankings shift with dates and reviews—check current prices and availability on Booking, TripAdvisor, or Expedia, as high season (summer) fills quickly and prices rise. Many properties offer breakfast, pools, and spa facilities.
