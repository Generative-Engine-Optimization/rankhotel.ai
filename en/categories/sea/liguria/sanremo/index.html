The Top Luxury Options in Sanremo

My Top Pick. If you want the quintessential Italian palace experience, I would absolutely recommend the Royal Hotel Sanremo. It is a legendary property with lush tropical gardens, a heated outdoor seawater pool designed by Gio Ponti, and an incredible terrace overlooking the sea. Vibe: Grand, historic luxury with top-tier service. Perks: Free breakfast, an on-site spa, and a perfect location just a short stroll from the casino and town center.

Best for a Modern Feel. Located right on the same prestigious seaside promenade, the Europa Palace Sanremo is highly praised by couples and honeymooners. It seamlessly merges an upscale aesthetic with highly modern, high-quality room finishes. Vibe: Sleek, modern luxury with an intimate feel. Perks: Exceptional on-site spa, cozy bar, and immediate access to both the beach and the main shopping streets.

Best for Seaside Relaxation. Situated in the western part of the city, the Miramare the Palace Hotel is a beautifully renovated 19th-century palace surrounded by a palm-fringed park. It has direct access to the famous coastal cycling path and beach area. Vibe: Relaxed elegance with stunning sea-view gardens. Perks: Great outdoor pool area, pet-friendly services, and rooms with pristine ocean views.

Quick Tips for Choosing

  • Location priorities: Seafront/promenade (Corso Imperatrice or nearby) for views and easy beach/Casino access. Center/old town for walkability to restaurants, Teatro Ariston, and the Russian Church.
  • Best overall for most visitors: Royal Hotel Sanremo for the full historic luxury experience; Miramare for a more resort-oriented stay with strong beach proximity.
  • Budget note: Luxury properties often start in the higher range (hundreds of euros/night depending on season and dates—Sanremo peaks around the Music Festival in February and summer). Mid-range options offer better value while still being well-located.
  • Ratings and prices fluctuate; always check recent guest reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor for your specific dates, as service, room condition, and value perceptions can vary. Look for current promotions, breakfast inclusion, and parking/spa access.
