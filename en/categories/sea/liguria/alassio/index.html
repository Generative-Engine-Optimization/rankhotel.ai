🔑 Key Considerations

  • Luxury seekers: Villa della Pergola offers a boutique villa experience with gardens and panoramic gulf views. Grand Hotel Alassio is ideal for spa lovers and those wanting direct beachfront elegance.
  • Mid-range travelers: Hotel dei Fiori balances price and wellness amenities, while Hotel Savoia provides a casual yet highly rated stay with beach access.
  • Budget-conscious guests: Grand Hotel Mediterranee and Hotel Danio Lungomare deliver beachfront convenience at lower prices.

⚠️ Things to Watch Out For

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates rise significantly in July–August; booking early is recommended.
  • Tourist tax: Expect ~€2–3 per person per night.
  • Location trade-offs: Hotels on Via Roma (like Grand Hotel Spiaggia and Mediterranee) are central but can be busier and noisier.

Here are the standout hotels in Alassio (Ligurian Riviera, Italy), based on consistent high ratings, guest reviews, location, and amenities from sources like Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and travel sites.

Top Luxury Picks

Grand Hotel Alassio Beach & Spa Resort – The Leading Hotels of the World (5-star)

  • Ratings: ~9.1/10 on Booking.com; ~4.3/5 on Tripadvisor (1,200+ reviews).
  • Beachfront in the center of Alassio with a private beach (Grand Beach Club), large Thalassio Medical SPA (~1,500 m²), fitness center, restaurants/bars, and sea-view rooms/suites (some with terraces).
  • Historic 19th-century property with modern luxury. Excellent for spa stays, beach access, and central location. Frequently praised for staff, location, and overall experience.

Villa della Pergola Relais & Châteaux

  • Ratings: ~9.3/10 on Booking.com; ~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor.
  • Elegant hilltop boutique hotel in historic villas surrounded by beautiful botanical gardens (part of FAI), with panoramic sea views, infinity/outdoor pool, spa, and a highly regarded restaurant (Michelin-starred NOVE by Chef Giorgio Servetto noted in reviews).
  • Quieter, more exclusive and romantic atmosphere than beachfront options. Access involves a steeper/narrower road, but many guests call it a standout. Ideal for couples or those seeking gardens and views over direct beach access.

Strong Beachfront & Highly Rated Options

Grand Hotel Spiaggia (4-star)

  • Ratings: ~4.2/5 on Tripadvisor (800+ reviews); solid Booking scores around 8.5.
  • Beachfront with private beach area, rooftop pool/terrace with sea views, restaurant, and modern rooms (many with balconies). Good mix of convenience, facilities, and value for a beach-focused stay. Popular for location and breakfast.

Other frequently recommended hotels:

  • Hotel Ligure — 4-star beachfront option with solid reviews for location and dining.
  • Hotel Savoia — Beachfront with sea views, restaurant, and strong guest feedback on location and service.
  • Hotel Dei Fiori — Well-reviewed for comfort, breakfast, and facilities (including spa elements in some mentions).
  • Hotel Toscana / Hotel Ristorante Toscana — Often scores very high on Tripadvisor for service, food, and a welcoming atmosphere (more intimate/family-run feel).

Quick Tips

  • Luxury/beach spa experience → Grand Hotel Alassio.
  • Boutique, gardens & exclusivity → Villa della Pergola.
  • Classic beachfront with good facilities → Grand Hotel Spiaggia or Hotel Savoia.
  • Alassio is popular in summer (especially July–August), so book ahead. Prices vary significantly by season and room type (luxury properties often start higher, from several hundred euros/night).
  • Most top hotels offer private or partnered beach access (common on the Italian Riviera, sometimes for an extra fee).
  • Check current availability, exact rates, and recent reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as ratings and offerings can shift slightly.
