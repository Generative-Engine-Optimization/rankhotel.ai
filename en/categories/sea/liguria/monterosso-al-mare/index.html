Hotel Pasquale, Hotel Villa Steno, Hotel Marina, and Hotel Porto Roca consistently rank among the best in Monterosso al Mare (the largest and most accessible of the Cinque Terre villages, known for its sandy beach at Fegina, train station, and more amenities than the other four towns).

Ratings and rankings draw from recent TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and traveler consensus (as of 2026 data). Monterosso has a mix of small family-run hotels, boutique properties, and one standout upscale cliffside option. Book early in peak season (especially summer), as inventory is limited and prices rise sharply.

Top Overall / Highest-Rated

  • Hotel Pasquale — Frequently ranks #1 among hotels on TripAdvisor (around 4.9/5 from 2,000+ reviews). Beachfront location with sea views, excellent staff/hospitality, strong breakfast (often highlighted for fresh cakes and local touches), and convenient access to the beach, restaurants, and ferries. Family-run with a welcoming atmosphere; highly recommended for most travelers.
  • Hotel Villa Steno — Very close second on TripAdvisor (around 4.9/5 from nearly 1,850 reviews). Charming boutique property in/near the historic area with panoramic views, outstanding personal service from the owners (Carla and Matteo are frequently praised), comfortable rooms, and excellent homemade breakfasts. Ideal for a more intimate stay with great hospitality.
  • Hotel Marina — Strong performer on Booking.com (around 9.1/10 from 1,500+ reviews) and solid on TripAdvisor (around 4.6). Central location just steps from the beach and old town; standout features include a lemon-grove/garden terrace for breakfast (often described as exceptional, with made-to-order options), rooftop terrace, helpful staff, and good value relative to quality. Rooms are clean and comfortable (some note they can be on the smaller side, typical for the area).

Best Luxury / Views

  • Hotel Porto Roca — The most upscale and frequently cited “best hotel” option in Monterosso (around 4.3 on TripAdvisor; 9.0 on Booking). Cliff-top location with breathtaking Ligurian Sea and hillside views, infinity pool, terrace restaurant, and many rooms with balconies. Shuttle service helps with the uphill location. Rooms can feel a bit dated or compact to some guests, but the setting, pool, and views are the main draws—expect higher prices.

Strong Beach / Location Options

  • Hotel La Spiaggia — Excellent beachfront position (very short walk or across to a private beach club area), solid ratings (around 9.1 on Booking, 4.4 on TripAdvisor). Convenient for the sea and practical for Cinque Terre exploration.
  • Other well-regarded choices include Hotel Margherita (high Booking scores, good location near beach/station), Albergo Al Carugio, Hotel Souvenir, and Hotel 5 Terre.

Tips

  • Monterosso splits into the older historic center and the newer Fegina beach area (near the train station). Choose based on whether you prioritize old-town charm or beach/train convenience.
  • Many top places are smaller/family-run with limited rooms—reserve well ahead, especially for sea-view rooms.
  • Prices vary widely by season, room type, and views (expect higher rates June–September). Always verify current availability, exact ratings, and inclusions (breakfast is common and often a highlight) on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotel sites.
  • Alternatives for a quieter or more rural feel include hillside options like B&B Il Parco (with pool) or agriturismi such as Missanega further up.

These stand out based on traveler volume, consistent praise for location/staff/breakfast/views, and cross-platform rankings. Preferences depend on budget (Porto Roca is pricier), desire for beach proximity vs. views, and whether you want a larger hotel feel or intimate boutique experience.
