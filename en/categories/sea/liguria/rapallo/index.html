The top hotels in Rapallo (Italian Riviera, Liguria) consistently highlighted across TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and other sources are luxury or highly rated properties with sea views, spa facilities, or prime waterfront/central locations.

Rapallo is a charming base for exploring Portofino, Santa Margherita Ligure, and the wider Riviera, with a mix of historic grand hotels and modern boutique options. Rankings and ratings can shift with season and reviews, so always check current availability, prices, and recent guest feedback.

Top Recommendations

Here are some of the best, based on recent high ratings, guest praise for views/service/amenities, and frequent appearances in “best of” lists:

  1. Excelsior Palace Portofino Coast (5-star)
    Historic cliffside hotel with panoramic views of the Portofino promontory and Ligurian Sea. Highlights include an infinity/outdoor pool, beach club, spa (indoor heated pool, saunas, treatments), restaurants, and many sea-view rooms. Strong ratings (around 9.1–9.4 on Booking.com; ~4.4 on TripAdvisor from thousands of reviews). Frequently tops or near-tops best-hotel lists for location and facilities. Ideal for a classic luxury Riviera stay.

  2. Grand Hotel Bristol Spa Resort (by R Collection Hotels)
    Elegant historic property (Art Nouveau roots, renovated) overlooking the Gulf of Tigullio/Portofino. Features a large spa (one of the biggest in the area), outdoor pool with views, rooftop/restaurant dining (Ligurian cuisine), and many rooms/suites with balconies and sea views. Praised for atmosphere, wellness, and service. A strong luxury pick for relaxation.

  3. Hotel Astoria (4-star)
    Highly rated (often ~4.7 on TripAdvisor; ~9.1 on Booking) family-run hotel near the start of the seaside promenade. Guests love the sea views (many rooms with balconies), central location (walkable to beach, restaurants, station, and castle area), cleanliness, and staff. Spa facilities available. Excellent mid-to-upscale option with a more intimate feel than the grand hotels.

  4. Tigullio Royal Hotel (Best Western Plus)
    Strong ratings (~4.6 TripAdvisor; often 9.3–9.4 elsewhere). Sea views, modern rooms, sky bar, and good hospitality. Convenient location with easy access to the waterfront and town. Popular for its combination of quality and value relative to pure 5-stars.

  5. Splendide Riviera Boutique Art Hotel and Rosabianca Boutique Hotel
    Smaller boutique options with very high guest scores (often 9.3–9.5 range). Near the beach/promenade, stylish design, restaurant/bar, and strong service praise. Great if you prefer intimate, contemporary stays over grand historic hotels.

Other Notables

  • Hotel Italia e Lido — Waterfront location right by the castle and promenade; solid ratings and convenient for exploring on foot.
  • Hotel Vesuvio — Promenade setting with good reviews for location and comfort.
  • Smaller/highly rated spots like Hotel San Desiderio or certain B&Bs/villas for quieter, more personal stays.

Tips:

  • Prioritize sea-view rooms where possible—the scenery is a major draw.
  • Peak season (summer) fills quickly and prices rise; shoulder seasons offer better value.
  • Many properties are pet-friendly (with possible fees) and offer parking (often paid).
  • Rapallo has good train/ferry links for day trips.

For the latest rates, availability, and detailed recent reviews, check Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotels’ official sites directly, as conditions change.
