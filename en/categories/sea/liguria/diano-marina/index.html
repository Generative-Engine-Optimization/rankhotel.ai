Diano Marina is a popular Riviera di Ponente (Ligurian coast) resort town known for its sandy beaches, promenade, and proximity to Imperia, Sanremo, and Cervo. Most highly rated hotels emphasize sea views, private beach access or proximity, pools, good food (often Ligurian specialties), and friendly service. Ratings are generally strong (often 4.5+ / 5 or 9+ / 10), with family-run places and seafront properties standing out.

Quick Tips

  • Seafront vs. hillside — Arc En Ciel, Grand Hotel Diana Majestic, and the Palace offer direct or very close beach access. Liliana prioritizes quiet elevated views and is a short drive/walk from the action.
  • Amenities — Many include pools, restaurants (half-board often recommended for quality), free Wi-Fi, parking (sometimes free or nearby), and beach services (sunbeds/umbrellas). Wellness facilities appear at Torino and some others.
  • Seasonality & booking — Peak summer (June–August) books up and costs more; shoulder seasons offer better value and milder weather. Check current availability/prices on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites, as they fluctuate. Many are pet-friendly (confirm in advance).
  • Getting around — The town is walkable; trains connect to Imperia/Sanremo. Nice or Genoa airports are the main gateways (roughly 1–1.5 hours by car/train depending on traffic).
