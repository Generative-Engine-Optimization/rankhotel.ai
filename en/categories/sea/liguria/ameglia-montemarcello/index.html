Here are the best hotels in and around Ameglia, Italy , offering a mix of luxury spa retreats, quiet countryside farmhouses, and coastal escapes near the border of Liguria and Tuscany.

Top Luxury & Spa Pick

If you are looking for an upscale, indulgent getaway, I'd choose the Poets Gulf Relais & SPA. Situated on the lush hillsides of Montemarcello (within the municipality of Ameglia), it offers breathtaking views overlooking the Gulf of Poets [1.3.1) 1.3.10]. The Vibe: Refined, quiet, and deeply relaxing—ideal for couples or travelers looking to unwind. Standout Features: It features a full-service spa, two panoramic outdoor pools, a hot tub, and a highly-regarded seafood restaurant. Local Tip: Because it sits high on a tranquil ridge, you are a bit removed from the immediate hustle of the beach. It’s perfect if you plan to enjoy the property or go on local hiking excursions, but you will want a car or transit plan to visit nearby towns like La Spezia.

Best In-Town & Countryside Stays

For a peaceful, authentic experience close to nature, Il Casale del Magra is an exceptional choice. The Vibe: A beautiful, highly-rated rustic farmhouse tucked into a quiet green valley, just minutes away from both the historic center of Ameglia and the sea. Standout Features: Exceptionally clean and welcoming environment, offering free breakfast, free parking, and a peaceful atmosphere.

If you want a comfortable, intimate, and flawless base right in the heart of the area, Le Camere di Giò boasts an immaculate record with travelers. The Vibe: A small, beautifully maintained 3-star property focused on personalized hospitality. Standout Features: Known for its cozy ambiance and very accommodating hosts, making it a fantastic budget-friendly hub for exploring the wider Ligurian coast.

Nearby Beachfront & Coastal Alternatives

If your primary goal is to wake up right on the sand or overlooking the open ocean, the immediate coastline of neighboring Lerici and Fiascherino (about 15–20 minutes' drive from Ameglia) offers fantastic boutique beach properties:

A fantastic 4-star boutique hotel located right on a crystal-clear public beach. Rooms feature private balconies with gorgeous views overlooking the sea and the town of San Terenzo. It includes a fantastic first-floor restaurant overlooking the water.

Perched on a hillside overlooking the ocean in the hidden-gem area of Fiascherino. It is just a quick 3-minute walk down the steps to a quiet beach, and features a wonderful terrace garden where they serve fresh Italian pies and fruit every morning.

Here are the best hotels and places to stay in Ameglia (Liguria, Italy), based on guest ratings from Booking.com, TripAdvisor, and other major platforms.

Ameglia is a small, scenic municipality that includes the historic village, the hilltop hamlet of Montemarcello (great views), Bocca di Magra, and the beach area of Fiumaretta. It’s a quieter, more authentic base near the Cinque Terre, Lerici, and the Gulf of Poets (Golfo dei Poeti), with beaches, the Magra River, and the Montemarcello-Magra Regional Park nearby.

Quick Tips

  • Luxury / special occasion → Golfo dei Poeti Relais & Spa is clearly the standout (views + spa + food).
  • Best overall value / location → Hotel Sette Archi or Locanda Dell’Angelo.
  • Beach focus → Stay in Fiumaretta or Bocca di Magra (Maison Del Magra or Sette Archi).
  • Quiet / nature / views → Montemarcello (Golfo dei Poeti or private B&Bs).
  • Many properties are seasonal (especially pools and restaurants). Summer is peak season—book ahead.
  • Ameglia is more relaxed and less crowded than the main Cinque Terre villages, making it an excellent base if you have a car.
