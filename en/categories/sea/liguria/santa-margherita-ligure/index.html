If you want the quintessential Italian Riviera experience without the chaotic crowds of nearby Portofino, Santa Margherita Ligure is the perfect home base. My absolute top recommendation is the Grand Hotel Miramare , an Art Nouveau icon managed by the same family since 1945 that effortlessly blends old-world "La Dolce Vita" glamour with incredibly warm, personalized service. However, depending on whether you want direct beach access, a hip boutique vibe, or a peaceful cliffside hideaway, there are a few other stellar properties to consider.

Here is my curated list of the best places to stay in town:

✨ 5-Star Grand Luxury Hotels

I'd choose the Miramare if you want a fairytale setting with modern updates. Highlights include their newly reimagined Bagni Mira beach club with its 1950s-inspired stripes, and the Vistamare restaurant which serves exquisite seafood overlooking the Gulf of Tigullio. It sits comfortably on the flatter coastal stretch, making it an easy 45-minute stroll along the "Walk of Kisses" straight into Portofino.

This historic, palatial property sits on a dramatic stretch of rocky beach just outside the town center. It leans heavily into its Belle Époque roots with frescoed ceilings and manicured historic grounds. Choose this if you want a grand, stately atmosphere and don't mind a brief, scenic walk to reach the harbor restaurants.

🏖️ 4-Star Premium & Beach Resorts

The Continental is arguably your best option if your holiday priorities revolve around sunbeds and direct swimming. Perched on a high promontory, it features a fantastic private cliffside beach club and a 400 m² spa with an infinity pool that merges seamlessly with the sea. Note: Because it's up on a cliff, be prepared for a steep walk back up from the marina.

Located right next to the Continental, the Metropole offers a premium experience with spacious rooms and gorgeous sea views. It boasts a luxurious wellness center, an indoor pool, and a lush park setting. It's highly favored by travelers who want close proximity to both the train station and the town's central promenade.

It's tucked centrally into town, making it exceptionally convenient for exploring local shops, bakeries, and public beaches.

These stand out based on guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trivago, and travel sites (as of recent 2025–2026 data), with strong scores for location, sea views, service, pools/beaches, and overall experience. Rankings vary by platform (e.g., Tripadvisor emphasizes traveler popularity/value; Booking emphasizes recent guest scores), and prices fluctuate seasonally (higher in summer).

Top Luxury / Highly Rated Picks

  • Grand Hotel Miramare (5-star)
    Elegant historic hotel overlooking the Gulf of Tigullio. All rooms/suites have sea views and balconies; features a tropical garden, saltwater pool, private beach club access, restaurant, and strong service. Frequently praised as one of the best overall (Tripadvisor ~4.7/5 from 1,300+ reviews; Booking ~9.1–9.3/10). Ideal for panoramic views and classic Riviera luxury.

  • Imperiale Palace Hotel (5-star)
    19th-century villa with lush gardens, private beach, saltwater pool, fitness center, spa elements, sea-view restaurant, and excellent breakfast. Short walk to the center; easy access to Portofino. High guest ratings (often 9.1–9.4/10; Tripadvisor strong). Romantic dinners and attentive staff are frequently highlighted.

  • Hotel Continental
    Hillside luxury option with panoramic bay views, modern renovated rooms, gardens, and strong staff praise. Often ranks high for value and location near the sea (Tripadvisor frequently lists it near the top, ~4.3/5 from 1,300+ reviews; Booking ~8.8/10). Good for a relaxing base near Portofino/Cinque Terre.

  • Hotel Blu di Te (design/boutique)
    Stylish, renovated property with vintage-modern décor, pool, Turkish bath, excellent breakfast, and central location (short walk to beach/harbor/restaurants). Very high guest scores (Booking often ~9.4/10). Praised for design, service, and value relative to luxury peers.

  • LHP Hotel Santa Margherita Palace & Spa (5-star boutique)
    Contemporary design with terrace/Jacuzzi suites, spa/wellness area, pool, restaurant, and convenient location near the train station (easy Portofino access). Solid ratings and good for spa-focused stays.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Best Western Regina Elena — Seafront location, pool/spa options, solid mid-to-upper ratings, and good value.
  • Hotel Metropole — Comfortable 4-star with sea proximity and amenities.
  • Higher-rated smaller or boutique options (e.g., Hotel Villa Anita, Albergo Minerva, or Il Corso) often score 9.4+ for personal service and location but have fewer reviews or less full-hotel infrastructure.

Quick Tips

Santa Margherita Ligure is a charming base for Portofino (short bus/boat/taxi), the Portofino Natural Park, and day trips. Prioritize sea-view rooms and book early for peak season (June–September). Check current availability, exact ratings, and packages on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or hotel sites, as they change. Private parking, beach access, and breakfast quality are common differentiators among the top properties.
