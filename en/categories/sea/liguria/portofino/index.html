Gemini

When it comes to staying in Portofino, the hotel scene is small, highly exclusive, and split between two distinct experiences: staying directly on the vibrant harbor (the Piazzetta) or perched high in the hills with sweeping, cinematic views of the Ligurian coast.

Because space in the village is strictly limited, a few of the region's absolute best properties are technically located just around the coastline in neighboring Paraggi or Santa Margherita Ligure , which often offer better beach access and a slightly more relaxed pace.

The Icons of Portofino (In the Village)

Perched high on a hillside overlooking the bay, this former 16th-century monastery is one of the most famous luxury hotels in the world. It is the ultimate choice for privacy, old-world glamour, and jaw-dropping views. The Vibe: Pure Hollywood-on-the-Rivira glamour. Standout Features: A heated saltwater infinity pool that looks like it hangs over the ocean, lush terraced gardens, and Italy's first permanent Dior Spa . Good to Know: It is highly exclusive and commands top-tier pricing, but staying here grants you access to its sister property down on the harbor.

If you want to be steps from the yachts and cafes, this is the premier option. Located directly on Portofino's iconic Piazzetta, it functions as an intimate, 14-room guesthouse that trades sprawling grounds for unmatched village energy. The Vibe: Chic, cinematic, and modern maritime boutique. Standout Features: Exquisite seafood dining at DaV Mare and complimentary access to all the hillside amenities (pool, spa, gardens) at the main Splendido hotel up the hill. Good to Know: Harbor-facing rooms get lively; if you prefer absolute silence, ask the concierge for a courtyard-facing layout or utilize the provided double-glazing.

Highly Rated Alternatives Just Minutes Away

Tucked just behind the main harbor, this boutique hotel offers a quieter, more understated slice of luxury compared to the Belmond giants. It acts as a serene hideaway with a private garden and outdoor jacuzzis, located right where the village meets the hills. The Vibe: Modern, intimate, and discreet. The Sister Property Benefit: Guests here often get access to Eight Hotel Paraggi , meaning you can easily escape the harbor for a day on one of the area's rare sandy beaches.

Located about 10 minutes down the coast in neighboring Santa Margherita Ligure , this historic 1903 property is ideal if you want classic Italian luxury with easy, flat walking access to shops and standard beaches. The Vibe: Art Nouveau grand-dame elegance with family-run warmth. Standout Features: The retro-glam Bagni Mira beach club, an exceptional concierge team, and the open-air pool deck. Good to Know: You can walk directly into Portofino along the scenic "Walk of Kisses" coastal path (about 45 minutes) or have the front desk arrange a quick water taxi or shuttle.

How to Choose Your Stay

For Absolute Privacy & Views: Book the hillside Splendido. You get the iconic panoramic vistas of the coast without any of the daytime tourist foot traffic.

To Be Part of the Scene: Choose Splendido Mare. Stepping right out of your lobby into the heart of the Piazzetta for your morning espresso is an unforgettable experience.

For a Beach-Focused Trip: Look toward Eight Hotel Paraggi or the Grand Hotel Miramare. Portofino itself is a rocky harbor; if you want soft sand and easy swimming, the neighboring bays are much more accommodating.

Grok · grok-3

The top hotels in Portofino (a tiny, exclusive Ligurian village with limited options) are consistently the Belmond properties, Eight Hotel Portofino, Hotel Piccolo Portofino, and Albergo Nazionale. Portofino itself has only a handful of hotels; many travelers also consider nearby Santa Margherita Ligure for more choices and often better value.

Here’s a breakdown of the standouts based on expert reviews (Condé Nast Traveler, Hotel Guru, etc.), guest ratings on Tripadvisor/Booking/Trip.com, and recent feedback:

Ultra-Luxury Icons (Belmond)

  • Splendido, A Belmond Hotel — Widely regarded as the best and most iconic hotel in Portofino. Set on a hillside in a former 16th-century Benedictine monastery, it offers sweeping views of the harbor and bay, elegant rooms/suites (many with balconies), a heated saltwater infinity pool, Dior spa, gardens, and excellent dining (notably La Terrazza). Recently renovated (Martin Brudnizki design). Celebrity history (Elizabeth Taylor, Ava Gardner, etc.). Seasonal (typically late May–early October). Extremely high ratings and repeated Condé Nast Readers’ Choice/Gold List recognition.
  • Splendido Mare, A Belmond Hotel — The smaller, more intimate sister property right on the Piazzetta (harbor square). Perfect for those who want to be in the heart of the action with harbor views, strong service, and access to the main Splendido’s facilities (including beach club privileges). Also very highly rated (often 9.5+ / 4.8 on guest sites).

Strong Boutique Luxury

  • Eight Hotel Portofino — Stylish 5-star boutique hotel in a renovated Ligurian house near the square. Modern-elegant rooms, rooftop/garden area with jacuzzi, excellent staff, and a more contemporary feel than the classic Belmonds. Frequently praised as a top alternative if you want luxury without the full Belmond price or formality. Good shuttle to Paraggi beach.

Excellent Mid-to-Upper Options

  • Hotel Piccolo Portofino — Popular 4-star in a historic villa with modern interiors, strong sea views from many rooms/terraces, and a great location just outside the main hub (short walk or easy access). Often ranks high on Tripadvisor for value and guest satisfaction; solid breakfast and atmosphere. A frequent “best overall/value” pick.
  • Albergo Nazionale — Charming, family-run 3-star right on/near the harbor with unbeatable location (views of the piazzetta), friendly service, and comfortable rooms. Excellent location score (often near-perfect) and strong guest feedback for authenticity and convenience; more affordable than the 5-stars.

Quick Tips

  • Prices are high across the board (especially Belmond properties), and availability is limited in peak summer—book early.
  • Location trade-offs: Splendido for peaceful hillside luxury + views; Splendido Mare / Eight / Nazionale for being steps from the harbor and restaurants; Piccolo for a slightly quieter sea-view setting.
  • Nearby alternatives (if Portofino is fully booked or too expensive): Imperiale Palace Hotel or Grand Hotel Miramare in Santa Margherita Ligure (short boat/taxi ride).

For the absolute best experience with classic Portofino glamour, start with Splendido (or Splendido Mare if you prefer the village center). Check recent reviews and availability on major booking sites, as seasonal renovations and demand can affect options.
