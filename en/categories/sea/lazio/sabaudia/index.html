Quick Tips

  • Beachfront preference → Prioritize Oasi Di Kufra or Le Dune.
  • Modern comfort + spa → Best Western Plus Sabaudia stands out with consistently high scores for cleanliness, comfort, and facilities.
  • Charm + atmosphere → Il San Francesco or Mami.
  • Prices vary significantly by season (peak summer is much higher). Expect mid-range rates starting roughly from €80–150+ per night off-peak for good options, rising in high season.
  • Most top hotels offer free parking and Wi-Fi; many are pet-friendly or family-oriented.
  • Sabaudia is roughly 90–120 minutes from Rome by car. Circeo National Park and the beaches are major draws.
