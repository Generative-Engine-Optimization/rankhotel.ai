🎯 Practical Tips

  • For seaside luxury: Grand Hotel Dei Cesari and Hotel Lido Garda are ideal, with pools and private beach access.
  • For central convenience: Villa Sarsina offers charm and walkability to Anzio’s historic sites.
  • For budget stays: La Bussola and La Casa di Barbara provide affordable comfort close to transport and beaches.
  • For families: Hotel Parco Dei Principi has spacious rooms and gardens, perfect for longer stays.

⚠️ Things to Consider

  • Seasonality: Prices rise in summer (June–August). Booking early is recommended.
  • Transport: Anzio is about 1 hour by train from Rome, making it a good seaside escape.
  • Amenities: Not all B&Bs include air conditioning or parking—check before booking.

👉 Final Recommendation:
If you want a beachfront resort experience, choose Grand Hotel Dei Cesari. For a charming, central stay, go with Villa Sarsina. If you’re traveling on a budget, La Bussola Hotel is a solid option.

Grok · grok-3

Hotel L'Approdo, Serpa Hotel, and Hotel Riviera consistently rank among the best in Anzio, based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and similar sites (2025–2026 data). Anzio is a charming coastal town south of Rome (about 1 hour by train), known for beaches, history (including the WWII landing and Nero’s villa), and a more relaxed vibe than bigger Italian destinations. Most top options are solid 3-star hotels or well-rated B&Bs rather than ultra-luxury resorts.

Top Recommendations

Here are the strongest performers by ratings, location, and guest feedback:

  • Hotel L'Approdo (often ranks #1 or near the top)
    Booking.com ~9.1/10 (100+ reviews); Tripadvisor 4.5/5 (270+ reviews).
    Stylish 3-star seafront (or just across the road) property with a rooftop terrace featuring a heated hydromassage pool and sea views. Guests praise the excellent breakfast, modern/clean rooms (many with balconies or sea views), extremely helpful family staff, and convenience—near Anzio Colonia train station for easy Rome day trips and a clean sandy beach. Ideal for couples or families wanting comfort + location. Some note it’s a short walk (or longer relaxed stroll) to the town center.

  • Serpa Hotel
    Booking.com ~8.4/10 (450+ reviews); Tripadvisor 4.5/5 (120+ reviews)—frequently tops “best value” lists.
    Right on the seafront with free parking, sun terrace, and rooms (many with sea-view balconies). Standouts include friendly/helpful staff, cleanliness, solid breakfast, and the beach just across the street (with a nearby seafood restaurant). It’s quieter and a bit farther from the main center (walkable or short transport), making it great for beach-focused stays. Consistent positive feedback on comfort and value.

  • Hotel Riviera
    Booking.com ~8.5/10; often well-reviewed for atmosphere.
    Overlooking the beach in a more central spot (near Villa of Nerone). Features a terrace/bar and free Wi-Fi. Guests like the location, friendly staff (frequent mentions of good reception), clean rooms, and overall pleasant boutique feel. Good choice if you want easier access to restaurants, bars, and sights while still being beachside.

Other Strong Options

  • Hotel Sirenetta — Beachfront 3-star with garden, terrace, and restaurant. Solid mid-range choice (~8.2 on Booking) with good views and facilities; popular for its location and food.
  • High-rated B&Bs / smaller stays (excellent scores, fewer rooms/reviews):
    • LA CASA DEL SOLE (Beachfront B&B) ~9.2
    • La Maison sul Porto ~9.2
    • Il Paradiso sul Mare ~9.1
    • B&B di Mare e di Sale (very high Tripadvisor scores)
      These shine for personal service, quiet settings, and direct beach proximity—great for couples seeking something more intimate.
  • Hotel Parco Dei Principi — Larger grounds with pool, tennis, and park-like setting. More family/activity-oriented (ratings more mid-range).
  • Grand Hotel Dei Cesari — Bigger traditional hotel with beach access and pool; mixed reviews (some love the sea views and facilities, others find it dated).

Quick Tips

  • Beachfront vs. center — L’Approdo, Serpa, Sirenetta, and many B&Bs excel for sea access. Riviera and some central options are better if nightlife, restaurants, or museums matter more.
  • Practical notes — Free or easy parking is available at several (Serpa is a standout). Train links to Rome are convenient from nearby stations. Summer (June–August) is peak season with higher prices and more crowds; shoulder seasons are quieter and often better value.
  • What guests value most — Cleanliness, staff friendliness, breakfast quality, and sea views/access come up repeatedly. True 4–5 star luxury is limited in Anzio itself.

Prices fluctuate (often starting from roughly €80–150+ per night for doubles depending on dates and season). Check current availability, exact ratings, photos, and cancellation policies on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, and filter by your priorities (pool, parking, breakfast included, pet-friendly, etc.).
