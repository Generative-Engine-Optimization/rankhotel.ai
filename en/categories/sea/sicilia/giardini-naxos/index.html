Nautilus Hotel, Hotel Villa Daphne (or Villa Daphne), and UNAHOTELS Naxos Beach Sicilia consistently rank among the top options in Giardini Naxos (Sicily), based on recent guest ratings from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites.

Giardini Naxos is a beach town near Taormina with a mix of large resorts, boutique hotels, and smaller properties. Ratings emphasize location (beachfront or easy access to the sea and buses to Taormina/Etna), cleanliness, staff, and facilities like pools. Here’s a breakdown of standout choices:

Top Highly Rated Picks

  • Nautilus Hotel (4-star, beachfront on Lungomare Tysandros)
    Frequently tops or near-tops lists. Tripadvisor ~4.8/5 (around 180–190 reviews); Booking.com ~9.2/10 (800+ reviews). Modern/renovated rooms (many with sea-view balconies or jacuzzis), excellent staff (often praised as among the best), strong breakfast, pool/jacuzzi options, and a convenient central seafront spot near restaurants and buses. Ideal for couples or those wanting a stylish, smaller-scale stay with great views.

  • Hotel Villa Daphne (elegant boutique-style)
    Strong ratings: Tripadvisor ~4.7/5; Booking.com ~9.1/10. Peaceful setting with a garden, pool, and solarium; rooms in typical Sicilian style; restaurant serving local cuisine; beach a short walk away. Convenient for buses to Taormina and Etna. Guests highlight cleanliness, friendly staff, and overall comfort. Good for a more intimate, refined experience.

  • UNAHOTELS Naxos Beach Sicilia (large 4-star resort)
    Often ranked high for value and family appeal (Tripadvisor ~4.2/5 with 3,500+ reviews). Expansive grounds with multiple pools (including Olympic-size), private beach (seasonal), tennis, fitness, restaurants, and activities. Seafront location with Etna views possible; popular for all-inclusive or half-board stays. Suited to families, groups, or those wanting resort facilities and easy excursions. Some notes on food or occasional updates, but overall highly recommended for its amenities and scale.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Delta Hotels by Marriott Giardini Naxos — Upscale beachfront property with lush gardens, spa/wellness, private beach access, pools, and Etna/sea views. Rooms have classic Italian style; good for couples or those seeking Marriott-level service and facilities. Reviews praise location, staff, and views (ratings typically solid, often in the 8+/10 range on major sites).
  • Arathena Rocks Hotel — Cliffside/sea-edge location with saltwater pool, direct sea access or strong views (including Etna), traditional Sicilian touches. Quieter vibe; good for relaxation with easy town access.
  • Hotel San Giovanni — Well-rated 3-star option (Tripadvisor ~4.3/5) in a central spot, short walk to the beach, bus links to Taormina. Friendly staff, cozy rooms (some sea or garden views), solid value.
  • Hellenia Yachting Hotel & SPA — 4-star with spa focus, pool, and sea proximity; frequently listed among nicer options.
  • Smaller/high-rated alternatives: Hotel Palladio (strong guest scores around 9+), various well-reviewed B&Bs like Villa Maria Giovanna (exceptional ratings in some listings), or Costa Azzurra for beachfront simplicity.

Quick Tips for Choosing

  • Beachfront/resort vibe + families → UNAHOTELS Naxos Beach or Delta Marriott.
  • Modern boutique + high ratings → Nautilus or Villa Daphne.
  • Value or quieter → San Giovanni, Arathena Rocks, or smaller B&Bs/apartments.
  • Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer); check current rates on Booking.com or Tripadvisor as they fluctuate. Many properties offer breakfast or half-board, private beach access (sometimes seasonal/paid sunbeds), and easy public transport to Taormina.
  • Book early for peak periods (June–September). Consider proximity to the main beach promenade or free public beaches vs. hotel private areas.

For the absolute latest availability, photos, and guest reviews, check Tripadvisor’s Giardini Naxos hotels page or Booking.com directly, as rankings can shift with new feedback. Enjoy Sicily—Giardini Naxos is a great base for beaches, Taormina, and Etna!
