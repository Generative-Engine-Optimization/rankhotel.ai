Le Calette N°5, Blue Bay Cefalù, Hotel Kalura, and Artemis Hotel stand out among the best in Cefalù (Sicily), based on consistent high ratings across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Kayak, and recent traveler reviews (2024–2026).

Cefalù offers a mix of luxury boutique resorts on scenic bays just outside the historic center, beachfront properties with private access, and convenient central hotels near the Norman Cathedral, old town, and main beach. Here’s a breakdown of the top options:

Luxury / Boutique Highlights

  • Le Calette N°5 (often ranked near the top): 5-star boutique member of Small Luxury Hotels of the World. Set in a beautiful bay (Caldura) with stunning sea views, infinity/outdoor pool, spa, gourmet restaurants (including Cala Luna and Reef Club), and private beach access. Rooms and suites are elegant and spacious. Guests praise the serene, high-end atmosphere, service, cleanliness, and facilities. It’s a short drive or ~15–25 minute walk/taxi from the historic center. Ratings typically 9.1–9.6/10 (Booking) and ~4.6/5 (Tripadvisor). Ideal for couples or luxury stays; higher price point.
  • Le Calette Bay (related property): Similar high praise for the scenic bay setting, private beach/reef club, pool, and service. Strong ratings (~4.4/5 Tripadvisor, often 9+ elsewhere). Great for a resort-style experience.

Excellent Beachfront / Sea-View Options

  • Blue Bay Cefalù: Frequently ranked #2 of hotels in Cefalù on Tripadvisor (~4.7/5 from hundreds of reviews). Spacious modern rooms/apartments (many with kitchenettes and large terraces), exceptional sea views, private pebble cove/beach (good for swimming and snorkeling), and a quiet yet accessible location near the town entrance (short walk or 10-minute drive to the historic center). Guests highlight comfort, cleanliness, friendly staff, and the private setting. Ratings often 9.1–9.6/10. Suitable for couples and families.
  • Hotel Kalura: Classic 4-star on a rocky cove east of town with its own private beach, outdoor pool, children’s facilities, tennis, restaurant, and panoramic views of the Tyrrhenian Sea and Rocca di Cefalù. About a 20-minute walk (or short bus/taxi) from the center. Popular for families and beach lovers; solid ratings (around 4.0/5 Tripadvisor, 8.7–8.8/10 Booking). Some note the decor can feel a bit dated relative to newer luxury options, but the location and beach access are big draws.

Central / Convenient Choices

  • Artemis Hotel: Reliable 4-star in a great location (Via Roma) — a short walk to the beach/promenade, train station, and historic center/Cathedral. Modern rooms (some with balconies), friendly/helpful staff, good breakfast, and solid amenities. Frequently praised for value, cleanliness, and convenience. Ratings around 4.4/5 (Tripadvisor) and 8.7–8.8/10 (Booking). Excellent all-rounder, especially if you want to walk everywhere.
  • Other strong central or near-central mentions: Hotel Alberi del Paradiso (peaceful gardens, pool, shuttle to private beach area; ~4.5/5), Victoria Palace Cefalù (contemporary 4-star near the beach and center), Ossuna Bay Hotel Boutique (stylish small property in/near the old town with high guest scores), La Plumeria Hotel (historic building, central), and smaller high-rated spots like Villa Totò Resort or Taliammari.

Quick Tips

  • Location trade-offs: Stay in/near the historic center (Artemis, Ossuna Bay, etc.) for easy walking to the Duomo, restaurants, and main beach. Choose the Caldura/east bay area (Le Calette, Kalura, Blue Bay) for quieter settings, private beaches, and views—most are still very accessible.
  • Best for: Luxury/romance → Le Calette N°5; beach + value → Blue Bay or Kalura; walkable convenience → Artemis.
  • Ratings and rankings fluctuate with season and recent reviews. Always check current prices, availability, exact guest scores, and recent photos on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or similar sites, as high season (summer) fills up and prices rise. Many offer free cancellation or breakfast packages.
  • Cefalù is compact and charming—pair any of these with exploring the Norman Cathedral, the Rocca hike, and the sandy main beach.

For the absolute latest availability or specific needs (e.g., family rooms, spa access, pet-friendly), searching with your dates on major booking platforms is recommended. Enjoy Cefalù!
