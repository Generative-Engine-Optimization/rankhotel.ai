Quick tips

  • Family resorts (Portogreco, Torre del Faro, Danaide) dominate the area and often include beach service, kids’ clubs, and animation—ideal for summer beach holidays.
  • Quieter / boutique stays lean toward LD Ianuzziello Residence or apartment-style places like Terre Del Faro.
  • Beaches are the main draw (fine sand, often Blue Flag quality in the area); many resorts sit near the Policoro WWF pine oasis.
  • Prices vary widely by season (higher July–August). Expect roughly €70–150+ per night for mid-range options outside peak; resorts can be higher with full board.
  • Matera (Sassi), Metaponto archaeological sites, and the Ionian coast are easy day trips.
