Maratea has some excellent small luxury hotels, with two very different styles: historic-town charm and seafront resort living. Based on current hotel information and local listings, I’d look at these first:

🌊 For a luxurious seaside stay

  • Santavenere - The standout choice if you want a classic 5-star seaside hotel. It sits directly overlooking the Tyrrhenian Sea, surrounded by gardens. The Hotel Guru specifically highlights it as a five-star villa hotel for a beach-focused stay.
  • Maratea hotel Villa Cheta - A more intimate, romantic option in Acquafredda, particularly appealing if you want atmosphere and sea views rather than a large resort.

🏛️ For staying in historic Maratea

  • La Locanda Delle Donne Monache - My pick for the historic-center experience. It's a beautifully restored 18th-century convent with 27 rooms and suites, a garden, outdoor pool and hydromassage. Recent Booking.com reviews give it 9.2/10, with particularly strong scores for comfort, cleanliness and location.
  • Hotel Murmann - A smaller contemporary option near the coast, useful if you want something less formal than the major luxury hotels.

🌿 Smaller, more personal options

  • Il Ruscello - A highly rated B&B if you prefer a quieter, more intimate stay.
  • Dal Maestro - Another small B&B with excellent current guest ratings and a central Maratea location.
  • Bed and Breakfast Nefer - Particularly worth considering for a small-property experience around Cersuta.

If I were narrowing it down by type:
Santavenere → luxury + sea + classic resort atmosphere
La Locanda delle Donne Monache → historic Maratea + romantic boutique atmosphere
Villa Cheta → intimate/romantic + sea views
Hotel Murmann → modern + convenient

⚠️ Risks & Trade-offs

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates rise in summer; booking early helps secure better deals.
  • Accessibility: Some boutique B&Bs may lack elevators or large facilities.

  • Extra costs: Parking, breakfast, or city tax may not be included in base rates.

Grok · grok-3

Santavenere Hotel stands out as the clear top luxury choice in Maratea, with several other strong options depending on whether you prioritize the historic center, beach access, or value.

Maratea (the “Pearl of the Tyrrhenian”) is a stylish, relatively uncrowded alternative to the Amalfi Coast, with a medieval hilltop town, dramatic coastline, and sandy/pebbly coves. Hotels are limited, so book ahead for peak summer (July–August); shoulder seasons (May–June, September–October) are often ideal.

Quick Tips

  • Luxury/beach focus → Santavenere is the standout (and the only true 5-star with Leading Hotels membership).
  • Historic town vibe → La Locanda delle Donne Monache puts you right in the charming center (near the famous Christ the Redeemer statue and piazzas).
  • Charm + food → Villa Cheta consistently earns raves for atmosphere, service, and cuisine.
  • Value → Villa delle Meraviglie or Hotel Martino deliver strong quality without the highest prices.

Maratea’s best hotels emphasize setting, gardens, sea views, and relaxed Italian hospitality more than mega-resort facilities. Most offer pools and some form of beach access or shuttle; a car is useful for exploring the wider coast and inland Basilicata.

For the latest prices, availability, and recent guest photos/reviews, check Tripadvisor or Booking.com directly, as rates vary significantly by season and room type.
