The best hotels in and around Campomarino depend heavily on which "Campomarino" you are looking for, as Italy has two popular seaside destinations with this name. The primary town is Campomarino in the Molise region , known for its historic core and the bustling beach at Campomarino Lido. Alternatively, there is Campomarino di Maruggio in Puglia , famous for its crystal-clear Ionian waters and luxury masserie (historic fortified farms).

The top places to stay in both locations are grouped below, including a few premium beachside options in the neighboring city of Termoli (just a 10-minute drive from Molise's Campomarino).
