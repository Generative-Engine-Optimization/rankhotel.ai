Hotel Bermuda, Residence Rivaverde, Alba Hotel, and Hotel Maddalena stand out as the strongest options right in Marina di Ravenna, based on consistent guest ratings from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and similar sites. Nearby Terme Beach Resort (in adjacent Punta Marina) is also frequently recommended for a more resort-style experience.

Marina di Ravenna is a smaller, quieter coastal resort area near Ravenna (about 10–15 minutes by car or bus), known for its pine forests, sandy beaches, and proximity to the city’s mosaics and historic sites. Accommodation options are limited (mostly 2–3 star hotels and residences/apartments rather than luxury properties), with a focus on beach access, free parking, and family-friendly or self-catering stays. Ratings and availability can vary by season—summer is peak—and prices typically start around €70–150+ per night depending on dates.
