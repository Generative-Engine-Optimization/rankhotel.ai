Here are some of the top-rated hotels and accommodations in Piombino (Tuscany, Italy), based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Trip.com, and similar sites (as of 2025–2026 data).

Piombino is a coastal port town (main gateway to Elba Island) with a mix of historic-center hotels, sea-view resorts, family-oriented resorts, and agriturismi (farm stays). True luxury 4–5 star hotels are limited in the town itself—many highly rated options are smaller hotels, resorts, or apartments. Nearby areas like Baratti, Riotorto, or Venturina often appear in broader searches.

Top Recommendations in/near Piombino

  • La Rosa dei Venti Resort (Viale Giovanni Amendola 12)
    Frequently praised for its clifftop location with panoramic sea/Elba views, infinity/sea-view pool, small private-ish beach access, gym, restaurant, and stylish rooms (some with terraces). Ratings typically 8.2–8.6/10 (Booking) or ~4.2–4.4/5 (Tripadvisor). Guests highlight the views, peaceful atmosphere, friendly service (owner Antonio often mentioned positively), and good restaurant. Ideal for couples or a scenic stay; walking distance to the center. Some notes on facilities feeling a bit dated or pool being shared/busy in peak season.

  • Albergo Italia (Via XX Settembre 39)
    A solid, well-reviewed 3-star option right in the historic center (near restaurants, castle area, and convenient for the port/ferries). Ratings around 8.3/10 (hundreds of reviews on Booking). Guests consistently praise the central location, cleanliness, friendly/helpful staff, and good Italian-style breakfast. Rooms are comfortable but can be compact or slightly dated. Good value and practical for short stays or ferry connections; parking assistance often available.

  • Poggio All’Agnello Sport & Family Holidays (Via dei Flabelli 31)
    Larger resort-style property with sports facilities (pools—including Olympic-sized in some descriptions—tennis, bikes, diving, etc.), family amenities, restaurant, and spacious apartments/studios. Ratings around 8.4/10 or 4.1/5 on Tripadvisor (hundreds of reviews). Popular for active/family holidays, nature setting near Baratti Gulf, and organized facilities. Some mixed feedback on cleanliness or staff in older reviews, but many recent ones are positive about organization and amenities.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Agriturismo / Glamping Gli Etruschi (and similar farm stays) — High ratings (often 8.8–9.2+), nature immersion, family/pet-friendly, tents or apartments, relaxed vibe. Great for a countryside experience near Piombino.
  • Hotel Centrale or Hotel Est — Central options with solid practical ratings; Hotel Est often ranks well for value on Tripadvisor.
  • Highly rated apartments/B&Bs (e.g., La Vista Storica ~9.5, APPARTAMENTI TRA CIELO E MARE ~9.3, Il Balcone sull’Elba, Piombino 1428) — Excellent for independent stays with modern facilities and strong guest scores, especially if you prefer self-catering.

Tips

  • For sea views/romance: Prioritize La Rosa dei Venti.
  • For convenience (center + ferries): Albergo Italia or similar central hotels.
  • For families/activities: Poggio All’Agnello.
  • For nature/quiet: Agriturismi around Baratti or Riotorto.
  • Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer); check current availability and recent reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as smaller properties can fill quickly. Many places are pet-friendly.

Piombino itself is more practical than glamorous, but the surrounding Tuscan coast (beaches like Baratti, Sterpaia) and proximity to Elba make it a good base. For the absolute highest-end resorts, look slightly farther afield (e.g., toward Follonica or Castiglione della Pescaia).
