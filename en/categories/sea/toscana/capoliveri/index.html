ChatGPT · gpt-5-6

If you're looking at Capoliveri on Elba, I'd focus on these properties, depending on the kind of trip you want:

  • Tenuta delle Ripalte - Best for a secluded, upscale countryside escape. It's on the Monte Calamita peninsula with dramatic sea views, vineyards, and a more private, estate-like atmosphere. Booking.com guests currently rate it around 8.6/10.
  • Hotel Capo Sud - Best for a classic beach holiday. Located in the Lacona area, with a strong reputation for service and a particularly high traveler rating (4.7/5 on Google business listings).
  • Hotel Dino - Best for a quieter seaside stay. It's in Pareti, south of Capoliveri, and is a good choice if beach access and a relaxed atmosphere matter more than being in town.
  • JR Resort Elba International - Best full-service resort option. It overlooks the Bay of Naregno and has a private beach and two pools.
  • Hotel Villa Rodriguez - Best smaller hotel / good all-rounder. It's frequently booked and currently scores 8.6/10 on Booking.com; guests particularly mention its sea views, breakfast, and convenient location.
  • Country Hotel Residence Da Pilade - Best for families or longer stays. It combines hotel rooms with residence-style accommodation and has a current Booking.com score of 8.8/10.
  • Residence Belvedere - Best apartment-style option. Particularly worth considering if you'd rather have more space and independence; its current business rating is 4.9/5.

If I were narrowing it down

Romantic / special occasion: Tenuta delle Ripalte
Beach-focused: Hotel Capo Sud
Resort with lots of facilities: JR Resort Elba International
Family / longer stay: Da Pilade
Quiet and scenic: Hotel Dino
Self-catering: Residence Belvedere

One thing to know about Capoliveri: many of the best properties aren't actually in the historic hilltop center. They're around Naregno, Pareti, Morcone, Lacona, or the Monte Calamita coast. That's great for beaches and views, but you'll generally want a car if you plan to explore the island.

When staying in Capoliveri on Elba Island, you have access to some of the most spectacular coastal views and beaches in Tuscany. The "best" place to stay largely depends on whether you are looking for a classic beach resort, an active estate tucked away in nature, or a charming boutique Bed & Breakfast.

Here are the top-rated hotels in Capoliveri and the immediate surrounding area, each offering a distinct style of stay.

Top Recommendations in Capoliveri

Best for: A premium, effortless beach vacation. Why choose it: Located directly on the popular Lido di Capoliveri beach, Hotel Antares provides the quintessential dolce far niente island vibe. It features a private beach area, a beautifully maintained outdoor pool, and an on-site restaurant serving phenomenal food. It's widely regarded by guests as an absolute must-stay when visiting Elba.

Best for: Nature lovers, sports enthusiasts, and privacy. Why choose it: Perched on a dramatic promontory surrounded by nature, Tenuta delle Ripalte is a massive wine estate and resort. It is a haven for peace and outdoor activities, offering an on-site bike trail, a dive center, horseback riding, and access to pristine, uncrowded private beaches.

Best for: A relaxed, quiet getaway. Why choose it: Located in the quiet Lacona area of the Capoliveri municipality, Hotel Capo Sud features independent small villas scattered throughout a lush Mediterranean park. It offers a private beach, a tennis court, an outdoor pool, and two highly regarded restaurants.

Best for: Couples seeking a boutique, intimate stay. Why choose it: This immaculately clean, beautifully landscaped 4-star boutique B&B is highly rated for its stunning views, intimate pool area, and central location. It sits right in the middle of Elba's main attractions, making it incredibly easy to explore the village of Capoliveri, Porto Azzurro, and nearby beaches.

Worth the Short Drive (Nearby Porto Azzurro & Portoferraio)

If you want absolute luxury or specialized spa amenities, you can look just outside the Capoliveri border:

For Luxury: The 5-star Hotel Villa Ottone in nearby Portoferraio sits in a historic 1900s villa with a private beach, high-end dining, and a luxurious spa.

For Views & Village Life: Hotel Plaza in neighboring Porto Azzurro overlooks the marina. It features a cliffside spa and incredible panoramic breakfast terraces.
