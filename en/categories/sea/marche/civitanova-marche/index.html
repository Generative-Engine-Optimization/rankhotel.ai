Gemini

The best hotels in Civitanova Marche vary depending on whether you are looking for a beachfront vacation, a modern business stay, or a relaxing countryside getaway. Here is a curated shortlist of the top-rated properties in the area:

Best for Luxury & Sea Views

Ghibli Boutique Hotel: Generally considered the top overall hotel in the city. This polished 4-star design hotel sits right on the promenade, offering rooms with picture-postcard sea views. It features two on-site restaurants, upscale family suites, and high-end modern amenities.

Hotel Miramare: Another excellent 4-star choice right by the water. Known for exceptionally warm and helpful hospitality, it offers classic comfort with great proximity to both the beach and the town center.

Best for Modern Comfort & Business

Cosmopolitan Hotel: Located a bit further inland near the main highway access point, this highly rated business-chic hotel is praised for its clean, comfortable rooms, professional service, and great breakfast.

Hotel Dimorae: Ideal for longer stays or travelers wanting more autonomy, this property offers clean, contemporary rooms, suites, and apartments.

Best for Relaxation & Romance

Nest House & Relax: A highly-rated intimate property just a short walk from the southern beach (Spiaggia Libera). It stands out for its wellness focus, featuring dedicated spa facilities and deluxe rooms with private in-room hot tubs.

Hotel La Radice: If you prefer rolling hills to the beach, this relaxed countryside retreat sits just outside the core center. It offers simple, peaceful quarters, a private garden, and an outdoor swimming pool perfect for summer lounging.
