Top Recommendations

Here are standout options that appear frequently across major platforms:

  • Manè Boutique Hotel (Via Somalia 15)
    Modern 3-star boutique hotel near the beach and center. Guests praise the clean/spacious rooms (often with sea-view balconies or terraces), friendly staff, good breakfast selection, and convenient location (a short walk to the sea and promenade). Ratings typically around 4.3/5 on TripAdvisor and 8.8–9.1/10 on Booking.com (with solid numbers of reviews). Some note occasional noise from nearby spots or limited parking. Great for couples or those wanting a stylish, central base.

  • Agriturismo Frontemare (Via degli Acquaviva, Cologna Spiaggia area)
    Beachfront agriturismo/country house with private beach access, garden/lawn areas, and a restaurant known for excellent seafood and local dishes. Reviewers highlight the peaceful setting, well-kept grounds, helpful owners/staff, and overall value. Ratings commonly 8.8–9.2 range. Ideal for a quieter, more authentic stay with direct sea access—popular with couples and families.

  • Albergo Gemma
    Very close to the beach (often described as a 1-minute walk). Guests like the professional/kind staff, restaurant, cleanliness, and solid value. Booking.com scores around 9.1 (Wonderful) based on a good volume of reviews. A reliable mid-range choice.

Other Strong Contenders

  • La Casa Di Filò Boutique / B&B — Frequently earns near-perfect or very high scores (e.g., 5.0 on some TripAdvisor listings or 9.7 Excellent elsewhere). Modern, clean rooms, excellent breakfast, central yet peaceful feel.
  • Seta Luxury — High Booking scores around 9.5 (Exceptional) with mountain views and good amenities; more apartment-style.
  • Terrazza sul Mare — Strong reviews (around 9.1 with hundreds of ratings), attentive hosts, modern and meticulous.
  • Di Matteo Hotel — Popular value option with outdoor pool, private beach access nearby, and many reviews (typically mid-to-high 8s). Good for families.
  • Elite Boutique Hotel — Near the beach with a seasonal outdoor pool and free bikes; solid mid-to-high ratings.
  • Villa Imago and Agriturismo Il Borgo degli Ulivi — Frequently appear in traveler rankings for modern amenities, food, or peaceful settings.

Quick Tips

  • Beach proximity is a big plus here—many top places are steps from the sand or have private beach areas.
  • Seasonality matters: Summer is peak (book early); spring/fall are quieter and often better value.
  • Look for properties with private beach access, gardens, or good on-site dining if that’s important. Agriturismi like Frontemare offer a more rural/coastal feel, while boutique hotels suit those who want to be closer to the town promenade and restaurants.
  • Compare current prices and availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or similar, as they change frequently. Guest reviews emphasize cleanliness, staff friendliness, and location most often.
