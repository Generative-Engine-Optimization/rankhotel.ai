Victoria Hotel consistently ranks as one of the top (often the #1) hotels in Pescara, followed by strong options like G Hotel Pescara, Hotel Plaza, and beachfront properties such as Hotel Maja.

Pescara is a lively Adriatic coastal city in Abruzzo, Italy, with a long sandy beach, a pedestrian shopping/dining area (Corso Umberto I), and good transport links (train station and nearby airport). Most highly rated hotels are solid 3–4 star properties or boutique/B&Bs rather than large luxury resorts. Other Strong Options

  • High-rated smaller properties and B&Bs (often 9.5+) include places like B&B Oasi, Thebed, Le Mie Città, Marini Bed&Breakfast, and various luxury-style suites or boutique stays. These can score extremely well but typically have fewer reviews.
  • For more upscale or spa-focused stays, Victoria Hotel stands out locally; true large-scale luxury is limited in the city center itself (nearby countryside or other Abruzzo spots offer more resort-style options).

Tips for Booking

  • Location matters: Choose Victoria Hotel, Plaza, or Alba for the lively center + easy beach access. Go for Maja or Regent if you want to be right on the sea. G Hotel is ideal if arriving/departing by train.
  • Prices vary by season (summer is peak on the Adriatic). Expect mid-range rates for most of these; check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites for current availability and deals.
  • Guest feedback frequently highlights friendly staff, cleanliness, and convenience more than ultra-luxury amenities.
  • Pescara works well as a base for exploring Abruzzo (beaches, national parks, or day trips).
