Grok · grok-3

Hotel Mara, Ortona’s standout beachfront option, along with highly rated B&Bs like OrizzonteMare and the historic Torre Della Loggia, rank among the best.

Ortona (Abruzzo, on the Adriatic and gateway to the Costa dei Trabocchi) has a mix of beach resorts at Lido Riccio and smaller historic or countryside stays. Options are limited compared to bigger Italian destinations, with many highly rated properties being B&Bs or boutique spots rather than large luxury hotels. Rankings draw from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and similar sites (ratings as of recent 2025–2026 data).

Top Picks

  • Hotel Mara (4-star, Lido Riccio): Classic beachfront hotel with private beach access, outdoor pool (including options for kids), restaurant, and sea-view rooms with balconies. Guests praise the friendly/professional staff, cleanliness, comfort, and food (especially seafood and pizza). Ratings typically ~4.3/5 on Tripadvisor (400+ reviews) and 8.5/10 on Booking. Ideal for beach-focused or family stays; a short drive from Ortona’s historic center. Some note limited non-seafood menu options or peak-season beach/sunbed rules.

  • OrizzonteMare (B&B): Exceptionally high-rated (often 9.8/10 on Booking with 300+ reviews; 5/5 on Tripadvisor with fewer). Modern, clean rooms (some with garden/sea views or balconies), free bikes, garden, sun terrace, hot tub, excellent homemade breakfast, and very welcoming hosts. Quiet location a few km from the center; free parking. Strong choice for couples or those wanting a peaceful, personalized stay.

  • Torre Della Loggia - Dimora Storica: Charming restored 12th-century historic residence in the medieval Terravecchia quarter, steps from the Aragonese Castle and Cathedral of St. Thomas. Sea views from terraces/loggias, garden, and a romantic atmosphere blending history with modern comforts. Ratings around 4.0/5 (Tripadvisor) to 8.3–8.4/10 elsewhere. Great for culture-focused visitors; central location.

Other Strong Contenders

  • La Casa del Palombaro: Frequently highly rated (~4.7/5), family-run with a warm atmosphere, clean rooms, and good breakfast. More countryside/relaxed vibe.
  • Domora BEDROOMS: High scores (often 4.8/5 or 9.4/10); modern and well-reviewed for comfort and location.
  • B&B Falcone: Near the sea, spacious/clean rooms, attentive owners, and strong breakfast feedback (ratings ~4.7–9.2).
  • Aurum Ortona: Beach-area option with pool and easy sea access; solid mid-range reviews.

Tips

  • Beach vs. center: Choose Lido Riccio (Mara, Aurum) for direct beach/pool time. Opt for the historic center (Torre Della Loggia or similar) for walkable access to the castle, cathedral, and WWII history sites.
  • Nearby alternatives: Consider properties in San Vito Chietino (e.g., La Chiave dei Trabocchi) for more options along the scenic Trabocchi Coast.
  • Practical notes: Many places offer free parking/Wi-Fi and breakfast. Peak summer fills quickly—book early. Ortona suits relaxed beach + light sightseeing trips; a car helps for exploring the wider coast or hinterland. Prices vary widely by season (often starting ~€80–150+/night for good options).

Check current availability and recent reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as ratings and amenities can shift. For specific needs (e.g., family rooms, pet-friendly, or dates), provide more details!
