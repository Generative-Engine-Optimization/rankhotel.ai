If you’re looking for a great stay in Chioggia, I’d focus on historic-center hotels for atmosphere and sightseeing, or Sottomarina for beach access. Current guest-review data points to several particularly strong options.

My shortlist

  • Hotel Grande Italia 4S - best for a classic hotel experience. A 4-star hotel right on Piazzetta Vigo in the historic center, with lagoon views. It currently has a 4.7/5 rating from about 936 reviews.
  • Palazzo Carlo Goldoni - best for character. A smaller B&B in the historic center, with a 4.7/5 rating.
  • Casa Sansera - best intimate B&B. Central, highly rated, and a good choice if you prefer something personal over a conventional hotel.
  • La Corte - excellent boutique/B&B option. Currently 4.9/5 from 131 reviews.
  • Mosella Suite Hotel - A Lagoon Retreat - best for a resort/beach feel. Located in Sottomarina rather than the historic center; currently 4.9/5 from 872 reviews.
  • Hotel Mediterraneo - good beach-oriented hotel. On the Sottomarina seafront, with a 4.5/5 rating from 800+ reviews.
  • Hotel Bristol - another established Sottomarina option, close to the beach.

One particularly notable alternative is Duomo Boutique Hotel: Booking.com currently shows 9.2/10 from more than 1,500 reviews, while Hotel Grande Italia is at 9.1/10 from more than 1,200.

If it were my trip: for a first visit to Chioggia, I'd stay in the historic center, particularly around Corso del Popolo/Piazzetta Vigo. It puts you in the atmospheric part of town rather than treating Chioggia simply as a beach destination.

⚠️ Key Considerations

  • Location matters: Staying in Chioggia’s historic center offers charm and easy access to canals, while Sottomarina provides beaches and lagoon views.
  • Booking pressure: Boutique hotels like Casa Sansera and Palazzo Carlo Goldoni have limited rooms and book quickly.
  • Price range: From ¥10,200 at B&B Hotel Airone to ¥23,060 at Palazzo Carlo Goldoni.
  • Style trade-off: Larger hotels (Grande Italia, Airone) offer facilities like gyms and pools, while boutique stays emphasize atmosphere and intimacy.

Gemini

Chioggia, often called "Little Venice," offers a wonderful mix of historic canal-side guesthouses and nearby beach resorts. The best hotels in Chioggia depend entirely on whether you want a full-service historic stay right on the water, a relaxed boutique guesthouse tucked into the narrow streets, or a luxury resort retreat with private beach access.

🌟 Top Full-Service Picks

This formal 4-star hotel is located right on Hotel Grande Italia 4S at Piazzetta Vigo, offering panoramic views of the Venetian Lagoon. Why choose it: It is the premier classic hotel in town, complete with an on-site restaurant serving excellent local cuisine, a fitness center, and a sauna. Best for: Travelers seeking full hotel amenities, seamless service, and a front-row seat to the historic canals.

Technically situated in neighboring Sottomarina, Mosella Suite Hotel offers a more modern, resort-style escape. Why choose it: It features stunning harbor-view breakfasts, spacious suites, an outdoor pool, a gym, and access to a private area on the beach. Best for: Those who want a peaceful retreat, resort amenities, and easy access to the sea while remaining minutes away from Chioggia's historic center.
