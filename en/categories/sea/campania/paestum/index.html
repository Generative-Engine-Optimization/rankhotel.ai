Grok · grok-3

Here are some of the consistently top-rated hotels in and around Paestum (Capaccio-Paestum), Italy, based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and other travel sources. Paestum is known for its ancient Greek temples (a UNESCO site), so proximity to the archaeological park is a common priority, along with beach access, pools, and local cuisine.

Luxury / High-End Options

  • Savoy Hotel & Spa – Preferred Hotels & Resorts (5-star)
    Elegant resort with a large spa (Holos), pools, beach club access, and strong service. Recently renovated with stylish rooms. Good for a relaxed, upscale stay near the coast and temples. Highly praised for cleanliness, food, and facilities (Tripadvisor ~4.5/5 from 1,000+ reviews; Booking scores often around 8.8–9).

  • Il Cannito
    Intimate 4-room converted farmhouse/hideaway in the hills above Paestum (about 5–7 km away). Features private terraces, a pool with views of the temples and sea, and excellent Mediterranean cuisine using local produce. Ideal for a romantic or peaceful retreat. High praise for design, privacy, and atmosphere.

Boutique / Highly Rated Near the Archaeological Site

  • Tenuta Duca Marigliano Boutique Hotel (4-star)
    Very close to the ruins (often a short walk). Features a garden, pool, and the well-regarded Casa Coloni restaurant. Consistently strong reviews for location, staff, and comfort (Booking ~9.1 from hundreds of reviews; frequently tops “near the site” lists).

  • Kalos Paestum Hotel
    Newer, stylish option with high design standards, restaurant, and excellent location near the temples and beach. Outstanding recent scores (Booking often 9.7–9.8 from 100+ reviews). Guests highlight cleanliness, modern rooms, and food.

  • Borgo La Pietraia (4-star)
    Hilltop setting with panoramic views, Mediterranean gardens, pool, and strong local cuisine. Rooms are airy with terraces. Frequently praised for overall experience and value (Booking ~9.5–9.6).

Other Strong Contenders

  • Il Granaio dei Casabella — Converted barn right by one of the ancient gates; convenient for early/late temple visits, with solid restaurant and traditional style (Booking ~9.0).
  • Hotel Cerere and Hotel Le Palme — Popular for value, beach/pool access, and solid ratings (Tripadvisor often 4.6/5). Good family or longer-stay options.
  • Clorinda Paestum Hotel — High Tripadvisor ranking (~4.8/5) with family-friendly amenities and entertainment.

Tips:

  • For temple visits, prioritize Tenuta Duca Marigliano, Kalos, or Il Granaio.
  • For beach + spa luxury, choose Savoy.
  • For a quieter countryside escape with views, go for Il Cannito or Borgo La Pietraia.
  • Ratings and availability fluctuate by season (peak summer is busier and pricier). Check current prices and reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as small boutique places can book up quickly. Many offer free parking, Wi-Fi, and local Cilentan cooking.

Paestum pairs well with the broader Cilento National Park or day trips toward the Amalfi Coast. Enjoy the temples and mozzarella!

Best overall

Tenuta Duca Marigliano Boutique Hotel

A refined boutique hotel near the archaeological area, with attractive historic architecture, gardens, and a quieter atmosphere. It is one of the most consistently highly rated options in Paestum; Hotels.com lists it at 9.4/10, while Skyscanner identifies it among travelers’ top-rated stays.

Best for: couples, design-conscious travelers, and visiting the temples.

Best for the archaeological site

Hotel Villa Rita

Hotel Villa Rita is especially convenient for exploring the Greek temples and archaeological park. It is a relaxed, traditional property with gardens and a pool, and travelers rate it highly for location, amenities, and service.

Best for: first-time visitors, families, and anyone planning to spend substantial time at the ruins.

B&B La Chora di Paestum

A smaller, highly regarded bed-and-breakfast option near the ancient site. It is a good choice if you prefer personal service and a more intimate setting over full-service resort facilities. Skyscanner lists it among Paestum’s highest-rated accommodations.

Best for: couples and travelers who prefer a small B&B.

Best for the beach

Paestum Inn Beach Resort

This 4-star seafront resort offers a private beach area, free bicycles, and a seasonal outdoor pool with a hydromassage corner.

Best for: summer holidays, families, and travelers who want to combine Paestum’s temples with time by the sea.

Oleandri Resort

A resort-style property with pools, gardens, and family-oriented facilities. It is a practical choice for a longer stay or a beach-focused trip, although it is less intimate than the boutique hotels near the temples.

Best for: families, pools, and resort amenities.
