  • Sorriso Thermae Resort & SPA - A larger thermal resort with multiple pools and sea views, and generally a more moderate price point than the luxury properties.

Grok · grok-3

Botania Relais & Spa, Mezzatorre Hotel & Thermal Spa, Hotel Boutique Ideal, Hotel Punta Imperatore, and Hotel Residence Torre Sant’Angelo consistently rank among the top hotels in Forio (on Ischia, Italy), based on traveler reviews from TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites.

Forio is a popular area on the western side of Ischia known for thermal springs, beaches (such as Citara), gardens, and a mix of luxury wellness resorts and charming boutique/family-run properties. Rankings can shift with dates, season, and traveler priorities (luxury vs. value, spa focus, location near town or beach), so check current availability and recent reviews.

Top Luxury / Standout Options

  • Botania Relais & Spa (often 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor with hundreds of reviews; high 9s on Booking): A top-rated 5-star Relais & Châteaux/Leading Hotels property set in lush gardens. Highlights include thermal/spa facilities, multiple pools, excellent service, and a Michelin Green Star vegetarian restaurant (Il Mirto) using on-site produce. Peaceful and refined, with strong praise for staff, food, and atmosphere. Ideal for a high-end wellness retreat.
  • Mezzatorre Hotel & Thermal Spa (around 4.6/5 TripAdvisor; often 9.4+ elsewhere): Historic 5-star property in a former Aragonese watchtower on a rocky promontory with Gulf of Naples views. Features a private bay/beach area, thermal spa with multiple pools (including mineral waters), restaurants, and parkland setting. Stylish and glamorous, with strong reviews for location, spa, and overall experience.

Excellent Boutique / Highly Rated Options

  • Hotel Boutique Ideal (frequently 4.5–9.6 ratings): Family-run boutique favorite praised for warm hospitality (owners often mentioned by name), stylish rooms (many with hand-painted tiles or sea views), beautiful gardens, two pools (including thermal), and strong food. Convenient near beaches and town; guests often call it one of their best stays on the island.
  • Hotel Punta Imperatore (around 4.7/5 on TripAdvisor): Well-reviewed for location near Citara Beach and Poseidon Terme, thermal pools, gardens, and sea-view rooms. Charming rather than ultra-modern, with good value and convenient access.
  • Hotel Residence Torre Sant’Angelo (around 4.7/5): Family-run with dramatic cliffside/sea views (toward Sant’Angelo and beyond), thermal pools/spa elements, solid cuisine, and a peaceful setting. Frequently noted for excellent value and service.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Hotel Grazia Alla Scannella — Solid higher ratings and positive feedback for location and facilities.
  • Corallo Blu Hotel De Charme — High guest scores, sea/spa access.
  • Sorriso Thermae Resort & Spa or similar thermal resorts — Often appear in “best value” or popular rankings for all-inclusive/thermal experiences (ratings more mixed than the luxury picks above).

Tips: Ischia’s thermal waters are a major draw—many top hotels incorporate them into pools or spas. Forio offers good access to beaches, the Poseidon thermal gardens, La Mortella gardens, and ferry connections. Peak season (summer) is busier and more expensive; shoulder seasons can offer better value and milder weather. Prices vary widely (from mid-range boutique to luxury several hundred euros/night). Always verify current ratings, exact amenities, and booking policies on TripAdvisor, Booking.com, or hotel sites, as they update frequently.
