Choosing the right hotel in Amalfi is less about finding a "good" room and more about choosing the exact type of cliffside magic and layout you want. The town’s dramatic typography means a hotel might look close to the center on paper but require climbing hundreds of stone steps or catching a shuttle.

Here is a curated breakdown of the finest hotels in Amalfi, categorized by exactly what type of experience they deliver:

The Ultra-Luxury Great Dames

Every single space maximizes the blue of the sea, and their private beach club—complete with attentive staff catering to your every whim—is a serene haven. Logistics: Located just past Amalfi town, making it feel very exclusive and secluded.

Historic Monastery Conversions

The Vibe: Deeply historic, serene luxury inside a meticulously restored 13th-century Franciscan monastery. Concierge Tips for Amalfi Hotels

The "Luggage" Pitfall: If booking an independent villa or smaller boutique hotel away from the main road, always verify the step count. Grok · grok-3

The standout hotels in Amalfi (the town itself, plus the immediate surroundings on the Amalfi Coast) are primarily luxury properties known for dramatic cliffside settings, sea views, private beach access or pools, and strong service. Amalfi works well as a base because it is the main ferry and bus hub for the coast, with a livelier town center (Piazza del Duomo and the cathedral) than steeper spots like Positano—especially in the evenings after day-trippers leave.

Here are the top recommendations, drawn from recent expert guides (Santorini Dave, Condé Nast Traveler, National Geographic, U.S. News, Forbes, and others) and high guest ratings:

Top Luxury Picks

  • Hotel Santa Caterina — Frequently called the grand dame of Amalfi and a top overall choice. This classic, family-owned property sits on a secluded clifftop about 1 km from the center (complimentary shuttle available). Highlights include an elevator down to a private beach club and heated saltwater pool, a spa, terraced gardens, sea-view terraces, and Mediterranean restaurants (fine dining + casual). Rooms feature marble bathrooms, majolica tiles, and balconies; some suites have private pools. Excellent service and old-world glamour with modern comforts. Highly rated (around 9.8 on major sites). Ideal for classic luxury and easy sea access.

  • Borgo Santandrea — A standout newer luxury hotel (opened around 2022) on a dramatic cliff between Amalfi and Conca dei Marini (roughly a 10-minute drive). It offers one of the rare private sandy beaches on the coast (serviced), a saltwater pool, holistic wellness center, and refined restaurants. Design draws on mid-century Mediterranean style (inspired by Gio Ponti influences in some descriptions), with marble bathrooms, handmade tiles, sea-view balconies, and some suites with private plunge pools. Frequently praised for romance, design, and beach access.

  • Anantara Convento di Amalfi Grand Hotel — Converted 13th-century monastery (Capuchin history) perched on a cliff just above/near Amalfi town. It retains historic elements like a celebrated cloister, stone archways, and a church, blended with modern luxury: infinity-style pool, spa, terraces with sea views, and restaurants. Rooms are comfortable and unfussy with contemporary touches. Strong location for walking into town; often ranks #1 or very high in Amalfi-specific lists (e.g., U.S. News). Great for history + amenities.

  • Hotel Miramalfi — Highly rated 5-star boutique (often 9.8–9.9 guest scores, Michelin Key recognition in some recent coverage). Cliffside with excellent views over Amalfi and the sea, a private beach/pool area accessed by lift, restaurants, spa elements, and warm, attentive service with a family-home feel. Rooms typically include sea-view balconies/terraces. A strong alternative to the bigger-name classics, slightly outside the absolute center but convenient.

  • Hotel Luna Convento — Atmospheric historic option in a former Franciscan monastery (founded 1222, with ties to St. Francis legend), a short walk (~5 minutes) from Amalfi’s center. Features include a restored cloister, sea-view restaurant, a defensive tower now used as a restaurant, and a saltwater pool built into the coastal rocks with sea access. Traditional furnishings with modern comforts; good for those wanting history and a central location without full ultra-luxury pricing.

Other Strong Options

  • Hotel Marina Riviera — Solid in-town choice with sea views, rooftop pool/terrace, and convenient location near the waterfront and ferries. More mid-to-upper range than the pure luxury icons above; consistently high guest scores.
  • Midrange/central gems (if you prefer being right in the action near Piazza del Duomo): Residenza Luce (boutique with terrace views) or Albergo Sant’Andrea (unbeatable square location overlooking the cathedral).

Quick Tips for Staying in Amalfi

  • Book early, especially for May–October peak season—top properties fill up and rates rise significantly in high summer.
  • Skip the car if possible. Parking is scarce/expensive, and the coastal road is congested. Rely on ferries (scenic and efficient for Positano, Capri, etc.), buses (buy tickets in advance at tabacchi), or hotel shuttles/taxis.
  • Expect steep terrain and steps in many places; elevators/lifts at the top hotels help with beach access.
  • Prices for luxury properties typically start in the high hundreds to well over €1,000+ per night (depending on season, room type, and inclusions like breakfast), with shoulder seasons offering better value.

These recommendations prioritize consistently high rankings for location, views, amenities (especially sea access), service, and atmosphere specific to Amalfi town. Preferences vary—classic elegance (Santa Caterina), modern design + private beach (Borgo Santandrea), or historic monastery vibes (Anantara or Luna)—so check recent availability, exact room views, and current reviews for your dates. Enjoy the lemons, seafood, and coastline!
