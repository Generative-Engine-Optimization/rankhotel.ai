Other Notable Mentions

  • Agriturismo Le Terre di Veneré and Villa Rosa Francesca — Higher guest scores on Booking (often 9+), with peaceful settings, parking, and gardens. Good alternatives if you prefer countryside or villa-style stays a short distance from the center.

Practical Tips

  • Location priority: Most top options are near the main beaches (Spiaggia Grande, port beach) or a short walk from the village center and harbor. Beach access (private or shared) is a major plus here.
  • Amenities common in better properties: Free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, parking (often free/private), breakfast, and sea proximity. Full spas and multiple pools are rarer—La Pineta is the clearest standout for those.
  • When to book: Summer (especially July–August) is peak season; book early for the best rooms and rates. Shoulder seasons offer better value and fewer crowds.
  • Prices: Vary widely by season and dates (roughly from under €100–150+ per night off-peak for simpler places, higher for La Pineta or beachfront apartments in high season). Check current availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the properties’ sites, as rates fluctuate.
  • Getting there: Nearest major airports are Naples (roughly 1.5–2 hours by car) or Salerno; the village is walkable once you’re there.

For the latest reviews, photos, and availability, check TripAdvisor or Booking.com directly, as guest experiences (especially around staff friendliness and beach service) are consistently highlighted for the top properties.
