Here are some of the consistently best-rated and most popular hotels in Scalea (Calabria, Italy), based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites.

Scalea is a coastal town on the Tyrrhenian Sea (Riviera dei Cedri) known for its beaches, so proximity to the sea, pools, and hospitality stand out in top picks. Options range from classic beachfront hotels to smaller highly rated B&Bs. Ratings and availability change, so always check current reviews and prices.

Top Full-Service / Beachfront Hotels

  • Grand Hotel De Rose (4-star)
    Beachfront location with private beach access (chairs/umbrellas often included or available), outdoor pool, gardens, and sea-view rooms. Guests frequently praise the excellent location, friendly staff, breakfast, and classic/retro charm (some note the property feels a bit dated but still comfortable).
    Typical ratings: around 8.6/10 on Booking and 4.3/5 on Tripadvisor (hundreds of reviews). Strong choice for couples and families wanting easy beach access.

  • Hotel Talao
    Near the beach with pool, garden, restaurant, and often highlighted for hospitality, cleanliness, food, and a relaxed atmosphere. Many returning guests (including families) comment on attentive staff and good value.
    Typical ratings: around 4.6/5 on Tripadvisor. Frequently ranks among the higher-rated traditional hotels in town.

  • Santa Caterina Village (Hotel Santa Caterina Village Club)
    Popular family/resort-style option with direct beach access, pools (including kids’ facilities), and all-inclusive packages available. Praised for food, amenities, and convenience, though average scores are a bit lower due to the larger scale.
    Typical ratings: around 3.9/5 on Tripadvisor, but it has a high volume of reviews and strong family appeal.

  • Hotel Parco dei Principi
    Beach-oriented with pool and solid facilities; often appears in top lists for location and overall stay quality.

Highly Rated Boutique / Smaller Properties

  • La Suite del Faro
    Intimate option with outdoor pool, terrace, and strong guest feedback on cleanliness, views, breakfast, and hosts. Often scores in the 9.5–9.7 range. Great for a quieter, more personal stay.

  • B&B Torre Talao
    Excellent ratings (frequently 9.6–9.8/10), convenient location near the historic Torre Talao and beach area, spacious/clean rooms, and outstanding hosts (guests often single out personal service).

  • Kalispera Residence (and similar residences/aparthotels)
    Modern apartment-style stays with good access to beaches; high scores and practical for longer stays or families wanting more space/independence.

Other frequently mentioned well-reviewed spots include smaller B&Bs/guesthouses such as Suite 14, Il Belvedere Guest House, CIVICO 8, and I Registi (strong for location and guest satisfaction).

Quick Tips

  • Beach focus: Prioritize Grand Hotel De Rose, Hotel Talao, or Santa Caterina for direct or very easy beach access.
  • Families: Santa Caterina or Grand Hotel De Rose tend to work well.
  • Quieter/romantic or higher personal service: Look at La Suite del Faro or B&B Torre Talao.
  • Scalea has many apartments and B&Bs in addition to hotels—worth considering if you prefer self-catering.
  • Peak season is summer; book ahead and compare recent reviews for things like air conditioning, noise, and exact beach quality (some stretches are pebbly).
