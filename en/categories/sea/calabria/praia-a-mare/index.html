Quick Tips

  • Location priorities: Central seafront/town (Hotel Tea, Tea Luxury, Hotel Garden, Al Vecchio Pioppo) for easy walking to beaches, restaurants, and the promenade. Slightly elevated or hillside options (e.g., Blu Eden) for views and quieter settings.
  • What guests praise most: Clean rooms, friendly/helpful staff (especially at smaller B&Bs), proximity to the beach/sea, and good breakfasts.
