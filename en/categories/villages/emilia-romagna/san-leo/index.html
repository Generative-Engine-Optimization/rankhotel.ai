San Leo (the historic hilltop village in Emilia-Romagna, near San Marino) has limited accommodation—mostly small hotels, B&Bs, alberghi diffusi, and agriturismos rather than large resorts. Options center on the medieval borgo itself or the nearby countryside.

Other strong options

  • Terraluna Country House / Villa Vacanze San Leo – San Marino: Very high scores (often 9.8–9.9). More private/countryside feel, higher prices.
  • Casa Giogano: Exceptional ratings (near 10 in some listings), pool, pet-friendly—can be pricier.
  • Le Cicale B&B, Palazzo Serre, and Montefeltro B&B: Solid high reviews, good facilities (some with pools or strong views).

Practical tips

  • San Leo is tiny, so central properties put you steps from the fortress, piazza, Duomo, and restaurants. Countryside options (like Eutopia) are quieter but require a short drive or taxi.
  • A car is very useful for exploring the Montefeltro area and nearby San Marino.
  • Prices typically start around €80–150+ per night (higher in peak summer or for private villas). Book ahead, especially weekends and high season—inventory is limited.
  • Most places offer free WiFi, parking (sometimes underground or private), and breakfast. Many emphasize views of the Rocca or surrounding hills.

For the latest availability, prices, and reviews, check Booking.com or TripAdvisor directly, as options and scores can shift. San Leo works well for 1–2 nights to enjoy the fortress and atmosphere before or after the coast or San Marino.
