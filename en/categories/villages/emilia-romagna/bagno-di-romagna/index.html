Top Thermal/Spa Hotels (Most Popular for Wellness)

These stand out for access to Bagno di Romagna’s famous thermal waters:

  • Ròseo Euroterme Wellness Resort (4-star)
    Highly popular and frequently ranked among the top options. Large thermal pools (indoor/outdoor), spa treatments, wellness center, buffet restaurant, and family-friendly amenities. Ratings typically around 8.0–8.4/10 (Booking) or 4.0/5 (Tripadvisor, thousands of reviews). Location is central/convenient. Guests praise the pools and atmosphere; some note mixed service, rooms, or value depending on the season/package.

  • Hotel Delle Terme Santa Agnese (4-star)
    Historic building with free outdoor thermal pool, spa facilities, restaurant serving local Emilia-Romagna cuisine, and strong central location. Ratings around 8.2–8.4/10 (Booking) or ~3.8–4.3/5 across sites. Praised for location, cleanliness, staff, and thermal experience; good for couples and relaxation.

  • Hotel Miramonti (4-star)
    Scenic setting by Lake Acquapartita (slightly outside the main town center, surrounded by woods), with indoor/outdoor pools, spa, restaurant, and nature access. Often ranks very high (e.g., #1 of hotels on some Tripadvisor views), with ratings around 4.2/5 or 8.1–8.8/10. Strong reviews for staff attentiveness, cleanliness, food, and peaceful lakeside atmosphere.

  • Hotel Tosco Romagnolo (4-star)
    Frequently mentioned with wellness center, heated/rooftop pool options, restaurants, and good value. Ratings typically ~8.0–8.4. Solid choice for spa stays and central access.

High-Rated Alternatives (Often Smaller or Agriturismo-Style)

These frequently score higher on guest satisfaction (especially for quieter or more intimate stays) but may have fewer amenities or be slightly farther from the main thermal center:

  • Il Palazzo Ridracoli — Exceptional ratings (~9.5/10 on Booking with hundreds of reviews); restaurant, garden, peaceful setting near nature.
  • Agriturismo Incisa — Strong scores (~8.9); pool, nature views, pet-friendly.
  • Mulino delle Cortine, Lo Struzzo, Casina del Ponte, and similar agriturismi or B&Bs — Often 8.7–9.6+ ratings for cleanliness, location, and hospitality (fewer reviews than the big spa hotels).

Quick Tips

  • Best for thermal spas/wellness: Prioritize Ròseo Euroterme, Hotel Delle Terme Santa Agnese, or Hotel Miramonti.
  • Best overall guest scores: Smaller properties like Il Palazzo Ridracoli or top agriturismi often edge out the larger resorts.
  • Location notes: Central options (Santa Agnese, Euroterme, Tosco Romagnolo) put you near the historic center and main spas. Miramonti offers more nature/lake immersion.
  • Prices and availability vary seasonally (higher in peak spa/holiday periods). Check current rates and packages (many include thermal access or half-board) on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites, as reviews and offerings can shift.

Bagno di Romagna is ideal for relaxing thermal stays combined with hiking or exploring the Apennines—pair a spa hotel with time in the national park for the full experience.
