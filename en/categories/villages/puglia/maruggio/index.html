Grok · grok-3

Maruggio (in Puglia’s Province of Taranto, near the Ionian coast and Campomarino beaches) offers a mix of charming B&Bs, villas, and a larger resort-style hotel. Most highly rated options are intimate B&Bs and guesthouses rather than large chain hotels, with strong emphasis on hospitality, proximity to beaches (especially Campomarino’s sandy stretches and dunes), gardens/pools, and homemade breakfasts. Ratings come primarily from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Kayak, and similar sites (as of recent data).

Top-rated options

Here are standout places based on guest scores, review volume, and consistency across platforms:

  • B&B Le Vele (often ranks near the top)
    Exceptional ratings (around 9.6–9.7/10 on Booking/Kayak; 5/5 on Tripadvisor with 100+ reviews).
    Charming renovated villa in the countryside (Contrada Colonne), short walk (~800 m–1 km) to Giannarelli Beach and Campomarino. Features an outdoor pool with garden views, sun terrace, barbecue, free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, and highly praised homemade breakfasts. Guests love the warm hosts, nautical theme, cleanliness, and peaceful setting. Ideal for couples or small groups seeking a boutique feel.

  • Fraledune
    Near-perfect scores (5/5 on Tripadvisor with dozens of reviews; strong Booking ratings).
    Beachfront (or steps from the sea/dunes) in Campomarino di Maruggio (Mirante area), Blue Flag beach nearby. Recently renovated with sea views, panoramic terrace for breakfast, garden, stylish rooms, and excellent hospitality. Praised for location (quiet yet walkable to town/beach), homemade cakes/focaccia, and relaxed vibe. Great for a magical, nature-immersed stay.

  • Villa Bruno
    High scores (around 9.3–9.4/10; 4.7/5 on Tripadvisor with 100+ reviews).
    Very close to the beach and Campomarino center (often ~100 m). Offers rooms and apartments with garden/sea views, terraces, free bikes, garden seating, air-conditioning, and good breakfasts. Eco-friendly renovations noted by some. Family-friendly with strong location scores.

  • B&B Sole Mare Vento
    Excellent ratings (9.3/10 range; ~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor).
    In Maruggio (Via Crocifisso area), a short drive/walk toward the coast. Clean, comfortable rooms with AC, garden/terrace, free parking, and highly rated Italian breakfasts. Guests highlight hospitality, cleanliness, and value. Good central-ish base.

  • Grand Hotel dei Cavalieri
    Solid larger option (around 8.5–9.0/10 or 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor with 1,000+ reviews).
    4-star resort in Campomarino (Contrada Commenda/Vento), ~600 m from beaches/dunes with shuttle or short walk to private/affiliated lido. Amenities include outdoor pool, spa/wellness, restaurant (Puglian cuisine), bar, free parking, and 101 rooms/villas (some with balconies/patios). More full-service than pure B&Bs; suits families or those wanting facilities.

Other strong mentions

  • Pinulà (or B&B Pinulà) — High ratings (~9.4+), garden/terrace, near beaches.
  • Masseria-style or luxury villas (e.g., Masseria Le Fabriche, various country villas with pools) — Higher-end, often with pools and more space, though sometimes slightly outside the immediate center.
  • Smaller high-scorers like B&B Benefici, Mama Damì, or La Giada by Perle di Puglia appear frequently in top lists for location and hospitality.

Tips: Maruggio/Campomarino shines for beaches (fine sand, clear water, dunes), so prioritize sea proximity if that’s your priority. Peak season (summer) books up and prices rise—check current availability/prices on Booking, Tripadvisor, or Kayak, as they fluctuate. Many places are family-run with excellent personal service. Brindisi Airport is the nearest major one (~50–60 km). For the absolute latest reviews and rates, search the specific names, as offerings can change seasonally.
