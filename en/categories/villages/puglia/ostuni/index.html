Highly Rated & Popular Options

  • Masseria Salinola — Frequently ranks at or near the top on Tripadvisor (often 4.8/5 from 1,000+ reviews). A peaceful countryside masseria with pool, excellent food, and tranquil olive-grove setting. Ideal if you prefer a classic Puglian farm-stay experience over staying right in town.
  • Primo Ostuni Hotel — Modern 4-star with excellent recent scores (often 4.9/5 or 9.6/10). Comfortable rooms, strong breakfast, good location, and free parking. Very popular with recent guests for overall quality and convenience.
  • Ostuni Palace Hotel Bistrot & SPA — Elegant 4-star in the historic center. Pastel rooms, spa, restaurant/bistrot, and central location for exploring the old town on foot.
  • Hotel Monte Sarago — Reliable 4-star in a convenient central spot. Clean, comfortable rooms, good breakfast, and easy access for sightseeing.
  • Hotel La Terra — Charming historic property in the old quarter (dating back centuries). High guest ratings and atmospheric setting.

Other Strong Contenders

Countryside masserie and resorts (e.g., Masseria Santo Scalone, Città Bianca Country Resort) offer pools, gardens, and a more relaxed, authentic feel a short drive from town. Smaller luxury suites or boutique spots also score very highly if you prefer intimate stays.

Quick Tips for Choosing

  • Historic center / walkable → La Sommità, Paragon 700, Ostuni Palace, Primo Ostuni, or Hotel La Terra.
  • Views + modern luxury → Vista Ostuni.
  • Peaceful countryside + authentic masseria vibe → Masseria Salinola or similar.
  • Ostuni is compact and hilly—staying in or very near the whitewashed centro storico makes exploring easiest, while masserie give more space and quiet.
  • Prices vary significantly by season (higher in summer); book early for peak periods. Many properties include breakfast and have pools/spas.

For the absolute latest availability, rates, and reviews, check Tripadvisor or Booking.com directly, as scores and offerings can shift. If you have preferences (budget, pool must-have, romantic, family-friendly, etc.), I can refine the recommendations further!
