Leonardo Trulli Resort, Ottolire Resort, and Masseria Grofoleo rank among the top highly rated options in Locorotondo (Puglia, Italy), based on consistent traveler reviews across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Hotels.com, and similar sites.

Locorotondo is a charming whitewashed hilltown in the Valle d’Itria known for its circular historic center, trulli (traditional conical stone houses), and surrounding countryside of olive groves and vineyards. Many top stays are rural resorts or masserie (restored farmhouses) a short drive from the center, offering pools and quiet settings ideal as a base for exploring Alberobello, Cisternino, Martina Franca, and more. Historic-center options provide walkable access to restaurants and views but often involve parking outside the limited-traffic zone (ZTL).

Top Recommendations

Here are some of the best-regarded hotels and resorts (ratings approximate and based on recent aggregated guest feedback; always check current availability and prices, which vary by season):

  • Leonardo Trulli Resort (often 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor with 700+ reviews; ~9.4–9.6 elsewhere): A standout luxury-feeling resort in the countryside with authentic trulli accommodations, lush gardens/olive trees, a heated magnesium/saltwater pool, spa facilities, excellent homemade breakfasts, and strong dining. Guests frequently praise the peaceful setting, attentive (often family-run) service, and romantic atmosphere. It’s a short drive from Locorotondo’s center and well-positioned for the trulli area. Popular for couples and those seeking a relaxing base.

  • Ottolire Resort (frequently 4.7–4.8/5 or 9.4–9.8/10): A stylish 4-star countryside resort with modern-yet-traditional rooms/suites (some in trulli or cummerse with private features like jacuzzis), outdoor pool with views, spa (sauna, steam room, treatments), fitness options, free bikes, and a restaurant focusing on local/Mediterranean cuisine with good breakfast. It’s praised for quiet surroundings among olive trees, cleanliness, and staff. About a 10–15 minute drive from the historic center.

  • Masseria Grofoleo (very high scores, often 9.8–9.9/10 or 5.0/5 on Tripadvisor): An elegant restored 18th-century masseria with trulli and cummerse elements, panoramic outdoor pool overlooking the valley/Locorotondo, gardens, free parking, and strong breakfasts (homemade options). Guests highlight the location (peaceful yet walkable or a short distance to the center), hospitality, cleanliness, and authentic feel. Excellent for a more intimate, high-end rural stay.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Coppola di Seta: Highly rated (~9.8 or 4.9/5) trulli-style B&B/resort in the countryside with pool, spacious rooms, excellent breakfast, and welcoming hosts. Good value and strategic for visiting nearby towns.
  • Palazzo Gentile: Central location in Locorotondo with very high scores (~9.8); modern, clean rooms and helpful staff. Convenient for exploring the historic center on foot.
  • Sotto Le Cummerse (Albergo Diffuso): Atmospheric option with rooms/apartments in traditional buildings in or near the historic center; solid ratings for character and location.
  • Hotel Donna Crescenza or Tenuta del Lauro: Reliable mid-to-upscale choices closer to town or with good amenities like pools.

Tips for Staying in Locorotondo

  • Historic center vs. countryside: Center stays (e.g., Palazzo Gentile or alberghi diffusi) put you amid the whitewashed streets and restaurants but mean parking outside the ZTL and potentially more noise/crowds in peak season. Rural resorts offer pools, space, parking, and tranquility—most guests rent a car.
  • Best for: Couples and relaxation seekers favor the resorts with pools/spas. Families or longer stays may prefer masserie or places with more space. Authentic trulli experiences are widely available.
  • Practical notes: Book ahead for high season (late spring–early fall). Many properties include breakfast and free Wi-Fi/parking. Locorotondo works well as a quiet base for the Valle d’Itria. Prices often start from around €100–150+ for good options and rise significantly for luxury resorts in peak periods.
  • Check recent reviews on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or Hotels.com for the latest guest experiences, as small properties can vary, and confirm amenities (e.g., seasonal pools).
