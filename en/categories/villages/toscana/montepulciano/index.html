Some of the highest-rated and most frequently recommended hotels and boutique stays in Montepulciano (Tuscany) include a mix of historic properties in the hilltop center and countryside villas/resorts with pools and views. Ratings draw from sources like Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and travel guides (as of recent 2025–2026 data). Preferences vary by location (walkable historic center vs. peaceful countryside), style (intimate boutique vs. resort amenities), and budget.

Top picks in or near the historic center

  • Meublé Il Riccio — Frequently tops traveler lists for its central location just off Piazza Grande. Family-run medieval-chic hotel with excellent views (some rooms overlook the countryside or rooftops), clean/comfortable rooms, strong Wi-Fi, and warm hospitality. Guests praise the helpful owners/staff and convenience for walking to restaurants, shops, and sights. Highly rated (around 4.8/5 on Tripadvisor with 1,000+ reviews; often 9+ on booking sites). Good mid-range option.
  • La Locanda di San Francesco — Intimate 14th-century palazzo boutique with just four rooms, antique furnishings, library/bar, gardens, and panoramic views over the Val d’Orcia and Valdichiana. Includes a well-regarded restaurant/wine bar with terrace. Excellent for a romantic, historic stay in the heart of town (near the top of the hill). Strong guest feedback for views, atmosphere, and service.
  • Palazzo Nobile di San Donato — Renaissance-era palace B&B/guesthouse (often highlighted as a top luxury historic option) with spacious rooms featuring original 19th-century furniture, modern amenities, and Val d’Orcia views. Central within the city walls; praised for authenticity and comfort.

Excellent countryside/villa options (short drive from town)

  • Villa Cicolina — 16th-century noble residence restored as a small relais, set among gardens and olive groves just outside town. Features antique furniture, frescoes, a scenic infinity/pool area with views, restaurant (Tuscan cuisine), wine cellar, and cooking classes. Guests rave about the peaceful setting, warm staff, and pool views. Consistently high ratings (around 4.8/5 Tripadvisor; 9.5–9.7 on booking platforms).
  • Villa Poggiano — Restored 18th-century villa on ~6 hectares of parkland with rural views toward Montepulciano. Elegant suites/rooms with beams, terracotta floors, and antiques; notable for its historic travertine pool with statues/fountains. Family-run with strong hospitality, breakfast, and options for activities (wine tours, etc.). Highly rated for exclusivity and tranquility (often 4.8/5 or higher).
  • Terra Antica – Resort, Winery & SPA — Modern eco-focused resort/winery with suites, infinity pool, spa, and organic wines (Nobile di Montepulciano and more). Peaceful vineyard setting with tastings, workshops, and wellness. Strong recent scores (around 9.6 on Booking).
  • Precise Tale Poggio Alla Sala — Larger 5-star luxury resort in a neoclassical villa setting amid hills/vineyards (about 20 minutes away). Multiple pools, full spa/wellness, restaurant with Tuscan + international influences, fitness, and gardens. Good for those wanting resort amenities and views (ratings often 9.2–9.3).

Other strong mentions

Other frequently praised options include Agriturismo San Martino (rustic-chic eco villa with vegetarian/vegan focus and cooking classes), Palazzo Carletti, Salcheto WineHouse (vineyard setting with hot tub), Hotel I Due Cigni (family-run near the thermal area), and various highly rated agriturismi like La Bruciata or Relais Ortaglia.

Tips: Montepulciano’s historic center is steep and car-restricted in parts, so central stays are ideal for walking; countryside properties usually offer parking, pools, and quieter settings (car recommended). Book ahead for peak seasons (spring–fall), especially smaller boutiques. Check current rates/availability on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites, as prices vary widely (€100–400+ per night depending on season and room type). For wine lovers, many properties arrange Nobile tastings or are near vineyards.
