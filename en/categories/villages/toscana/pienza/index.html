If you’re looking for the nicest places to stay in and around Pienza, I’d focus on a few different styles rather than a single “best” hotel. Current guest-review data and hotel listings point to these as particularly notable:

🌿 In Pienza itself

  • Relais Cloister Pienza - Right in the historic center, in a former monastery. A good choice if you want to walk everywhere and have the medieval-town atmosphere on your doorstep.
  • Hotel Corsignano - A well-regarded, family-run hotel with Orcia Valley views, a terrace/hot tub and a Tuscan restaurant. Booking.com currently shows a 9.1/10 from roughly 785 reviews.

🏡 For a more luxurious Tuscan countryside experience

  • L'Olmo - In nearby Monticchiello, with an intimate, refined country-house feel. Tripadvisor currently gives it 4.8/5, and guests particularly highlight the suites, private patios and valley views.
  • Monteverdi Tuscany - A high-end destination property in Castiglioncello del Trinoro, with a much more secluded, design-focused atmosphere.
  • Il Miraggio in Val d'Orcia Relais & Spa - A good choice if a spa/pool and countryside setting are priorities.
  • Locanda in Tuscany - Rural resort-style stay surrounded by Val d'Orcia scenery.

Top in-town / central picks

  • La Bandita Townhouse — Frequently highlighted as a standout boutique option (minimalist-contemporary interiors in a traditional building, often called a former convent or townhouse). Central location on Corso il Rossellino, excellent restaurant with local produce, stylish rooms/suites (some with soaking tubs), warm service. Ideal for couples/romantics and foodies. High praise for design and atmosphere.
  • Hotel San Gregorio — Very popular, strong value, pet-friendly suites, outdoor pool (seasonal), garden, good breakfast, central but with views toward the Val d’Orcia. Frequently tops “best value” or traveler-ranked lists with hundreds/thousands of reviews.
  • Hotel Corsignano — 4-star with panoramic Val d’Orcia views, clean/comfortable rooms (some with balconies), solid breakfast, convenient location. Consistently high ratings and frequent top-10 appearances.
  • Relais Il Chiostro di Pienza (Relais Cloister Pienza) — Converted 13th-century Franciscan convent with cloister, garden, terrace, and views of the hills/historic center. Mix of modern comforts in a historic setting; often noted for atmosphere and value.
  • Piccolo Hotel La Valle — Close to the center (short walk), rooftop terrace with Val d’Orcia views, good base for exploring the town’s main sights (cathedral, Palazzo Piccolomini, etc.). Reliable mid-range choice with strong reviews.
  • Pienzalettings Suites — Highly rated suites in a historic central location (near Piazza S. Carlo/Palazzo Piccolomini). Spacious, stylish (Mediterranean/chic), clean, and well-reviewed for short stays.

Strong countryside / agriturismo options (short drive from town)

  • La Saracina (Relais La Saracina) — Traditional-style rooms/apartments in the Tuscan countryside, swimming pool, free parking/Wi-Fi, exceptional ratings (often near 9.8). Peaceful and scenic.
  • Tenuta Santo Pietro — Farmhouse-style with beautiful scenery, high scores for environment and personalized service. Good for a more rural, relaxed stay.
  • Podere Spedalone — Picture-perfect farmhouse with grounds, pool, fresh breakfast, optional dinners, rustic-comfortable rooms. Frequently tops TripAdvisor traveler rankings; about 10–12 minutes’ drive from town. Note: some older reviews mention limited A/C.

Other well-regarded mentions include Rossellino (central, renovated historic building), Monastero Sant’Anna in Camprena (historic monastery setting with strong reviews), and various smaller B&Bs or suites like Affittacamere Maria Gabriella.

Tips: Book early for peak season (spring–fall). Many places emphasize local food, wine, and Val d’Orcia views. Central hotels put you steps from the main square, shops, and restaurants; countryside ones offer more space, pools, and quiet.
