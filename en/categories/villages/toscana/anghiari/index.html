Grok · grok-3

Anghiari (a beautiful medieval hilltown in Tuscany’s Valtiberina, Province of Arezzo) has mostly charming B&Bs, agriturismi (farm stays), and small hotels rather than large resorts. Top picks consistently earn high ratings (often 9+ / 10 or 4.8–5/5) for hospitality, views, pools, and authentic Tuscan atmosphere.

Here are some of the best, based on guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites:

Top-Rated Standouts

  • Relais Il Pozzeto (Il Pozzeto): Frequently ranks among the highest (around 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor with 100+ reviews; ~9.9 on Booking with 200+ reviews). A restored 17th-century Franciscan monastery/relais on a hilltop with panoramic valley views, outdoor pool, gardens, and excellent homemade Tuscan food (breakfast and dinners by hosts Mario and Carine). Peaceful and romantic; guests rave about the hospitality and setting. A short drive from the town center (note: access involves an unpaved road).

  • Nero Gioconda: Exceptional scores (often 9.9/10 or 5/5). Central location in the historic center with panoramic views over the Tiber Valley. Stylish rooms, attentive host (frequently praised), free Wi-Fi, and a cozy atmosphere. Ideal if you want to walk everywhere in town.

  • B&B Villa Cardeto: Near-perfect ratings (5/5 on Tripadvisor with 200+ reviews). A Tuscan villa on a hill near Anghiari with a large pool (great views of the Tiber Valley), garden, sauna area, and comfortable rooms/apartments in traditional style. Warm family-run hospitality; good for couples or families.

Other Strong Options

  • Il Cardo Resort: Highly popular (around 9.0–9.3/10 or 4+ stars). 15th-century stone farmhouse just outside town with luxury-style rooms (wood beams, exposed stone), outdoor pool with countryside views, spa/wellness options, and excellent food. Guests frequently praise the hosts (e.g., Dania) and relaxing vibe.

  • Agriturismo Il Sasso: Strong reviews (around 4.8/5 or 9.3/10). Family-run farm stay a few km from the center with a panoramic pool, gardens, playground, and authentic atmosphere. Good for nature lovers and families; breakfast with local products.

  • Hotel La Meridiana: Convenient 3-star option right in the historic center (Piazza IV Novembre area). Solid ratings (around 8.4–8.6/10), city/valley views from some rooms, restaurant/bar, free parking/Wi-Fi. Best for easy walking access to sights, museums, and restaurants.

  • Locanda Del Viandante B&B: High ratings (~9.3–9.4). Countryside setting with views, traditional rooms, and an on-site osteria/restaurant serving local cuisine. Peaceful base with garden and parking.

  • Agriturismo / Podere Tovari: Well-regarded farm stay with pool, spa elements, restaurant, and activities (e.g., nature experiences). Scenic location with good food and hospitality.

Tips for Booking

  • Style preference: Choose a central hotel/B&B (La Meridiana, Nero Gioconda) for walkability, or a countryside agriturismo/relais (Il Pozzeto, Il Cardo, Il Sasso, Villa Cardeto) for pools, views, and tranquility. A car is helpful for the latter.
  • Season: Summer is popular for pools; shoulder seasons (spring/fall) offer milder weather and fewer crowds. Book ahead for peak periods.
  • Prices: Vary widely by season and dates (often €80–€250+ per night for doubles); check current rates on Booking, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites as they fluctuate.
  • Nearby: Easy day trips to Sansepolcro, Arezzo, Cortona, or the Casentino forests. Anghiari itself is known for its historic center, museums (including the Battle of Anghiari links), and views.

Ratings and availability change, so verify recent reviews and book directly or via major platforms for the latest details. Enjoy Tuscany!
