Villa Moorings, Renaissance Tuscany Il Ciocco Resort & Spa, and La Mezza Luna (Residence la Mezzaluna) consistently rank among the top options in Barga, a charming medieval hill town in Tuscany’s Garfagnana area (Province of Lucca).

Barga has a relatively limited selection of hotels—mostly boutique properties, historic villas, B&Bs, and agriturismi—rather than large chains (with the notable exception of the Il Ciocco resort). Here’s a breakdown of the strongest contenders based on recent guest ratings from TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and similar sites:

Other Strong Contenders

  • Villa Gherardi — Frequently high on TripAdvisor traveler rankings (around 4.5–4.6/5). Central, clean, good views, and welcoming hosts. Solid value option.
  • Agriturismo I Cedri or similar farm stays (e.g., Al Benefizio, Il Musaccio) — Excellent ratings (often 4.9/5), pools, gardens, peaceful countryside setting. Popular for groups or special occasions.
  • Art Boutique Hotel Acchiappasogni or Casa Cordati — Smaller, characterful options right in or near the historic center with artistic/historic flair and strong guest feedback.

Practical Tips

  • Central vs. resort: Stay at Villa Moorings (or similar central properties) if you want to walk everywhere in Barga’s centro storico. Choose Il Ciocco for full resort amenities and sweeping views.
  • Getting around: The historic center is best on foot. A car is very useful for exploring the surrounding Garfagnana hills, Lucca (≈45–50 minutes), or the coast.
  • Amenities: Many places offer outdoor pools (seasonal), free parking, and breakfast. Higher-end options add spas and restaurants.
  • Prices: Expect a wide range depending on season—simpler or smaller properties often start lower, while the resort commands premium rates. Summer and festival periods book up faster.
  • Booking advice: Check recent reviews and current availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Marriott (for Il Ciocco), as small properties can have limited rooms.

Barga itself is known for its cathedral, views, Scottish connections, and relaxed atmosphere—pairing well with a stay that emphasizes Tuscan character over big-city polish.
