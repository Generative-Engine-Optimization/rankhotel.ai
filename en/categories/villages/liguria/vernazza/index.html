Vernazza has relatively few traditional hotels; many of the best places are small boutique guesthouses, rooms, and B&Bs. Based on current guest ratings and reviews, these are the ones I’d look at first:

My shortlist

  • La Malà - Best for a romantic stay & sea views. A charming, intimate property with a terrace overlooking the sea. Booking.com currently shows 9.7/10 from ~196 reviews.
  • Santa Marta Rooms - Cinque Terre - Best modern option. Stylish, recently updated rooms in a very central location; Booking.com shows 9.2/10.
  • La Polena & La Ripa - Vernazza 5 Terre - Best for character and location. Small-scale accommodation right in Vernazza, with the atmospheric feel you'd expect from the village.
  • Il Sorriso di Vernazza - Pensione Sorriso - Best traditional hotel feel. Has a garden, terrace, restaurant and bar; Booking.com gives it 9.0/10 from 400+ reviews.
  • Hotel Gianni Franzi - Best for the classic Cinque Terre experience. It's right below Doria Castle, with rooms close to the heart of Vernazza; 8.6/10 from 1,500+ Booking.com reviews.
  • Camere Fontanavecchia - Good value. Highly regarded on Tripadvisor (4.6/5) and a good choice if you don't need a full-service hotel.

Gemini

Vernazza is best known for its intimate, cliffside boutique guesthouses, historic "affittacamere" (room rentals), and upscale apartments rather than traditional large-scale luxury resorts. Because of the town's steep, vertical layout, almost all properties require navigating steps, but they reward you with incredible harbor views and authentic Italian charm.

Premier Sea Views 🌅

The Vibe: Understated, quiet, relaxed elegance. Standout Feature: Perched high on the cliff with a spectacular sun-bleached terrace offering direct 180-degree views of the Ligurian Sea. Good to Know: It is widely considered the premier boutique stay in town. Be prepared to climb some steep, winding stairs to get to the front door, which locals consider part of the charm. Check room availability directly via La Malà Official Site.

Central & Historic Stays 🔑

The Vibe: Modern comfort wrapped inside a traditional village townhouse. Standout Feature: Excellent location on the main street with cute private balconies overlooking the lively thoroughfare and green hillsides. Good to Know: Extremely highly rated for hospitality and cleanliness. Bookings can be arranged via Santa Marta Rooms.
