Grok · grok-3

Noli (on the Ligurian Riviera, near Savona, Italy) is a charming medieval seaside town with mostly boutique hotels, B&Bs, and small historic properties rather than large resorts. Top options stand out for high guest ratings (especially on Booking.com and Tripadvisor), location near the beach or old town, cleanliness, hospitality, and atmosphere.

Top-rated picks

Here are some of the consistently best-regarded places based on recent guest reviews and rankings:

  • Ca' de Tobia (B&B)
    Frequently ranks as the #1 or highest-rated option. Exceptional scores (around 9.7/10 on Booking.com from 100+ reviews; 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor from 250+ reviews). Right by the beach and a short walk to the historic center. Guests praise the stylish, carefully decorated rooms (with historical/coral-inspired touches), impeccable cleanliness, sea-view breakfast terrace, and outstanding host Andrea. Ideal for couples seeking a special, welcoming stay.

  • Paradiso Di Manù (hotel/guesthouse, often listed as 4-star)
    Strong ratings (around 9.6/10 on Booking.com from ~250 reviews; solid Tripadvisor scores). Set on a hillside with a seasonal outdoor pool, garden, bar, and panoramic views over the bay. Rooms/suites often have balconies or terraces; breakfast is frequently highlighted as generous and delicious. A bit outside the center (~1 km / short drive or walk), peaceful, and good for those wanting a pool and quieter setting. Some suites offer extra privacy or spa-like features.

  • Villa Salvarezza
    High ratings (often ~9.5 on Booking.com). Located inside the historic Castello di Noli complex, very close to the sandy beach. Rustic-style accommodations, family-run hospitality, and breakfast options indoors or in the courtyard. Pet-friendly in some reports; popular for its unique setting and atmosphere.

  • Palazzo Vescovile Hotel
    Historic property in the former bishops’ palace complex. Ratings typically in the 9.0–9.2 range. Free parking and Wi-Fi; a panoramic elevator connects to the beach. Classic-style rooms; good for those wanting character and convenient beach access.

  • Villa Aga
    Adults-only with an infinity pool, garden, sea views, balconies, free parking, and Wi-Fi. Ratings around 9.4. Quieter location away from the busiest beach area; praised for the owner’s helpfulness and overall experience.

Other solid options

  • Hotel Miramare — Beachfront in the historic center (very close to the sea). Classic air-conditioned rooms, free Wi-Fi; solid mid-to-high ratings (often ~8.7) and popular for location and views.
  • Hotel Italia — Recently renovated, in the pedestrian old town only steps from the beach. Air-conditioned rooms with private bathrooms; convenient and well-reviewed for location.
  • Relais Real Collegio — Near the beach with classic rooms and an internal courtyard; good staff feedback and solid ratings (~8.8).
  • B&B Cà de Badin (and similar small B&Bs like Elfo degli Ulivi) — Often score very high (9.7 range) for personal hospitality, cleanliness, and value in a more intimate setting.

Practical tips

Noli’s best stays tend to be small and characterful, so book early in peak summer (June–August). Prices typically range from roughly €100–300+ per night depending on season, room type, and demand (check current rates). Many places emphasize free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, and proximity to the beach or old town walls/castello. Parking can be limited in the historic center—properties with free private parking (like Palazzo Vescovile or Paradiso Di Manù) are convenient if you’re driving.

For the latest availability, prices, photos, and reviews, check Booking.com or Tripadvisor directly, as rankings and scores can shift slightly with new feedback. If you have preferences (e.g., beachfront, pool, budget, adults-only, or family-friendly), more tailored suggestions are possible!
