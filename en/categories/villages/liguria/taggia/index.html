What to expect

  • Most highly rated places are 3-star or boutique-style, not big resorts. Guests love the personal service, cleanliness, and proximity to the beach and cycle path.
  • Parking can be limited in the area—hotels with private parking or a garage (like Sappia and Giuan) are a big plus.
  • Many offer good breakfasts and are pet-friendly.

Nearby higher-end alternatives

If you prefer more luxury or spa facilities and don’t mind a short drive (5–15 minutes):

  • Royal Hotel Sanremo or Europa Palace / Miramare The Palace in Sanremo
  • Grand Hotel Del Mare Resort & Spa in Bordighera
  • Best Western Hotel Anthurium or Aregai Marina Hotel & Residence closer by in Santo Stefano al Mare

Tips

  • Arma di Taggia is the main beach area most visitors mean when they say “Taggia hotels.” The historic inland town of Taggia is quieter and more residential.
  • Book directly or via Booking.com/Tripadvisor for the latest availability and prices, as small hotels fill up in summer.
  • Check recent reviews for any specific needs (e.g., sea-view rooms, accessibility, or exact beach distance).
