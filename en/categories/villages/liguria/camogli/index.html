Because Camogli is a beautifully preserved, vertical fishing village, your choice will likely come down to whether you want to step directly onto the beach promenade or retreat up the hillside for quiet panoramic views.

Camogli is a charming Ligurian fishing village on the Italian Riviera (near Portofino), so top properties emphasize sea views, beach access, and proximity to the promenade, train station, and trails.

Top Picks

Here’s a breakdown of the standouts:

  • Hotel Cenobio dei Dogi (4-star)
    Frequently tops or near the top of lists. Guests praise its prime seafront location (near the train station and town center), private beach, heated seawater pool, excellent restaurants, strong breakfast, and attentive staff. It has a resort-like feel with thousands of reviews (around 4.6/5 on TripAdvisor and 9.3–9.4 on Booking/Trip.com). Ideal for a full-service stay with views over the bay.

  • Sublimis Boutique Hotel (Adults-Only / WorldHotels Elite) (4-star)
    A stylish 19th-century villa right on the sea with direct beach access, panoramic terraces, strong breakfast, and high service scores. Ratings are excellent (often 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor and ~9.1–9.4 elsewhere). Best for couples seeking a quieter, more intimate boutique experience (guests 16+). Sea-view rooms and sunset aperitifs are highlights.

  • Carrick Hotel Camogli (Portofino Coast) (5-star)
    Modern and well-reviewed for cleanliness, comfort, location near the station and water, professional staff, and quality breakfast. It often appears high in rankings (around 9.0). Good facilities including restaurant, fitness, garden, and parking options. Suits those wanting a polished, contemporary stay.

  • La Camogliese Hotel B&B (3-star)
    Excellent value with very high guest scores (frequently 9.6 on some platforms and strong TripAdvisor marks). Perfect location above the beach/promenade, friendly staff, comfortable rooms, and standout breakfasts. Many return visitors call it a quiet, welcoming base just steps from the action.

  • Villa Rosmarino
    A charming boutique villa/B&B higher up (short walk or drive from the center) with a lovely garden, outdoor pool, sea/hill views, and stylish, uniquely decorated rooms. Ratings are outstanding (often 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor and 9.4+). Great for a more secluded, design-focused stay with personal hospitality.

Quick Tips

  • Location matters: Seafront properties (Cenobio, Sublimis, La Camogliese, Carrick) put you steps from the beach, restaurants, and boats. Villa Rosmarino is quieter and elevated.
  • Seasonality & prices: Rates vary widely (often €150–500+ per night depending on season, view, and dates). Peak summer is busiest and most expensive; shoulder seasons offer better value and fewer crowds.
  • Other notes: Many places emphasize breakfast quality and staff hospitality. Parking can be limited in the historic center—hotels with private options are useful if driving. The area is walkable and well-connected by train for day trips to Portofino, Santa Margherita, or Genoa.

Check recent reviews and availability on sites like Booking.com or TripAdvisor for your exact dates, as ratings and offerings can shift. Camogli’s compact size means most highly rated stays put you close to the best of the village. Enjoy the pastel houses and Ligurian Sea views!
