The premier place to stay directly within the clifftop village of Framura is the Sesta Terra Natural Resort . Because Framura is a collection of small, quiet hamlets tucked into the dramatic Ligurian cliffs rather than a large resort town, traditional larger hotels are rare.

If you are looking for an immersive nature retreat, you'll want to stay right in town; however, if you prefer classic boutique hotel amenities, your best bet is to look just a few minutes away in neighboring Levanto or Deiva Marina , which connect effortlessly via the coastal train line.

Quick Tips

  • Framura is compact and spread across hillside hamlets, so many properties are a short drive or shuttle from the train station/beach. It’s ideal as a quieter base for Cinque Terre day trips rather than nightlife or big resorts.
  • “Best” depends on priorities: prioritize Sesta Terra for polished luxury + views/pool; Il Laghello or Sostio a Levante for high ratings + authentic countryside feel at better value.
  • Book early in peak season (summer). Check current prices, availability, and exact reviews on Booking.com or TripAdvisor, as rates fluctuate and many places have limited rooms.
  • Expect scenic but sometimes hilly access; a car can help, though trains connect well to nearby towns.
