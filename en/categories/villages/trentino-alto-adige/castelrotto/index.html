If you mean Castelrotto (Kastelruth) in South Tyrol, there are several excellent options. Based on current guest ratings, location, and the type of stay they offer, I’d look at these:

  • Hotel Villa Kastelruth - best for staying in/near the village. Highly rated (4.8/5 on Tripadvisor) and convenient for restaurants, shops and hiking.
  • Abinea Dolomiti Romantic SPA Hotel Südtirol - best for a spa/romantic trip. A 4.7/5 Tripadvisor rating with more than 1,200 reviews, plus a strong wellness focus.
  • Hotel Cavallino D'Oro Bed & Breakfast - best for charm and central location. It's right in the historic center of Castelrotto and has consistently high guest ratings.
  • Hotel Castel Oswald Von Wolkenstein - good traditional South Tyrolean option, with a 4.7-4.8/5 guest rating and spa facilities.
  • Hotel Lamm - best if you want to be right in the center. It's centrally located in Castelrotto and has a rooftop spa/outdoor hot tub.

⚠️ Risks & Trade-offs

  • Price premium: COMO Alpina Dolomites is significantly more expensive (£705/night).
  • Location: Some properties (Albion, COMO Alpina) are slightly outside Castelrotto, closer to Ortisei or Alpe di Siusi.

  • Hidden fees: Always confirm inclusions (Wi-Fi, spa access, breakfast) to avoid unexpected charges.

Quick Tips

  • Location matters: Central options (Cavallino d’Oro, Villa Kastelruth, Schgaguler, Abinea) put you in the pedestrian-friendly village. Properties slightly higher up (like Rosslaufhof) often deliver better views and quieter surroundings.
  • Season: Summer is ideal for hiking the Alpe di Siusi; winter for skiing (easy access via bus/cable car). Many hotels offer free or discounted Südtirol Guest Pass for public transport.
  • Booking: Prices vary significantly by season (higher in peak ski/hiking periods). Check current availability and half-board options, as many excel at food.
  • Ratings and rankings shift slightly with new reviews, but the hotels above consistently rank among the highest-rated in Castelrotto.
