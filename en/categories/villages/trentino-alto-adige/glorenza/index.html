Gemini

For a tiny, beautifully preserved medieval walled town like Glorenza (also known as Glurns), staying right within or just outside the historic city walls offers an incredibly charming experience. Because of the town's small size, the best options are character-filled boutique hotels, historic residences, and family-run alpine B&Bs.

Here are the top-rated places to stay in and around Glorenza:

Boutique & Historic Stays

flurin - Bar, Restaurant & Suites

  • The Vibe: A stunningly restored historic property where old stone walls meet modern alpine luxury.
  • Why Choose It: It's a true hidden gem featuring beautifully designed suites and a highly-regarded, passionate restaurant and bar onsite. Perfect for food lovers and couples looking for a romantic ambiance.

Belvenu Boutique Hotel

  • The Vibe: A Gothic-era residence transformed into a chic, modern boutique hotel right on the main town square.
  • Why Choose It: It effortlessly blends historic architecture with sleek modern comforts. It also features a fantastic wellness and spa area on the top floor boasting scenic views of the village rooftops and the surrounding mountains.

Traditional & Cozy Alpine Hotels

Garni Glurnserhof

  • The Vibe: An unpretentious, highly-rated family-run 4-star B&B surrounded by countryside views.
  • Why Choose It: Located just a few hundred feet from the historic center, it is highly practical if you are traveling by bike, sitting a mere 50 yards from the premier Val Venosta cycling path. Guests love the warm hospitality and excellent free breakfast.

Hotel Post

  • The Vibe: A laid-back, low-key traditional South Tyrolean hotel.
  • Why Choose It: This is a solid choice for travelers who want reliable, comfortable amenities without a hefty price tag. It features an onsite restaurant, a bar, a peaceful garden, a sauna, and a hot tub to unwind in after hiking.

Gasthof Grüner Baum

  • The Vibe: A crenelated historic building located directly on the central town square (Piazza della Città).
  • Why Choose It: It features a wood-paneled restaurant serving highly rated local cuisine and boasts an impressive modern rooftop spa complete with a large panoramic sauna and an outdoor jacuzzi.

Grok · grok-3

Glorenza (Glurns), a small medieval walled town in South Tyrol’s Vinschgau/Val Venosta valley near Stelvio National Park, has a limited number of hotels. The top-rated options are mostly boutique/historic properties right in or very near the center. Ratings come primarily from Booking.com and similar sites (guest scores out of 10) and TripAdvisor.

Nearby highly rated options (Mals/Malles Venosta area, ~2–5 km)

These frequently appear in “Glorenza hotels” searches due to the town’s small size:

  • Hotel Garberhof — Often 9.5+, strong on wellness/pool and cuisine.
  • Das Gerstl Alpine Retreat — Around 9.6, more upscale/luxury alpine feel with pools.
  • Hotel Edelweiss or Hotel Tyrol — Consistently high 9+ scores, good value with parking and WiFi.

Quick tips

  • Glorenza is compact and walkable; staying inside the walls puts you in the heart of the historic center.
  • Many properties emphasize South Tyrolean hospitality, mountain views, and proximity to hiking, cycling, and the Stelvio area.
  • Prices vary by season (higher in summer and peak hiking periods); check current availability and exact rates on Booking.com, Kayak, or the hotel sites, as they fluctuate.
  • For the absolute latest reviews and photos, Booking.com and TripAdvisor are the most reliable sources for this small destination.
