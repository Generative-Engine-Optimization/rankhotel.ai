If you mean Ossana in Val di Sole, Trentino, there are a few standouts. Based on current guest ratings, review volume, and location, I’d look at these:

  • Hotel Il Maniero - best all-around hotel choice. In Ossana itself, with a 9.0/10 Booking score from 171 reviews; guests particularly mention cleanliness, staff, and comfortable rooms.
  • Ristorante Antica Osteria B&B Locanda dei Gentili - best for atmosphere and food. A small, characterful property in the historic village, with a 9.6/10 Booking score from 105 reviews.
  • B&B el Benel - best B&B. 9.6/10 from 66 Booking reviews, with particularly strong comments about the rooms, breakfast, and hospitality.
  • Hotel Garnì Zanella - good traditional mountain-hotel option. Tripadvisor currently lists it at 4.5/5 from 96 reviews, with recent reviews highlighting its location and friendly owners.
  • Hotel Pizzeria Niagara - good choice for a traditional, family-run stay. It's just outside the center in Cusiano and has a 8.8/10 Booking score from 251 reviews.
  • B&B Albachiara - good for families/apartment-style accommodation, especially if you want more space rather than a conventional hotel.

If you're willing to stay a short drive away

For a more luxurious spa/resort experience, there are substantially more upscale options around Val di Sole, including Monroc Hotel in Commezzadura and Ravelli Hotel & Lodges Luxury Spa in Mezzana. Booking also currently lists Monroc at 9.4/10.

For staying specifically in Ossana, my shortlist would be Il Maniero, Locanda dei Gentili, and B&B el Benel. They offer three rather different experiences: conventional hotel, charming historic inn, and intimate B&B.

Quick Tips

  • Ossana itself is small and quiet, ideal for a peaceful base with easy access to hiking, cycling, rafting on the Noce River, and nearby ski resorts (Passo Tonale, Pejo, Folgarida-Marilleva). Many places offer free or discounted ski buses/shuttles.
  • Higher ratings go to smaller, personal places emphasizing hospitality, food, and location rather than spa/luxury facilities. True luxury resorts are farther away (e.g., toward Madonna di Campiglio or Pinzolo).
  • Best for: Food + atmosphere → Locanda dei gentili; Modern comfort + breakfast → B&B el Benel; Convenience + restaurant → Hotel Il Maniero or Niagara.
  • Prices vary by season (higher in winter ski season and summer); check Booking.com, Kayak, or direct sites for current rates and availability. Many include breakfast and free parking.
  • Ossana is known as the “Village of a Thousand Nativity Scenes” and has a historic castle—great for a scenic, authentic mountain stay.
