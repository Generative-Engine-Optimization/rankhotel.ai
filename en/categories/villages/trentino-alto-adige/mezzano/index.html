Gemini

The best hotels in Mezzano, Italy (and the surrounding Primiero Valley) range from homey, family-run boutique properties directly inside the historic village to expansive spa resorts just minutes away in Fiera di Primiero and Transacqua.

Because Mezzano is a remarkably charming, historic alpine village known for its artistic woodpiles, standard large-scale hotels are limited right within the village borders, but world-class alpine resorts sit just a 5-minute drive away.

Top Choices Right inside Mezzano

Hotel Garnì Paradisi: My top pick for an authentic village stay. This highly rated 4-star family-run hotel balances a homey feel with upscale amenities. It features cozy rooms and suites, a relaxing spa area, an indoor pool, and includes a highly rated complimentary breakfast.

Alpenrooms B&B and Apartments: A fantastic modern alternative if you prefer the flexibility of an apartment setup or a quiet bed-and-breakfast vibe. It is highly rated for its cleanliness, sleek alpine style, and great location for exploring the valley.

Hotel Salgetti: A casual 3-star hotel with unpretentious rooms, an on-site restaurant, a garden, and its own spa corner. A fun local perk is the popular on-site ice cream parlor.

Luxury & Wellness Resorts (Within 5-10 Minutes)

If you are looking for extensive "Sky Spas," fine dining, and full-resort amenities, you will find excellent options just up the road in neighboring Fiera di Primiero and Transacqua:

Grok · grok-3

Mezzano (Mezzano di Primiero) in Trentino-Alto Adige, Italy—a picturesque village in the Primiero Valley near the Dolomites and San Martino di Castrozza (one of Italy’s “most beautiful villages”)—has a limited but high-quality selection of hotels and B&Bs, with strong nearby options in Fiera di Primiero, Transacqua, and Tonadico (just a few kilometers away).

Top picks in or very near Mezzano

Hotel Garnì Paradisi – Wellness & Spa (in Mezzano) stands out as one of the strongest overall choices.
It consistently ranks highly (around 9.2 on Kayak from 100+ reviews; 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor; up to 9.7 on Booking.com in some snapshots). Guests praise the modern/spacious rooms (many with balconies and mountain or garden views), excellent breakfast, family-run hospitality, cleanliness, and the Edonè Spa (whirlpool, sauna, bio-sauna, Turkish bath, emotional showers). It’s well-located for exploring the village and nearby trails/ski areas.

Lozen Ristorante B&B / Affittacamere Lozen (Agritur Malga Lozen area) (in the Valle del Vanoi near Mezzano) ranks at or near the top on Tripadvisor (about 4.6/5 from 100+ reviews). It is valued for its peaceful, green setting, strong local cuisine (restaurant is a highlight), warm hospitality, and suitability for families or pet owners. It feels more intimate and rural than a full-service hotel.

Other solid local options include smaller places and apartments such as Casa Jasmina, Casa Lucia, and Alpenrooms, which often score very high (9+ /10) for cleanliness, space, and location when available, though with fewer reviews.

Strong nearby hotels (highly recommended and frequently listed with Mezzano)

These are in Fiera di Primiero, Transacqua, or Tonadico (easy short drive or walkable in some cases) and appear prominently in “best of Mezzano” searches:

  • Hotel Luis (Fiera di Primiero) — ~9.3–9.4 ratings; pool, parking, pet-friendly.
  • Brunet – The Dolomites Resort (Tonadico/Fiera area) — ~9.3–9.4; wellness facilities, local cuisine, strong overall reviews.
  • Hotel Castel Pietra (Transacqua) — ~9.0–9.1 from a large number of reviews; quiet location, parking, Wi-Fi.
  • Hotel Isolabella Wellness (Transacqua) — solid wellness-focused option with pool.
  • Hotel Mirabello – Slow Hotel Benessere and others like Hotel Relais Orsingher also receive frequent positive mentions.

Quick tips

  • Mezzano itself is compact and charming (historic center, traditional architecture, “cataste” wood piles, cheese-related identity). Many visitors base themselves here or in the immediate Primiero area for hiking, skiing (San Martino di Castrozza/Passo Rolle nearby), mountain biking, and nature.
  • Options range from intimate B&Bs/agriturismi to wellness hotels; pure large luxury resorts are more common a bit farther into the ski areas.
  • Ratings and availability fluctuate by season (summer and winter peaks are busiest). Check recent guest reviews on Booking, Tripadvisor, or Kayak for current details, and book early for peak periods.
  • Prices vary widely by dates, room type, and board basis (many include breakfast; some offer half-board).
