For a stay in the charming alpine town of Chiusa (also known as Klausen), you have several excellent highly-rated choices, ranging from historic properties in the old town center to wellness retreats tucked into the surrounding hillsides.

✨ Top Picks for Luxury & Wellness

Hotel Goldener Adler : This refined 4-star hotel is situated right in the historical heart of town. It is highly regarded for its excellent hospitality, top-tier wellness center featuring a traditional sauna and hammam, and an outstanding breakfast buffet.

  • Best for: Travelers seeking understated luxury, central convenience, and deep relaxation.
  • Key Amenities: Free breakfast, spa/wellness center, pet-friendly, on-site restaurant.

Hotel Gnollhof : Tucked away at an altitude of 1,160 meters in nearby Gudon, this upscale 4-star mountain hotel is a true alpine escape. It features a spectacular heated infinity pool with 360-degree panoramic views of the Dolomites and its own private vineyard.

  • Best for: Nature lovers, hikers, and anyone looking for a secluded, quiet-luxury mountain retreat.
  • Key Amenities: Infinity pool, mountain panorama sauna, tennis courts, inclusive lunch buffet.

🏨 Traditional & Charming Alpine Hotels

Hotel Walther von der Vogelweide : A relaxed, authentic 3-star alpine hotel right in the village center. It is celebrated for its historic charm and famous on-site restaurant serving highly rated local Tyrolean and Italian dishes.

  • Best for: Foodies and families wanting direct access to the town's small shops and playgrounds.
  • Key Amenities: Free breakfast, garden, terrace restaurant, private parking garage.

Hotel Der Rierhof : This warm, family-friendly hotel offers a cozy, home-like atmosphere with plenty of amenities. Guests consistently highlight the friendly staff, clean, spacious rooms, and an impressive old-world wine cellar.

  • Best for: Families and active vacationers who want good leisure facilities.
  • Key Amenities: Indoor and outdoor pools, spa/hot tub, restaurant, free parking.

Hotel Bischofhof - Bistro Jasmin : A sleek, beautifully maintained family-run boutique option set in lovely grounds. It stands out for its high attention to detail, exceptional location, and a stellar breakfast that travelers note as a regional highlight.

  • Best for: Couples looking for a chic, intimate base to explore Northern Italy.
  • Key Amenities: Outdoor pool, hot tub, spa area, upscale bistro restaurant.

Hotel Schmuckhof : Centrally located right by the path leading up to the Säben Abbey, this casual property blends modern comfort with a lively local vibe. It boasts a brand-new panorama pool overlooking the town.

  • Best for: Casual travelers who appreciate great hearty food and central positioning.
  • Key Amenities: New panoramic outdoor pool, on-site pizzeria and pub, spa.

Grok · grok-3

Chiusa (Klausen in German), a charming medieval town in South Tyrol’s Eisack Valley, serves as a great base for exploring the Dolomites, Val Gardena, Alpe di Siusi, and nearby areas like Brixen. Hotels here are typically family-run 3- or 4-star properties emphasizing local hospitality, regional cuisine, and access to hiking/skiing.

Here are some of the consistently best-rated options based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites (ratings can fluctuate slightly by platform and date):

Top Picks

  • Cityhotel Goldener Adler B&B
    Frequently among the highest-rated (around 4.8/5 on Tripadvisor with 230+ reviews; ~9.4/10 elsewhere). Modern rooms, strong wellness facilities (sauna/spa), excellent breakfast, friendly staff, free parking/Wi-Fi, and a convenient location near the center and highway (handy for Dolomites day trips, ~25 minutes to some ski areas). Guests often praise the value and service.

  • Hotel Walther v.d. Vogelweide Superior (Walther Von Der Vogelweide)
    A standout historic option in a medieval building in the old town center, overlooking the Isarco River. Strong ratings (typically ~9.2/10 with a high volume of reviews, often 1,000+). Alpine-style rooms, well-regarded restaurant with vaulted ceilings, and excellent breakfast featuring local products. Atmospheric and central.

  • Hotel Bischofhof - Bistro Jasmin
    Often ranks high for best value on Tripadvisor. Features an outdoor pool, spa elements (e.g., jacuzzi/sauna), wood-beamed rooms, and a great location steps from the village. Family-run with positive feedback on location for Dolomites access, food, and overall stay.

  • Ansitz Fonteklaus
    Exceptional ratings (around 9.6/10 on Booking with 100+ reviews; strong Tripadvisor scores). Historic “Ansitz” (manor-style) property with panoramic mountain views, seasonal outdoor/natural pool, garden, excellent regional cuisine, and a warm family atmosphere. Quieter hillside location a short drive from the town center (car recommended). Ideal for relaxation and hiking.

  • Weingut & Genusshotel Spitalerhof Superior
    A winery-hotel hybrid (~9.0–9.1 ratings across platforms). Own wines/schnapps, strong restaurant with regional and creative dishes, modern comfortable rooms (some with balconies), outdoor pool, and garden. Popular with food and wine lovers; convenient access near the motorway exit while still offering a relaxed setting.

Other Noteworthy Options

  • Hotel Schmuckhof — Solid ratings, convenient location, good restaurant/breakfast.
  • Parkhotel Post — Central historic option with garden/pool elements.
  • Hotel Gnollhof or similar nearby properties — Often praised for views, spa facilities, and quiet settings (some slightly outside the immediate center).

Tips

Most properties emphasize South Tyrolean hospitality, regional food (half-board is common and often excellent), and outdoor access. Many include or are near parking. Prices typically start around €150–250+ per night (double, depending on season, room type, and board basis) and rise in peak summer/winter. Check current availability and reviews on Tripadvisor or Booking.com, as small family hotels can fill up and offerings (e.g., pools) are often seasonal.

Chiusa itself is walkable and scenic (Säben Abbey, old town), with easy drives to major attractions. A car is useful for flexibility, especially for more elevated or countryside properties.
