Quick Tips

  • Location preference: Stay in or near the historic center (e.g., Palazzo Illuminati or Casa Agello) for walkability to the narrow streets, views, and sights. Opt for Colle Serrano or similar for a quieter hillside/spa experience (still convenient to the Adriatic beaches, ~10–15 minutes drive).
  • Seasonality & booking: Peak summer fills up; prices vary by dates. Check current availability and reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as smaller properties have limited rooms.
