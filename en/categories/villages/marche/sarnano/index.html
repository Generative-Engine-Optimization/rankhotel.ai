Sarnano (in Italy’s Marche region, near the Sibillini Mountains) is a small medieval town known for its historic center, thermal spas, and outdoor activities like hiking and skiing. Accommodation options are limited and lean toward boutique, agriturismo (farm-stay), and small hotels rather than large luxury chains.
