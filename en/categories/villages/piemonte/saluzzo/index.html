Hotel San Giovanni Resort, Hotel Boutique Antiche Mura, Hotel & Apartments “Perpoin,” Antico Podere Propano, and Agriturismo Agli ulivi rank among the strongest options in Saluzzo (Piedmont, Italy), based on consistent high guest ratings across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Kayak, and similar sites.

Saluzzo is a compact historic hill town with a well-preserved medieval center, so most top properties are walkable to sights like the Castiglia, churches, and restaurants. Options range from converted historic buildings to more rural agriturismi. Ratings and details can shift with new reviews, so check current availability and prices for your dates.

Top Picks

Hotel San Giovanni Resort (4-star)
A converted 15th-century convent/monastery in the historic center with a picturesque courtyard/cloister. Elegant rooms (some with large windows, views over the town/Langhe hills or nearby museums, and modern bathrooms), free Wi-Fi, and a restaurant serving Piedmontese specialties in the former cellars.

  • Ratings: Around 9.1/10 on Booking (700+ reviews) and 4.6/5 on TripAdvisor. Guests frequently praise the atmospheric setting, helpful staff, cleanliness, breakfast (often in the courtyard), and quiet location.
  • Notes: Free parking available (but the area is a restricted traffic zone/ZTL—request a pass). Good for couples or those wanting historic charm.

Hotel Boutique Antiche Mura (4-star)
Boutique hotel in a restored historic (roughly 14th–15th-century) building, centrally located. Features a garden, terrace, restaurant, bar, room service, and air-conditioned rooms with modern amenities.

  • Ratings: Typically 9.2–9.3/10 on Booking and around 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor. Reviewers highlight the charming atmosphere, cleanliness, location, staff, and food.
  • Strong for those wanting a polished boutique stay with on-site dining.

Hotel & Apartments “Perpoin”
Centrally located, recently renovated property offering hotel rooms and apartments. Free Wi-Fi and parking; often noted as pet-friendly with a warm, family-run feel.

  • Ratings: Around 9.0–9.3/10 with hundreds of reviews. Guests like the convenient location (near sights, restaurants, and shops), breakfast, hospitality, and value.
  • Practical mid-range choice for exploring on foot.

Antico Podere Propano
Charming property with a more rural/authentic feel, free private parking, mountain views in some rooms, a bar, and solid breakfast.

  • Ratings: Around 9.1/10. Praised for atmosphere, helpful staff, space, and location (a short distance from the center).
  • Suits travelers wanting character and easier parking.

Agriturismo Agli ulivi
Farm-stay style with a garden, bar, terrace, and free private parking. Convenient for nearby castles (Castello della Manta).

  • Ratings: Often ~9.4/10. Guests appreciate the comfort, setting, and quiet.
  • Good alternative if you prefer a greener, more countryside vibe while still near town.

Other Highly Rated Options

Smaller apartments and B&Bs frequently score exceptionally high (9.7–9.9) with fewer reviews, such as Casa Piazzetta, La Casa dei Nuovi Mondagli, and similar self-catering or boutique stays. These can offer excellent value and privacy if you prefer independence over full hotel services.
Nearby Castello Rosso (in Costigliole Saluzzo) is sometimes listed for its castle setting and pool, though ratings are a bit more mixed.

Practical Tips

  • Location: Prioritize the historic center for walkability. Properties in/near the upper town often have more atmosphere but may involve ZTL rules for cars.
  • Prices: Expect roughly €100–200+ per night for two (varies by season, room type, and demand; higher in peak periods).
  • Amenities: Many include free Wi-Fi, breakfast, and parking. Confirm restaurant hours, pet policies, or accessibility if needed.
  • Getting there: Closest major airport is Cuneo-Levaldigi; the town is reachable by car or bus/train connections in the region.
  • Book via major platforms (Booking, etc.) for current reviews, photos, and flexible cancellation. Saluzzo’s small size means popular places fill up, especially weekends or during local events.

For the absolute latest ratings or specific needs (e.g., pool, spa, family rooms, or budget), search with your exact dates on Booking.com or TripAdvisor. Enjoy Saluzzo—it’s a charming base for exploring Piedmont’s historic towns, food, and nearby Alps/Langhe area.
