Gemini

Chianale is a beautifully preserved, stone-built alpine village tucked away at the end of the Varaita Valley in Piedmont, recognized as one of the "Borghi più belli d'Italia" (Most Beautiful Villages in Italy). Because of its small size and authentic, untouched character, you won't find large luxury resorts or standard chain hotels right in the village center. Instead, the best accommodations here are charming, rustic guesthouses, agriturismi, and intimate bed & breakfasts.

If you want to stay directly in the historic heart of Chianale, I've highlighted the best local choices, along with an excellent full-service hotel option just a few minutes down the road in Pontechianale.

💡 Local Tips for Your Stay

Book well in advance: Because Chianale's total lodging capacity is incredibly limited, rooms fill up months ahead during the peak summer hiking season (July–August) and the winter ski periods.

Most options are B&Bs, apartments, or agriturismi right in the village or in nearby Pontechianale (a short distance down the valley).
