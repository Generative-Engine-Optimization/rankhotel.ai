Viggianello (Basilicata, Italy, in/near Pollino National Park) has a limited selection of hotels and B&Bs, mostly small, family-run properties emphasizing nature, views, local cuisine, and hospitality rather than large luxury resorts.

Here are some of the best-rated options based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Kayak, and similar sites (ratings can fluctuate; check current availability and prices, which often start around €70–120+ per night depending on season and room type):

Top Standouts

  • Il Castello dei Principi Sanseverino
    Historic 14th-century castle converted into a hotel/B&B with mountain and valley views, garden, terrace, restaurant, and amenities like a hot tub in some reports. Guests frequently praise the unique atmosphere, kind/professional staff, cleanliness, spacious rooms, and quality local food (breakfast and dinners). Ratings typically around 4.7/5 on Tripadvisor (nearly 200 reviews) and 8.9–9.2/10 on Booking. Narrow access roads can be a minor challenge. Ideal for a special or romantic stay.

  • Happy Family B&B
    Highly rated for warm hospitality, clean/comfortable rooms (some with balconies or garden views), excellent breakfast with local products, garden/terrace, free parking, and family-friendly vibe. Often scores 9.4/10 on Booking (200+ reviews) and around 4.7–4.8/5 on Tripadvisor. Guests highlight the owners’ attentiveness.

  • La Locanda di San Francesco
    Comfortable hotel with mountain views, restaurant serving Lucanian/Pollino cuisine, and a welcoming atmosphere. Strong reviews for rooms, food, and location near the park (Tripadvisor often around 4.7/5 with hundreds of reviews). Good option if you want on-site dining.

Other Strong Contenders

  • L’Antica Fontana Casa Vacanze B&B — Frequently near-perfect scores (5.0 on Tripadvisor with smaller sample; ~9.6 on Booking). Praised for mountain views, garden, and overall quality.
  • Agriturismo Fiore — Popular for authentic rural experience, home-cooked meals with local ingredients, and peaceful setting amid nature. Solid mid-to-high ratings.
  • B&B Le Cinque Cime, Hotel Il Boschetto, Pollino Flower Park, and La casa di Tiziana — Consistently well-reviewed for cleanliness, hospitality, and location (many score 9+ on Booking or equivalent).

Tips

  • Most properties are small B&Bs or agriturismi rather than full-service hotels; expect personalized service and regional food.
  • The area is ideal for hiking and nature in Pollino National Park—many stays emphasize outdoor access.
  • Nearby towns like Rotonda or San Severino Lucano offer additional options if Viggianello itself is fully booked.
  • Always verify current ratings, photos, and cancellation policies on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the property’s site, as small places can change.

Note: There is also a Viggianello in Corsica (France, near Propriano). If that’s the one you meant, let me know for different recommendations. For the Italian location, the above are the strongest based on traveler feedback.
