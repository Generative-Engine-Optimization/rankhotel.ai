Grok · grok-3

The top-rated options in Melfi (Basilicata, Italy) are mostly high-scoring B&Bs, guesthouses, and a couple of small hotels/resorts rather than large luxury chains, reflecting the town’s size and focus on historic/Norman heritage (especially the Castle of Melfi) plus the surrounding Vulture area.

Here’s a summary of the strongest performers based on recent guest ratings from Booking.com, Kayak, Tripadvisor, and similar sites (ratings typically out of 10; numbers of reviews vary and can change):

Standout Highly Rated Options

  • Lo Stemma Guest House — Frequently the highest-rated (around 9.9 with 70–115+ reviews). Modern, fully renovated apartment in a historic building right in the old town (a short walk to the Castle of Melfi and the archaeological museum). Guests praise the comfort, cleanliness, mountain/city views, kitchenette, free Wi-Fi/parking options, and especially the welcoming host. Ideal for a central, home-like stay.

  • Casa Isabela Luxury Rooms — Strong 9.5 (often 160–300+ reviews). Clean, modern rooms (some with balconies, mountain or garden views), free parking, free Wi-Fi, shared kitchen/breakfast options, and a convenient location near the center (roughly a 10–15 minute walk to the castle). Frequently mentioned for helpful owners/staff and good value.

  • Il Casale della Gioia — Around 9.4–9.6 (hundreds of reviews across platforms; often tops Tripadvisor traveler rankings for Melfi). Peaceful countryside B&B with garden, terrace, mountain views, and homemade breakfast (including cakes and local produce). Family-friendly and quiet; a short drive from the town center. Guests highlight the hospitality, cleanliness, and relaxing setting.

  • Il Castagneto Hotel — Solid 9.3 (hundreds of reviews) 4-star property with a seasonal outdoor pool, restaurant, fitness center, garden, and free parking. Set a few kilometers outside Melfi (scenic area with Mount Vulture views). Good for those wanting hotel amenities and relaxation rather than a pure historic-center base; dinner and breakfast often praised.

Other Notable Mentions

  • Masseria Spinale Wine Resort — Attractive option with vineyard/pool setting, modern rooms, and good ratings (around 9); suits those interested in wine and a resort feel a bit outside town.
  • Smaller/highly rated spots that appear frequently include Nonna Ninetta, I Gelsomini, Relais La Fattoria (pool and grounds), and various central apartments/houses like Re RUGGIERO HOUSE or San Lorenzo Casa Vacanze (very high scores but fewer reviews).

Quick Tips

Melfi’s accommodation scene is compact—many places are independent B&Bs or apartments with strong personal service. Prioritize based on your priorities:

  • Historic center + walkability → Lo Stemma or Casa Isabela.
  • Quiet/garden/pool → Il Casale della Gioia or Il Castagneto.
  • Wine/nature → Masseria Spinale or similar countryside properties.

Prices typically start from roughly €70–120+ per night depending on season, room type, and dates (check current rates). Free parking and Wi-Fi are common. For the most up-to-date reviews, photos, and availability, compare on Booking.com, Kayak, or Tripadvisor, as guest feedback and offerings can shift.
