Which One to Choose?

Choose Hotel Palazzo Krataiis if you love the beach. Located right across the road from Scilla's main stretch of sand, this converted 18th-century palace is ideal if you plan to spend your days swimming. Locals and past guests highly praise its beachside restaurant, making it an incredibly convenient "park and relax" option.

Choose Il Casato Deluxe Rooms for a romantic, historic vibe. This hotel sits inside Chianalea, where buildings are constructed directly on the water. Dining at their seasonal overwater terrace while watching the waves crash beneath the Scilla castle is an unforgettable experience.

Choose B&B La Veduta for the ultimate view. Nestled slightly higher up, every single room here features a private balcony with panoramic ocean views. It is roughly a 10-minute walk to the beach, but the peaceful cliffside location and the sound of the waves right below your window make it a top pick for relaxation.

Grok · grok-3

The top-rated hotels and B&Bs in Scilla (Calabria, Italy) are mostly charming, smaller properties in or near the historic Chianalea fishing village (often called the “Little Venice of the South”), with strong emphasis on sea views, location, hospitality, and cleanliness. Scilla itself is a compact seaside town, so many options put you steps from beaches, restaurants, and the Ruffo Castle.

Here are some of the consistently highest-rated options based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Trip.com, Tripadvisor, and similar sites (ratings and review counts approximate and can fluctuate):

Top Picks

  • Il Casato Deluxe Rooms (Chianalea area)
    Frequently ranks among the very best, with scores around 9.2–9.4 (hundreds of reviews). Guests praise the excellent sea-view rooms/balconies overlooking the water, modern/clean interiors, helpful staff, strong restaurant (fresh seafood), and central location in the pedestrian historic village. Breakfast is often highlighted. Parking can require arrangement (common in Chianalea). Ideal for couples seeking atmosphere and views.

  • B&B La Veduta (Chianalea, beachfront)
    Exceptionally high ratings (~9.7–9.9, 200+ reviews). Beachfront setting with large balconies and outstanding sea views (sometimes to the Aeolian Islands). Warm, family-run hospitality (hosts often praised by name), delicious homemade breakfasts, clean/spacious rooms, and a relaxing vibe. Highly recommended for its location and personal service.

  • Signora Tita B&B Chianalea - Scilla
    Scores around 9.6 (200+ reviews). Located right in the heart of Chianalea near the docks, restaurants, and shops. Clean, modern rooms (some with sea-view terraces—possible Stromboli views), attentive host (Mimmo often mentioned), energy-efficient setup, and great atmosphere. Stairs only (no elevator); breakfast sometimes at a nearby café. Excellent for immersion in the historic quarter.

  • La Balza di Scilla (near Marina Grande beach)
    Ratings around 9.0–9.2 (200+ reviews). Modern renovated spaces with rooftop terrace, common areas, and prime beachfront location (steps from the sand and restaurants). Friendly staff, some kitchen facilities, and good for families or longer stays. A few notes on room size or stairs in older reviews.

Other Strong Options

  • Hotel Scilla — Classic family-run hotel in the upper/center town (not right on the beach). Solid ~8.4–8.8 ratings with many reviews. Friendly staff, free parking, homemade breakfast (often on a terrace with views), clean rooms (some with sea views), and convenient for the train station or driving. A 10-minute walk or short shuttle to the beach/Chianalea. Good value and reliable base.
  • Casa Vela B&B — High praise for hospitality, sea-view suites, and the on-site wine bar/café (excellent pastries and aperitivi). Ratings often in the mid-to-high 8s–9s.
  • Il Borgo — Frequently high scores (~9.7 in some listings) for sea proximity, owner hospitality, and the sound of the waves.
  • Others worth checking: Hotel U’ Bais (private beach access), Hotel Palazzo Krataiis (beachfront 4-star feel), and various smaller B&Bs like B&B Sunshine or Scilla Resort for high guest satisfaction in niche categories.

Quick Tips for Booking in Scilla

  • Chianalea is the most atmospheric area (narrow streets, houses over the water, excellent seafood) but is largely pedestrian—expect limited/paid parking and possible stairs.
  • Marina Grande beach and the upper town offer easier access and parking.
  • Peak season (summer) fills quickly and prices rise; shoulder seasons (spring/fall) are excellent for milder weather and better rates.
  • Many places are small B&Bs or boutique spots rather than large resorts—book early for sea-view rooms.
  • Check current availability and exact ratings on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or similar, as they update frequently. Look for recent reviews mentioning parking, stairs/elevators, and breakfast specifics.

Scilla is known for its dramatic setting on the Strait of Messina, clear waters, and authentic Calabrian feel rather than large luxury resorts. The highest-rated stays excel at location, views, and personal hospitality. Enjoy your visit!
