Other solid mentions

  • I Due Scogli — Beachfront guesthouse with free Wi-Fi (good scores around 8.6–8.7).
  • IL Borgo Del Benessere, La Poesia, Case Vacanze La Venere, and Raggio di Sole — Smaller B&Bs/apartments with high scores (often 8.5–9.5+) but fewer reviews.
  • Tenuta Rara Avis — Well-rated countryside option with parking and modern rooms.

Nearby alternatives (if you want more resort-style)

Nova Siri Marina (a short drive) has larger properties such as Club Giardini d’Oriente Village, Best Western Hotel Imperiale, and Eco Resort dei Siriti, which offer pools and more amenities.

Tips

  • The historic center (around the castle) is atmospheric but involves steep/narrow streets—Casa Castello is ideal if you want to stay right in it.
  • Villa Sveva and similar countryside spots are quieter with better views and parking.
  • Book ahead in summer (peak season); prices are generally moderate (often €60–150+/night depending on dates and type).
  • Check current availability and exact reviews on Booking.com or TripAdvisor, as scores can shift slightly.

Rocca Imperiale itself is one of Italy’s “Borghi più belli,” so staying in or very near the village maximizes the charming medieval atmosphere and views over the Ionian coast.
