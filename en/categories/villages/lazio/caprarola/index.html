Caprarola (Lazio, near Lake Vico and the famous Palazzo Farnese) is a small historic town, so accommodation options are limited and lean toward agriturismi (farm stays), B&Bs, and guesthouses rather than large hotels. The strongest-reviewed places combine proximity to the Palazzo Farnese, Lake Vico views or access, quiet settings, and strong hospitality.

Other solid options

  • Cottage Beach Vico — Positive feedback for the lakeside/resort-like feel and hosts (smaller review volume).
  • B&B Buongiorno Caprarola, Casa Checca e Casa Maria, La Casina, or similar apartments/holiday homes — Often score very high (9.5–9.8 range) for cleanliness, location, and value; good if you prefer self-catering.

Practical notes

  • Location trade-offs: Stay in the historic center (e.g., La Rocca or Il Vicolo) for easy walking to Palazzo Farnese. Choose lake-area places (La Valle Di Vico or La Bella Venere) for views, nature, swimming, and a more relaxed vibe (short drive to the palace).
  • Caprarola itself has few full-service hotels. For more amenities, pools, spas, or luxury, look a short drive away in Ronciglione or Viterbo.
  • Prices typically start in the €80–150+ range per night (varies by season, room type, and breakfast inclusion). Many places offer free parking and Wi-Fi.
  • The area is popular for day trips from Rome (~1 hour) and for exploring Lake Vico, Etruscan sites, and nearby towns.
