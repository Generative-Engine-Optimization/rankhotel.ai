The best hotels in Frascati (Castelli Romani, near Rome) consistently include a few standouts based on ratings and reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and similar sites.

Frascati is a charming hill town known for its wine, historic villas, and as a quieter base for visiting Rome (about 20–30 minutes by train). Options range from historic villas with panoramic views to central family-run hotels and a wine resort.

Top Recommendations

Villa Tuscolana (4-star)
A 16th-century historic villa on a hill above town, with elegant grounds, frescoes, gardens, a restaurant/bar, free private parking, and (small) pool. Many rooms offer views toward Rome.

  • Ratings: Typically 8.3–8.4/10 (Booking) or 4.0/5 (Tripadvisor, 1,000+ reviews).
  • Highlights: Atmosphere, panoramic views, staff, and setting for a peaceful stay.
  • Drawbacks: Some rooms feel dated; Wi-Fi can be inconsistent; best with a car or prepared for an uphill walk from the center/station.
    Ideal for those wanting historic charm and scenery.

Hotel & Spa Villa Mercede (4-star)
Features a spa, outdoor pool (often with views), restaurant, bar, and modern-ish facilities in a resort-like setting on the slopes.

  • Ratings: Around 8.3–8.6/10 or 4.2/5 (Tripadvisor, ~900–1,400 reviews).
  • Highlights: Pool, spa, food, and friendly staff; good for relaxation.
  • Drawbacks: Some guests note it can feel busy (part of a larger complex), occasional maintenance or breakfast variety issues, and it’s a short distance outside the very center.
    A strong choice if you want amenities like a pool and spa.

Hotel Cacciani (3-star)
Family-run since 1922 in the heart of Frascati (very near the train station and main square). Many renovated rooms have balconies with views of Rome/Frascati; excellent on-site restaurant specializing in traditional Roman cuisine. Breakfast often served on a panoramic terrace in season.

  • Ratings: Around 8.3–8.4/10.
  • Highlights: Central walkable location, food quality, cleanliness, and value. Guests frequently praise the restaurant and hospitality.
  • Drawbacks: Limited parking (street or paid nearby); more traditional than luxury.
    Excellent for exploring Frascati on foot, easy Rome day trips by train, and food-focused stays.

Merumalia Wine Resort
A smaller, boutique organic wine estate/resort with rooms in a renovated farmhouse amid vineyards and olive groves. Focus on wine tastings, local produce, and countryside calm (with views).

  • Ratings: Often 9.4/10 range on Booking-style sites.
  • Highlights: Peaceful setting, wine experiences, family-run hospitality, and high guest satisfaction for an authentic Castelli Romani feel.
  • Best for wine lovers seeking something more intimate and rural rather than a full-service hotel.

Other Solid Options

  • Hotel Colonna — Central historic building (overlooking a square); strong ratings (often ~4.4/5) and convenient location.
  • Hotel Roma Sud — Practical, good value, free parking, solid ratings (~8.4); convenient for drivers or airport access (near highway).
  • Green Hotel Poggio Regillo or Hotel Flora — Frequently mentioned with good reviews for comfort and location.

Quick Tips

  • Prices generally start from roughly €80–150+ per night (varies widely by season, room type, and dates—check current rates).
  • Location preference: Stay central (Cacciani, Colonna) for walking around town and easy train access to Rome. Choose hillside villas (Tuscolana, Mercede) for views and quieter atmosphere (car helpful).
  • Book with flexibility if possible, as Frascati is popular for weekend escapes from Rome and wine tourism.
  • Many properties offer free or paid parking, breakfast, and views—filter by your priorities (pool/spa, restaurant, historic feel, or walkability).

For the most up-to-date availability, prices, and photos, check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the hotels’ official sites directly, as ratings and conditions can shift. Enjoy Frascati’s wine, views, and relaxed pace!
