Greccio is a tiny, beautiful medieval hill town in Lazio (famous as the site of St. Francis of Assisi’s first live nativity scene in 1223), so it has limited hotel options—mostly small hotels, B&Bs, and agriturismi rather than large resorts.

Dimora della Fonte stands out as the main “proper” hotel in the historic center. Guests consistently praise the restored stone building, location (easy walking to restaurants and the square), and staff. It’s not ultra-luxury, but charming and well-kept.

B&B Il Cantico frequently earns raves for the warm welcome, garden setting, and proximity to the Santuario Francescano del Presepe.

Nearby alternatives (worth considering)

If you want more facilities, a bigger property, or panoramic views:

  • Albergo Diffuso Crispolti (Labro, ~6–10 km / short drive) — Highly rated (often 9+), scattered rooms in a medieval hill village with Lake Piediluco views.
  • Hotels in Rieti (about 15–20 minutes by car): Park Hotel Villa Potenziani (elegant, well-reviewed 4-star), Grande Albergo Quattro Stagioni, or Hotel Cavour. These offer more amenities (pools, larger restaurants, etc.).

Quick tips

  • Greccio is very compact and walkable once you’re there, but a car is useful for exploring the surrounding Valle Santa (other Franciscan sanctuaries), Cascata delle Marmore, and Lake Piediluco.
  • Book ahead if visiting around Christmas/Nativity season or weekends—the village is popular with pilgrims and day-trippers.
  • Most places emphasize Italian hospitality, homemade breakfasts, and peaceful mountain settings rather than spa/luxury facilities.
