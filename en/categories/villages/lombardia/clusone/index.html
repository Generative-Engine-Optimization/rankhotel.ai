Grok · grok-3

The top-rated options in Clusone (Val Seriana, Bergamo province, Lombardy, Italy) are a mix of a new luxury relais and highly scored B&Bs/small hotels, based on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and similar review sites.

Clusone is a small historic town (known as the “painted city” for its frescoes) near ski areas like Presolana/Monte Pora and roughly 30–40 minutes from Bergamo. Accommodations tend to be intimate rather than large resorts; many are family-run with strong hospitality scores.

Top picks

1. Collina Luxury Relais (best overall / luxury)

  • Newer 5-star property (opened ~2024) perched above town with panoramic mountain/valley views.
  • Highlights: Indoor-outdoor heated pools, spa/wellness, gourmet restaurant (fine dining with local ingredients), modern design, garden/terrace, free parking.
  • Ratings: ~9.3/10 on Booking (Wonderful, dozens of reviews); 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor; strong Google scores (~4.8). Guests praise views, spa, food, comfort, and staff.
  • Best for: Couples, wellness escapes, special occasions. Higher price point (often €400+). Michelin Selected status noted in some guides.

2. Bed & Breakfast Angela

  • Highly rated family-run B&B near the center (close to Orologio Planetario Fanzago).
  • Highlights: Clean comfortable rooms with mountain/garden views, excellent breakfast (buffet/continental options, including gluten-free), patio/terrace, private parking, warm hospitality.
  • Ratings: Exceptional ~9.6–9.7/10 on Booking (hundreds of reviews); 4.7/5 on Tripadvisor. Frequently tops “best of” lists for the area.
  • Best for: Value, personal service, and a central but quiet base.

3. B&B Del Centro

  • Central location (steps from the astronomical clock and historic sights).
  • Highlights: Shared lounge, clean rooms, strong guest feedback on hospitality and location.
  • Ratings: Around 9.1–9.4/10 on Booking with solid review volume.
  • Good mid-range/central choice.

4. Albergo Antica Locanda

  • Historic family-run 3-star hotel in the pedestrian historic center (Piazza Uccelli area).
  • Highlights: Restaurant serving traditional Bergamasque cuisine (polenta, casoncelli, pizza), garden/terrace, bar, renovated rooms, free Wi-Fi.
  • Ratings: ~9.0/10 on Booking (hundreds of reviews); solid Tripadvisor scores. Praised for location, food, and friendly service.
  • Ideal if you want an on-site restaurant and central atmosphere.

5. Hotel Ambra

  • Central family-managed hotel with restaurant.
  • Highlights: Clean rooms (some with hydromassage), good breakfast, attentive staff, convenient for walking the town.
  • Ratings: ~8.7/10 on Booking; around 4.3/5 on Tripadvisor. Consistent praise for location, cleanliness, and hospitality.

Other notables

  • Il Portico delle Rose — Newer/high-scoring small property (Exceptional ~9.7 on Booking in limited reviews), central and clean.
  • Albergo Commercio – Ristorante Mas-cì and Albergo La Bussola — Solid traditional options with restaurants; good for food-focused stays.
  • Nearby (Castione della Presolana area, ~10 km) there are more spa/resort-style places if you prefer ski-resort amenities.

Quick tips

  • Luxury/spa focus → Collina Luxury Relais.
  • Best value + hospitality → B&B Angela or B&B Del Centro.
  • Central historic + dining → Antica Locanda or Ambra.
  • Book ahead for peak seasons (summer hiking, winter skiing, or events). Prices and availability fluctuate; check Booking.com or Tripadvisor for current rates and exact review counts.
  • Clusone itself is compact and walkable; many places offer free parking or are near bus links to Bergamo.

Ratings and rankings can shift slightly with new reviews—verify directly on Booking or Tripadvisor for your dates. Enjoy the Val Seriana!
