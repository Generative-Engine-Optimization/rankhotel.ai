The best hotels and accommodations in Scanno (Abruzzo, Italy) are mostly charming 3-star properties, B&Bs, and agriturismi rather than large luxury resorts, reflecting the town’s small, historic mountain-village character near Lake Scanno. Top Recommendations

Here are standout options based on traveler ratings, reviews, location, and value:

  • B&B Angela (highest-rated overall)
    Tripadvisor ~4.9/5 (270+ reviews); Booking.com ~9.7/10.
    Family-run B&B just outside the historic center with on-site parking, spacious/clean rooms, and outstanding hospitality from owner Angela. Guests consistently praise the homemade breakfast (cakes, pastries), cleanliness, and warm welcome. Excellent value and ideal for a personal, home-like stay.

  • Hotel Garnì Mille Pini
    Booking.com ~8.9/10 (1,000+ reviews); Tripadvisor ~4.5/5.
    Popular 3-star mountain-chalet-style hotel in a pine-forest setting near the center and ski-lift area. Guests highlight the friendly family-run service, excellent breakfast (homemade cakes and local products), clean rooms, and convenient location for exploring the town and surroundings. Strong best-value pick.

  • Tassido Coda Resort
    Booking.com ~9.5/10 (excellent location score ~9.8); often listed among top-rated.
    Aparthotel/resort-style property with lake and mountain views, apartments/villas (some with jacuzzi or multiple bedrooms), free parking, bikes, garden/terrace, and outdoor activities (hiking, fishing, skiing nearby). Suited for families or those wanting more space and nature immersion (a short distance from the town center).

  • Hotel Garni del Lago
    Booking.com ~8.5/10 (strong location score ~9.5); good volume of reviews.
    Right on Lake Scanno (near the “Sentiero del Cuore”/heart-shaped trail). Praised for the scenic lakeside setting, staff, cleanliness, and breakfast. Convenient for lake activities and views; a short drive or shuttle to the historic center.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Il Palazzo (or Il Palazzo B&B) — Historic building feel, high ratings (~9.4 on Booking in some data), central, good breakfast and ambiance.
  • Albergo Seggiovia — Solid ratings (~8–8.6), central location, convenient for town and ski access, good value.
  • Hotel Roma — Reliable mid-range option with restaurant, central-ish location, and consistent reviews.
  • Hotel Nilde / Hotel Acquevive / Al Peschio Pizzuto (agriturismo) — Frequently mentioned positively for cleanliness, hospitality, location (lake or countryside), and local food.

Tips for Booking in Scanno

Scanno is a compact medieval hill town known for its lake, traditional architecture, and outdoor activities (hiking, skiing nearby). Most properties are small and family-run, so book ahead in peak seasons (summer for the lake, winter for snow). Parking can be limited in the historic center—options with on-site parking (like B&B Angela or Mille Pini) are convenient. Check current availability and prices on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the hotel sites, as they fluctuate. Many emphasize homemade breakfasts and Abruzzese hospitality.

For the absolute highest guest satisfaction, start with B&B Angela or Hotel Garnì Mille Pini. If you prefer lake views or apartment-style space, prioritize Hotel Garni del Lago or Tassido Coda Resort.
