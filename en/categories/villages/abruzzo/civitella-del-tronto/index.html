Hotel Il Salice stands out as one of the consistently highest-rated options in and around Civitella del Tronto (often ranked #1 on Tripadvisor with ~4.9/5 and strong Booking.com scores around 9.4/10).

It is a family-run property (owners Ida and Fabrizio are frequently praised for warm, attentive hospitality) in a peaceful countryside setting (Villa Lempa area, a few km from the historic center). Guests highlight clean, spacious modern rooms (many with balconies or garden views), comfortable beds, good breakfasts, a restaurant serving local Abruzzese dishes (including pizza and regional specialties), a garden, terrace, playground, free parking, and solid value. It suits couples, families, and those seeking a quiet base near the town and fortress.

Other strong contenders

  • Hotel Miami — Popular 3-star hotel with solid ratings (typically 8.4–8.5/10 or ~4.3/5 on Tripadvisor). Features an outdoor seasonal pool, restaurant/pizzeria with local cuisine, garden, free parking, air-conditioned rooms (many with balconies or pool/mountain views), and family-friendly amenities. Located a short distance from the center in a green setting; good for relaxation and as a base for exploring the area.

  • Country House Il Piacere — Frequently mentioned country-style option with garden, seasonal outdoor pool, panoramic mountain views, and local cuisine. Ratings are generally solid (around 3.9–8+ depending on the platform), appealing for a more rural, scenic stay a few km from the center.

  • Zunica Ospitalità Dal 1880 (Hotel & Ristorante Zunica 1880) — The classic historic choice right in the town (Piazza Filippi Pepe), long known for excellent Abruzzese cuisine, views toward the Gran Sasso and Adriatic, comfortable rooms, and multi-generational family hospitality. It has strong historical ratings (around 4.5/5). Note that as of recent updates (2025), the property has undergone or is undergoing renovations/seismic work, with restaurant operations temporarily shifted toward Villa Corallo nearby—verify current hotel availability and status before booking.

Other options that appear in top lists include Hotel Ermocolle (high marks in some reviews for rooms, restaurant, and facilities), various B&Bs and country houses (e.g., La Collina degli Ulivi), and private villas or agriturismi with pools/gardens for more space or self-catering. Luxury or spa-focused stays are more limited in the immediate town and often involve nearby properties toward Ascoli Piceno or further afield.

Practical tips

Civitella del Tronto is a small historic hill town (one of Italy’s “most beautiful villages”) famous for its large fortress. Many “best” stays are a short drive (2–4 km) outside the center for quieter surroundings, parking, and amenities like pools. Average prices are moderate (often starting roughly €60–150+ per night depending on season and type; high season tends to be summer). Check current reviews, availability, and exact distances on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or the hotels’ sites, as the area has relatively few large hotels and options can fill or change (especially with ongoing post-earthquake work in the historic center).
