Here are some of the best hotels and highly rated stays in Fagagna (Friuli-Venezia Giulia, Italy), based on guest reviews from Booking.com, Kayak, Trivago, Tripadvisor, and similar sites.

Fagagna is a small town, so options lean more toward boutique hotels, agriturismi (farm stays), and B&Bs rather than large chains. Ratings are generally high for the area.

Top Recommendations

Villaverde Hotel & Resort – Wellness Spa & Golf (most popular full-service option)

  • 4-star resort-style hotel with strong overall scores (typically 8.6–8.8/10 from 600–1,500+ reviews).
  • Highlights: Spa & wellness area, indoor and outdoor pools, fitness center, direct access to an 18-hole golf course with Alpine views, restaurant/bar, free parking, and modern rooms (many with golf or garden views).
  • Best for: Relaxation, couples, golfers, or anyone wanting amenities and a resort feel. It’s slightly outside the absolute center but in a peaceful, scenic setting.

Albergo Baschera (excellent central choice with strong value)

  • Consistently high scores (around 9.2–9.4/10 from hundreds of reviews).
  • Historic property (roots going back to 1806) that has been renovated; features a restaurant, bar/osteria, and well-regarded pizzeria (wood-fired, long-fermentation dough with local ingredients). Air-conditioned rooms, free private parking, and a convenient location right in/near the center of Fagagna.
  • Best for: Food lovers, central stays, and good value. Guests frequently praise the friendly staff, food, and location.

Casa Pellis (Alloggio Agrituristico)

  • High ratings (typically 9.1–9.3/10 or ~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor).
  • Charming agriturismo/B&B in a historic building with garden views, outdoor pool + solarium, free parking, pet-friendly policy, air-conditioned rooms, and breakfast included. Peaceful setting with a Friulian courtyard feel.
  • Best for: Families, couples seeking a quieter, more authentic countryside experience, or those who want a pool.

Stop & Sleep Fagagna on the Hills

  • Excellent scores (9.2–9.4/10 or 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor from 70–160+ reviews).
  • Elegant B&B in a restored 18th-century palace (Palazzo Saccovan) in the historic center. Stylish, design-oriented rooms/suites with private bathrooms, terrace, and a refined atmosphere. Close to the golf club.
  • Best for: Design lovers, romantic or short stays, and those who want to stay in the heart of the town.

Other Strong Options

  • B&B Casa del Vicario — Frequently scores around 9.5/10; well-liked for comfort, location, and hospitality.
  • Borgo Terra village — High ratings (~9.2); more holiday-park style with good reviews for space and atmosphere.
  • B&B La Marjolaine di Ale e Madda — Strong scores (9.3–9.4 range) for a quieter, personal B&B experience.
  • Il Fienile — Very high Tripadvisor scores (near-perfect with smaller review volume); praised for atmosphere, hospitality, and food.

Quick Tips

  • Villaverde is the standout if you want spa, pool, and golf facilities.
  • Albergo Baschera or Stop & Sleep are ideal for a more central, town-based stay with excellent food nearby.
  • Casa Pellis or similar agriturismi work well for a peaceful, nature-oriented break.
  • Fagagna is close to Udine, San Daniele del Friuli (famous for prosciutto), and the surrounding hills/morainic landscape, so most places make a good base for exploring the area.
  • Prices and availability vary by season—check recent reviews and book directly or via major platforms for the latest rates and free-cancellation options.
