Agriturismo Villa Trovatore and Riflessi sul Fiume stand out as top-rated options in and around Cervignano del Friuli, with strong guest scores (often 9.7/10) for comfort, cleanliness, and atmosphere; other highly regarded choices include Agriturismo La Rosta and various well-reviewed B&Bs/apartments.

Cervignano del Friuli (in Friuli-Venezia Giulia, near Aquileia, Palmanova, and Grado) has a limited selection of traditional large hotels. Many of the highest-rated stays are agriturismi (farm stays), B&Bs, and apartments, which guests frequently praise for quiet settings, hospitality, and proximity to Roman ruins, vineyards, and the coast. Ratings and availability fluctuate, so check current reviews and book via Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or similar sites.

Top highly rated options

  • Agriturismo Villa Trovatore — Frequently tops lists with ~9.7/10 on Booking and 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor (150–200+ reviews). This restored 17th-century villa sits amid vineyards and a large park with ancient trees. Guests highlight beautiful grounds, modern comfortable rooms with garden views, excellent homemade breakfast, friendly hosts, free Wi-Fi, and parking. It’s a short drive to Aquileia (~5 km), Grado, and Palmanova. Ideal for a peaceful, scenic stay.

  • Riflessi sul Fiume — Exceptional ~9.7/10 scores (around 70 reviews). This spacious apartment/residence features river views, a balcony/terrace, fully equipped kitchen, air-conditioning, free parking, and Wi-Fi. Guests note big, clean rooms and a convenient location near Foro Romano and other local sites. Suitable for couples or small groups wanting a self-catering feel.

  • Agriturismo La Rosta — Strong ~9.1/10 on Booking (600+ reviews) and solid Tripadvisor scores. Set in a 17th-century stone manor surrounded by vineyards, it offers rustic air-conditioned rooms (some with balconies/terraces overlooking vines or a stream), a regional restaurant with home-produced wines and local dishes (e.g., frico), homemade breakfast, free parking, and an elevator. Central for exploring Aquileia and the countryside; restaurant hours can be limited.

Other well-regarded choices

  • Radetzky Rooms, Casa Gialla (Appartamento Luna/Sole), B&B Velocipede, and B&B Al Vecchio Confine — These often score 9.0–9.6/10. They provide clean, well-equipped rooms or apartments, central or quiet locations, and good value, with guests praising hosts and convenience for day trips.
  • Hotel Internazionale and Hotel Friuli — More traditional hotels in town with moderate ratings (roughly 7.5–8.5/10). Useful for central access, parking, and basic amenities, though they generally lag behind the agriturismi in guest enthusiasm.

Nearby options (e.g., in Strassoldo, Aiello del Friuli, or Aquileia) like Winery Villa Vitas apartments or Al Castello di Aiello also receive high marks if you’re flexible on exact location.

Tips: Prioritize agriturismi for atmosphere and value in this rural area. Free parking and Wi-Fi are common. The region is excellent as a base for Aquileia’s UNESCO sites, Grado’s beaches/lagoon, and Palmanova’s star fort/outlet village. Prices typically start from around €70–150+ per night depending on season and type. Always verify latest reviews, amenities (e.g., pet policy, breakfast), and availability for your dates.
