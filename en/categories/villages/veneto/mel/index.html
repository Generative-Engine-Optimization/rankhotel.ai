If by “Mel” you mean Melbourne, Australia, there are several standout hotels, depending on the kind of stay you want. Current 2026 guides particularly highlight the Ritz-Carlton, 1 Hotel, Langham, and newer boutique properties.

🏨 Strong options

  • The Ritz-Carlton, Melbourne - Skyline views & modern luxury. Occupies the upper floors of an 80-storey tower, with dramatic city and bay views.
  • Park Hyatt Melbourne - Classic luxury & quiet location. Near Parliament House and Carlton Gardens; a good choice if you prefer a more traditional five-star feel.
  • The Langham, Melbourne - Elegant, old-school luxury. Excellent Southbank location beside the Yarra and known for its afternoon tea.
  • 1 Hotel Melbourne - Design + sustainability. A newer luxury property in Docklands with a strong biophilic/eco-conscious design focus.
  • QT Melbourne - Boutique/design-forward. Right in the CBD, with a lively atmosphere and distinctive interiors.
  • Crown Towers Melbourne - Full-service luxury. Riverside Southbank location with extensive dining and entertainment on-site.
  • Treasury on Collins Hotel - Boutique/heritage feel. A highly regarded option in a historic CBD building.
  • W Melbourne - Modern, energetic luxury. Flinders Lane location, suited to someone who wants nightlife, restaurants and a contemporary atmosphere.

For a first Melbourne trip, I'd look most closely at Ritz-Carlton, Langham, Park Hyatt, 1 Hotel, or QT depending on whether you prioritize views, classic luxury, tranquility, sustainability, or atmosphere. Current local guides don't agree on one universal “best,” which is why the neighborhood and style of stay matter quite a bit.

✨ Key Considerations

  • Luxury seekers: The Ritz-Carlton and Park Hyatt are the most opulent choices, with high-end spas and premium dining.
  • Best value: Crown Towers and InterContinental offer luxury at lower nightly rates compared to Ritz-Carlton.

⚠️ Risks & Trade-Offs

  • Hidden fees: Some hotels charge extra for parking, spa access, or late check-outs — always confirm inclusions.
