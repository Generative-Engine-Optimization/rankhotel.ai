Grok · grok-3

Hotel Roxy Plaza, Monte Tondo (winery B&B), Corte dei Soavi, and Damaranto Residenza e Cucina rank among the top options in Soave (the medieval wine town near Verona, Veneto).

Soave is a small, charming walled town famous for its castle and Soave wine. Accommodation is limited and often boutique, B&B, agriturismo, or small hotels rather than large resorts. Ratings and popularity come from sources like Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Trivago, Kayak, and Expedia (as of recent 2025–2026 data). Prices typically start around €80–150+ per night depending on season, dates, and room type—check current availability.

Top recommendations

Hotel Roxy Plaza (4-star, central)

  • Highly popular and frequently ranked near the top. Located just outside the medieval walls, with easy access to the historic center and Scaligero Castle.
  • Ratings: ~8.4–9.0 (hundreds of reviews). Guests praise the location, clean/spacious rooms (some with castle/vineyard views), helpful staff, and breakfast. Amenities include free Wi-Fi, parking (book ahead; some covered options), air conditioning, and a bar.
  • Best for convenience if you want to walk around the town.

Monte Tondo (Monte Tondo Winery / B&B)

  • Family-run agriturismo/winery on a hillside with views, about 1–1.6 km from the center. Produces Soave and Valpolicella wines; offers tastings/tours.
  • Ratings: ~8.8–9.0 (many reviews). Features an outdoor pool, free parking, free Wi-Fi, garden, and rooms/suites/apartments (some with jacuzzi). Guests like the peaceful setting, wine experiences, and hospitality.
  • Ideal for a wine-focused or relaxing countryside stay (car helpful).

Corte dei Soavi (relais / family rooms & apartments)

  • In the hills amid vineyards (Costeggiola area, short drive from the center). Restored historic property with garden and seasonal outdoor pool.
  • Ratings: Very high (~9.5–9.7 or 4.7–4.9/5). Guests highlight the peaceful setting, clean comfortable rooms/apartments (some with kitchens/terraces), excellent breakfast, pool, and wine-related experiences (tastings, e-bike tours available). Free parking and Wi-Fi.
  • Great for couples or families seeking nature and tranquility.

Damaranto Residenza e Cucina

  • Central location (Corso Vittorio Emanuele II) with panoramic castle views, garden, and on-site restaurant/bar serving Italian cuisine.
  • Ratings: ~9.1–9.2. Spacious rooms, good breakfast, friendly staff, and a romantic/modern vibe. Amenities include free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and outdoor seating/hot tub options in some setups.
  • Excellent for those who want to stay in town and dine on-site.

Other strong options

  • Antica Corte — High ratings (~9.2); historic building with garden, free parking, and pet-friendly options.
  • Residenza Ai Capitelli — Strong scores (~9.3); outdoor pool with hydromassage and good breakfast.
  • Agriturismo/Corte Tamellini — High ratings (~9.5+); pool, countryside views, and peaceful setting.
  • Albergo Al Gambero or smaller places like Star Soave Rooms — Solid central or well-rated budget-friendly alternatives.

Nearby (San Bonifacio or surrounding areas, a short drive) options like Best Western Plus Soave Hotel or Relais Villabella offer pools and more hotel-style amenities if Soave itself feels too limited.

Tips

  • A car is useful for vineyard visits and countryside properties (many are 1–3 km out). The town center is compact and walkable.
  • Book ahead, especially in peak wine/tourism season. Look for free cancellation and confirm parking.
  • For the latest rates, availability, and reviews, check Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or hotel sites directly—ratings can shift slightly with new reviews.

Soave pairs well with day trips to Verona, Lake Garda, or Valpolicella wine areas. Enjoy the white wines and medieval atmosphere!
