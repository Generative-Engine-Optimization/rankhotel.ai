Cusano Mutri is a small medieval village (one of Italy’s “Borghi più belli d’Italia”) in the Matese mountains of Campania (Province of Benevento). Most accommodations are B&Bs, agriturismi, or small guesthouses rather than large hotels. Options emphasize scenic views, nature access (Matese Regional/National Park), and a quiet, authentic atmosphere.

Piana la Gatta (Contrada San Felice) receives exceptional praise for its picturesque setting at the foot of Monte Mutri, spotless rooms, generous homemade breakfasts (local products, pastries, etc.), and warm hosts. Guests frequently call it a “quiet heaven” or “enchanted place.” It offers air-conditioned rooms (some with kitchenettes/patios), free Wi-Fi and parking, and is family-friendly.

Casale San Felice is a renovated country house with eco-friendly materials, large garden, barbecue area, and solarium/terrace—ideal for those wanting more outdoor space and privacy.

Other Notes

  • Nearby alternatives: If you want a fuller resort experience, consider Aquapetra Resort & Spa in Telese Terme (~8 miles / 13 km away; spa, pools, higher price point, solid ratings around 8.6–8.8). Other options appear in nearby villages like Faicchio or San Lorenzello.
  • Practical tips: Many places include free parking and breakfast. A car is highly recommended for exploring the area (hiking, gorges, nearby towns). Naples airport is roughly 70–75 km away. Prices typically range from ~€80–150+ per night depending on season and dates (check current rates, as they fluctuate).
  • When to go: The area is popular for nature, the medieval center, and events like the mushroom festival. Shoulder seasons (e.g., spring/fall) can offer better value and fewer crowds.

For the latest availability, prices, and exact reviews, check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Kayak directly, as scores and inventory can change. Piana la Gatta is the safest “best overall” recommendation based on volume and consistency of glowing feedback.
