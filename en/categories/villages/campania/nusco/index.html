Quick Tips

  • Best overall for most visitors: Hotel Colucci for convenience, on-site dining, and consistent praise as a reliable base for exploring Nusco and nearby Irpinia towns. Guests frequently highlight the friendly family atmosphere and good value.
  • Most highly rated intimate stay: Donna Chiara if you prefer a smaller B&B experience with strong personal service and central location.
  • Quieter/nature-focused: Tenuta San Martino.
  • Nusco is compact and walkable in the historic center. A car is useful for surrounding areas (Montella, Bagnoli Irpino, etc.). Prices fluctuate with season and demand—check current rates and availability on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Trip.com, as options fill quickly in peak periods.
  • Amenities commonly available: free parking, Wi-Fi, breakfast. True luxury hotels are scarce here; the appeal is authentic small-town hospitality and scenery.
