Quick Tips

  • Location note: Tempio Pausania is inland. The coast (Costa Smeralda, beaches) is roughly 30–60 minutes by car. Many guests use the town as a base for exploring cork forests, Monte Limbara, nuraghi, and Gallura culture.
  • Amenities: Look for free parking (useful in the historic center), pools (more common outside the very center), and breakfast. High-rated places often excel on hospitality and cleanliness.
  • When to book: Prices typically start around €70–150+ per night (depending on season, room type, and platform). Summer is busier; shoulder seasons can offer better value and quieter stays.
  • Recommendations by preference:
    • In-town charm + high ratings → Calycanto or Al Vecchio Corso.
    • Pool + facilities + value → Pausania Inn.
    • Unique/peaceful nature escape → L’Agnata di De André.
