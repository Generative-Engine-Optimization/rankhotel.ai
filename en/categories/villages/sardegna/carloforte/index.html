Nichotel, Lu’ Hotel Riviera (also called Lu’ Hotel Carloforte), Dimora Ferralasco, Hotel Hieracon, and Aquamadre Suites / Le Saline Suites stand out among the best options in Carloforte (on San Pietro Island, Sardinia).

Carloforte is a small, charming town, so most highly rated places are central (near the harbor, promenade, and historic center) or a short walk away. Ratings and rankings come primarily from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and traveler sites (as of recent data).

Other strong options

  • Hotel Villa Pimpina — Quirky, well-rated (~9.0+), good breakfast, more budget-friendly boutique feel.
  • Residenza Cuntin, Domo Cicindela, and Villa Iole — High guest scores for apartments/suites or B&Bs; central or quiet with good reviews for cleanliness and hosts.
  • Hotel Galman or Guardia dei Mori — Quieter or hillside options with views/parking (Galman is a bit farther out, better with a car/bike for beach access).

Practical tips

  • Location matters: Stay in or very near the historic center/harbor for walkability to restaurants, the waterfront, and ferries. Outer spots (or those near beaches like Giunco or Caletta) are quieter but may need transport.
  • Book ahead in peak summer (especially July–August) as the island is small and popular.
  • Many places include breakfast; check for parking (limited in the very center), air conditioning, and pet policies.
  • Prices vary widely by season (often starting from ~€70–150+ per night for good mid-range options, higher for top boutique rooms with views).

For the latest availability, exact prices, and reviews, check Booking.com or Tripadvisor directly, as ratings and inventory change. Carloforte itself is compact and walkable, with excellent seafood (especially tuna) and beaches nearby. Enjoy your stay!
