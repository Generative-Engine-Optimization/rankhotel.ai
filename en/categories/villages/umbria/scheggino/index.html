Torre Del Nera Albergo Diffuso & Spa (also known as Antica Torre Del Nera) stands out as the top choice in Scheggino, a small medieval village in Umbria’s Valnerina valley.

Top Recommendations

Here are the best-rated options based on consistent rankings from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Trivago, and similar sites (ratings as of recent data; always check current availability and reviews):

  1. Torre Del Nera Albergo Diffuso & Spa

    • Why it’s the best: Unique “albergo diffuso” (scattered hotel) concept with rooms and apartments spread through the restored medieval village. Panoramic Valnerina views, spa (heated pool, sauna, steam bath), restaurant serving local Umbrian cuisine, and strong emphasis on historic charm mixed with modern comforts.
    • Ratings: ~8.9–9.1/10 on Booking (hundreds of reviews), 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor (800+ reviews). Guests frequently praise the location, staff, cleanliness, spa, and breakfast.
    • Location: Right in the village center (Via di Borgo 72).
    • Best for: Couples, relaxation, and immersion in the village atmosphere. Note that access involves some walking/stairs in the car-free historic center (luggage help often available).

  2. Antico Casale Urbani

    • Why it’s strong: Historic 15th-century country house/farm stay owned by the Urbani family (known for truffles). Features a garden, restaurant with local specialties (truffles, porcini, Norcia products), outdoor pool (seasonal; rock-hewn pools mentioned in some descriptions), fitness room, bicycle rental, and family-friendly amenities.
    • Ratings: ~9.0/10 on Booking, 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor.
    • Location: About 0.8 km from the village center (Case Campore).
    • Best for: Nature lovers, foodies, families, or those wanting a quieter countryside setting with parking.

  3. B&B Beep Beep, Tranquillo

    • High guest scores (~9.2/10 on Booking with solid reviews for helpful hosts and views). A more intimate bed-and-breakfast option in Scheggino. Good mid-range alternative if the top two are booked.

Other Notable Mentions

  • Umbria Valnerina, Il TARTUFFO (or similar high-scoring apartments/houses): Exceptional ratings (9.8–9.9) but fewer reviews; a bit farther out (~3–4 km).
  • Smaller highly rated spots like Domus Petrae or private casali/villas with pools appear frequently in vacation rental listings.
  • Nearby alternatives (if Scheggino is full): Options in Spoleto (e.g., Hotel dei Duchi, Aurora Boutique Hotel) or Ferentillo (e.g., Abbazia San Pietro in Valle), roughly 8–15 minutes away.

Quick Tips

Scheggino is a tiny, peaceful hilltop village focused on nature, hiking, truffles, and the Nera River—expect limited large hotels and more boutique/agriturismo-style stays. Prices typically start around €100–200+ per night depending on season and room type (higher for spa suites or peak periods).

For the latest prices, availability, and reviews, check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the properties’ official sites. Book early for peak seasons (spring–autumn), as options are limited.
