Todi is a quintessential Umbrian hilltop town, and choosing the right place to stay depends entirely on whether you want to be right inside the medieval walls or tucked away in the surrounding vineyards and olive groves.

Because the historic center is mostly pedestrian-friendly and parking can be notoriously tricky, boutique guesthouses inside the town offer unparalleled charm and walkability, while the resorts just minutes outside the walls give you sweeping views, pools, and spas.

Grok · grok-3

Relais Todini, Roccafiore Wine Resort & Spa, Residenza D’Epoca San Lorenzo Tre, Torre Sangiovanni, and Borgo Petroro stand out among the best hotels in Todi (Umbria, Italy), based on consistent high guest ratings across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trivago, and similar sites.

Todi is a charming hill town, so options range from historic properties in or near the center to countryside resorts with views, pools, and spa facilities. Here are some of the top-rated ones:

Luxury / High-End

  • Relais Todini (Residenza d’Epoca): Frequently tops lists. Historic hilltop property surrounded by vineyards with stunning Umbrian views, outdoor pool, gourmet restaurant, and excellent service. Ratings typically around 4.6/5 on Tripadvisor and 9.4–9.5/10 elsewhere. Ideal for a romantic or upscale countryside stay (a short drive from the town center).
  • Roccafiore Wine Resort & Spa: Converted farmhouse resort overlooking vineyards and olive groves (about 3–4 km from Todi). Strong spa facilities (indoor/outdoor pools), wine focus, tennis, and high ratings (often 4.7/5 on Tripadvisor, 9.2–9.3/10). Great for relaxation and wellness.

Central / Historic Charm

  • Residenza D’Epoca San Lorenzo Tre: Excellent central location near the cathedral and main piazza. Family-run historic residence with panoramic terrace views, stylish rooms, and very high Booking scores (around 9.7/10). Homely atmosphere with strong guest praise for hospitality.
  • Monastero SS. Annunziata: Unique stay in a former monastery. Atmospheric common areas with antiques, friendly staff, and solid ratings (often 8.7–9.0). Short walk (uphill) to the historic center; practical parking nearby.
  • Hotel Fonte Cesia: Well-located 4-star option in the town with good breakfast and convenient access to sights. Reliable mid-to-upper range choice.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Torre Sangiovanni Albergo e Ristorante da Rosary: Popular for its antique-filled rooms, garden/rooftop terrace, and consistent high volume of positive reviews. Slightly outside the absolute center but highly regarded.
  • Borgo Petroro Country Relais & Restaurant: Scenic countryside property with panoramic views of Todi, outdoor pool, and strong ratings (around 4.7/5). Good for a peaceful base with on-site dining.
  • Tenuta di Canonica: Stylish historic conversion (former water tower) with gardens, valley/olive grove views, pool, and Umbrian cuisine. Often recommended for romantic escapes.
  • Smaller highly rated options include Il Ghiottone Umbro (charming former mill B&B with contemporary rooms and views) and Hortus Natural Living (excellent guest scores for its natural/peaceful setting).

Quick Tips

  • Location preference: Stay in/near the historic center (San Lorenzo Tre, Fonte Cesia, Monastero) for walkability, or choose a countryside resort (Relais Todini, Roccafiore, Borgo Petroro) for views, pools, and quiet—most are only a short drive away.
  • Ratings note: Scores are generally high (many 9+ on Booking or 4.5+/5 on Tripadvisor), but check recent reviews for your dates as smaller properties can vary seasonally.
  • Book via major platforms (Booking, Tripadvisor, etc.) for current rates, availability, and cancellation policies. Spring–fall is peak for the region; summer offers pool weather.
