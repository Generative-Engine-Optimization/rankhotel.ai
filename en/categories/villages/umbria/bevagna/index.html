Grok · grok-3

L’Orto degli Angeli, Antico Borgo Carceri & Wellness, Genius Loci Country Inn, Palazzo Brunamonti Boutique Hotel, and Residenza Porta Guelfa stand out among the best options in and around Bevagna (a charming medieval town in Umbria with Roman ruins, a picturesque historic center, and easy access to Assisi, Spello, and the Montefalco wine area).

Bevagna itself is compact, so central stays put you right in the historic core for walking, while countryside properties (agriturismi or restored borghi) offer more space, pools, and views. Ratings and rankings come from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, The Hotel Guru, Trip.com, and similar sources (as of recent data). Preferences matter—romantic/historic, pool/relaxation, central convenience, or countryside peace—so here’s a breakdown of the strongest options:

Top Picks

  • L’Orto degli Angeli (central Bevagna)
    Elegant 14-room hotel in a carefully restored 17th-century palazzo (actually two connected historic buildings). Features original details like terracotta floors, wooden beams, antiques, and frescoes; romantic rooms (some with four-posters); beautiful private gardens with kitchen produce; and the excellent Redibis restaurant set in part of an ancient Roman theater/amphitheater structure. Cooking classes available. Highly praised for atmosphere, history, food, and staff. Ideal for couples seeking an intimate, authentic Umbrian experience. Tripadvisor around 4.4/5 (150+ reviews); often recommended as one of the standout properties in town.

  • Antico Borgo Carceri & Wellness (countryside, a few km from Bevagna)
    Restored medieval borgo/country house with apartments and rooms, large outdoor pool (often with scenic views toward Assisi/Mount Subasio), gardens, spa/wellness elements (sauna, etc.), free bikes, and strong hospitality. High ratings (frequently 9.7–9.8/10 or 5/5 on Tripadvisor with smaller review volumes; strong praise for setting, cleanliness, breakfast, and hosts). Excellent for relaxation, nature, and a peaceful base while still close to Bevagna.

  • Genius Loci Country Inn (countryside near Bevagna)
    Family-run luxury country inn in a meticulously restored 1880s farmhouse amid vineyards and olive groves. Panoramic views, outdoor pool, garden/terrace, high-end rustic-elegant rooms, and often their own wine. Tripadvisor frequently ~4.9/5; Booking-style scores around 9.2+. Great for a refined countryside escape with personal attention.

  • Palazzo Brunamonti Boutique Hotel (central Bevagna)
    Boutique hotel in a restored historic building (linked to an Umbrian poet’s family) right in the town center. Convenient for exploring on foot, good breakfast, quiet atmosphere, and solid modern comforts. Frequently tops or ranks high on Trip.com/Booking lists (scores often ~9.2/10); well-reviewed for location, cleanliness, and hospitality.

  • Residenza Porta Guelfa (very near the center)
    Charming property with classic Umbrian-style rooms, outdoor pool, gardens, and easy walk into town. Strong Tripadvisor presence (around 4.5/5 with hundreds of reviews) and consistent mentions for location, value, and amenities. Good all-rounder.

Other Strong Options

  • Ostello Bello Assisi Bevagna (Torre del Colle area) — Higher-rated hostel/hotel hybrid with outdoor pool, atmospheric medieval setting, views (sometimes toward Assisi), and friendly vibe. Excellent value and guest scores (often 4.8/5 Tripadvisor or 8.7–8.9/10). More social/budget-friendly.
  • Agriturismi such as Agriturismo la Fonte, Agriturismo Etico Le Grazie, or Agriturismo I Getsemani — Peaceful farm stays amid olive groves/vineyards, often with pools, kitchens or kitchenettes, and authentic local feel. High ratings for setting and hosts.
  • il Monastero di Bevagna — Historic former monastery setting with cloister garden; strong reviews for atmosphere and location.

Practical Tips

Bevagna has a limited number of true hotels (many options are B&Bs, residences, or agriturismi). Central properties put you steps from Piazza Silvestri, churches, and the museum; countryside ones are better for pools, views, and quiet. The area is excellent for food/wine (Sagrantino), walking/cycling, and day trips. Closest airport is Perugia (San Francesco d’Assisi), roughly 30–40 minutes by car.

Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer and during events like the Mercato delle Gaite medieval festival) and can start from roughly €80–150+ per night for good mid-range options, higher for the top historic or luxury properties. Always check current availability, exact ratings, photos, and cancellation policies on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the property’s site, as they fluctuate. For personalized recommendations, consider your dates, budget, whether you want a car, and priorities (e.g., pool, restaurant, romantic vs. family). Enjoy Umbria!
