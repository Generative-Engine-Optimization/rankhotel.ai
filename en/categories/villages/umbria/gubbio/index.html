«what are the best hotels in Gubbio?»

ChatGPT · gpt-5-6

Gubbio has some lovely historic hotels, and the best choice depends on whether you want old-world atmosphere in the center or a more luxurious countryside stay.

My shortlist

  • Park Hotel Ai Cappuccini - A converted 17th-century monastery just outside the historic center. It has a pool, extensive grounds, spa/wellness facilities and a more full-service feel. Current guest ratings are around 9.1/10 on Booking.com and 4.6/5 on Tripadvisor.
    Best for: a comfortable/luxurious stay, couples, relaxing at the hotel.

  • Hotel Relais Ducale - Right in the medieval heart of Gubbio, beside Piazza Grande and close to the cathedral and Palazzo dei Consoli. It's particularly appealing if you want to walk everywhere and experience Gubbio after the day-trippers leave.
    Best for: historic atmosphere and location.

  • Hotel Gattapone - Another excellent choice inside the historic center, in a pedestrian area just around the corner from Piazza Grande. Booking.com currently gives it 8.6/10 from more than 500 reviews.
    Best for: central location and traditional Gubbio experience.

  • Hotel Bosone Palace - A characterful historic property in the center, with antique furnishings and spacious rooms. It's only about 200 feet from the cathedral.
    Best for: medieval ambience and historic interiors.

  • Hub Hotel - A more contemporary option, with excellent recent guest feedback; Tripadvisor currently shows 4.8/5, while Booking.com shows 9.0/10 from over 1,200 reviews.
    Best for: modern comfort and amenities rather than sleeping inside the medieval core.

If you want something really special

Castello di Petroia is worth considering even though it's outside the center. It's a genuine medieval castle setting with gardens, a seasonal pool and countryside surroundings; Booking.com currently shows a 9.3/10 guest score.

For a first visit, I'd focus on Relais Ducale, Gattapone, or Bosone Palace if being in the historic center matters most; choose Park Hotel Ai Cappuccini if the hotel itself is part of the experience.

🏖️ Key Considerations

  • Luxury & Spa: If you want indulgence, Park Hotel ai Cappuccini is the standout choice with its spa and pool.
  • Historic Charm: Hotel Relais Ducale and Bosone Palace immerse you in Gubbio’s medieval atmosphere.
  • Budget-Friendly: Hotel San Marco and Porta Marmorea balance affordability with comfort.

⚠️ Trade-Offs & Tips

  • Location vs. Views: Central hotels like Relais Ducale are convenient for walking around town, while hillside or countryside resorts like Cantina Semonte offer panoramic views but require transport.
  • Seasonality: Pools at Villa Montegranelli and Cantina Semonte are seasonal, so availability depends on the time of year.
  • Atmosphere: Choose between lively social settings (Relais Ducale) or serene retreats (Park Hotel ai Cappuccini).
  • Budget vs. Luxury: Prices range from ~$73 for Relais Ducale to ~$223 for Cappuccini.

Grok · grok-3

Park Hotel Ai Cappuccini stands out as one of the strongest overall options, frequently ranking near the top on Tripadvisor (around 4.6/5 from 1,500+ reviews) and other sites (often 9.0–9.2).

It is a converted 17th-century monastery just outside the historic center (short walk or quick drive). Highlights include a large spa/wellness center, indoor pool with unique design elements, gardens with views, a good restaurant serving Umbrian cuisine (including truffles), and a mix of classic and more modern rooms with vaulted ceilings and stone features. Guests praise the serene grounds, staff, breakfast, and facilities; some note that rooms can vary in size/style and that it is not the absolute cheapest option.

Other top recommendations

  • Relais Ducale — Excellent central location on or near Piazza Grande (one of Italy’s most scenic squares), in a historic building linked to the Ducal Palace. Rustic-chic rooms with exposed stone, wooden floors, and antique furnishings; terrace/garden with views; good for exploring on foot. Solid ratings (often ~8.5–8.7). Family-run feel; parking can require a bit of planning in the historic center.

  • Castello di Petroia (Castello Di Petroia Relais / Dimora d’Epoca) — High guest scores (frequently 4.8/5 or 9.3–9.5). A genuine medieval castle (birthplace of Federico da Montefeltro) set in the countryside roughly 15–20 minutes from Gubbio. Atmospheric rooms in historic buildings, seasonal outdoor pool, gourmet dining by candlelight, extensive grounds/park, and strong sense of history and privacy. Ideal for a romantic or special-stay experience rather than pure walkability to the town center.

  • Bosone Palace — Historic 15th-century palace right in the center (near the cathedral and Ducal Palace). Characterful rooms with beams and period details, good breakfast, and convenient location. Solid mid-to-upper ratings.

  • Hub Hotel — Frequently praised for cleanliness, modern comfort, value, and helpful staff (high scores such as 4.8 or ~9.0). Slightly outside the absolute core of the historic center but still walkable or a short distance; good practical base.

Additional solid options

  • Hotel Gattapone and Hotel San Marco — Reliable central choices with solid reviews, convenient for sightseeing.
  • Rural or agriturismo-style stays (e.g., Nikis Resort, various countryside properties or other castles like Castello di Baccaresca) for views, pools, and quieter settings if you prefer something outside the town.

Quick tips: Gubbio’s historic center is hilly with limited car access/parking in the core, so central hotels favor walkers while properties just outside (like Park Hotel Ai Cappuccini) offer easier parking and more facilities. Ratings and rankings can shift slightly with dates and platforms (Tripadvisor, Booking.com, etc.), so check recent reviews and current availability for your travel period. Prices vary by season—expect higher rates for the top properties, especially those with spa or castle ambiance.
