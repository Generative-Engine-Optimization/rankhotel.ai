Other Strong Contenders

  • B&B Le Riue and B&B Penak — High guest scores (often 9+ or 4.6–4.7), more intimate/smaller scale.
  • Residence Biancaneve — Frequently well-rated and more affordable.
  • Apartments and vacation rentals (many score 9.5–9.7 on Booking) are popular for groups or those wanting kitchens; several are within a short walk of the slopes.

Quick Tips

  • Ski focus: Prioritize Cristallo (true ski-in/ski-out feel), Ambrosini, Ginepro, or properties near Baradello/San Pietro lifts. Many hotels offer heated ski storage.
  • Families: Cristallo (mini-club + pools) and Serenella stand out.
  • Location: Aprica is compact—most central hotels are a short walk from lifts, shops, and restaurants. The village sits on a pass linking Valtellina and Val Camonica.
  • Season & prices: Winter (ski season) is busiest and more expensive; summer is quieter and often better value. Expect roughly €100–€200+ per night depending on dates, room type, and season (prices fluctuate).
  • Check recent reviews and availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotels’ official sites, as smaller properties can fill quickly in peak periods.
