Other Strong Contenders

  • Hotel Arlecchino (Dada Hotels): Pool, family-oriented, near lifts; solid mid-range option.
  • Albergo Mangusta: Good ratings (~8.5), local cuisine, views (somewhat farther from the main center in some listings).
  • Smaller/high-rated alternatives: Places like Ginepro Casa Vacanze, For Rest, or MadeBoutique Rooms often score very high (9+ on Booking) as apartments/B&Bs with great locations and personal service, but they are not full-service hotels.

Tips for Booking

  • Ski access is a major factor—prioritize central or ski-in/ski-out properties (La Meridiana, Soldanella, Sport Hotel Alpina excel here). Madesimo has ~60 km of slopes with good intermediate/advanced terrain and snow reliability.
  • Seasonality: Winter (ski season, roughly late Nov–mid-April) is peak; book early for holidays. Summer is quieter for hiking.
  • Prices: Vary widely by dates/season (often €150–400+ per night depending on room and board). Half-board is common and popular due to strong hotel restaurants.
  • Check current reviews and availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the official Madesimo tourism site, as smaller properties can fill quickly. Many are pet-friendly or offer parking/spa extras.

Madesimo is relatively compact and family-oriented rather than a large party resort, so most highly rated hotels emphasize location, food, and a warm Alpine welcome. For the absolute latest rates or specific dates, check the booking sites directly.
