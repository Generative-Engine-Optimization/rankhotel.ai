Best options directly in Campitello Matese

These are the strongest choices for ski-in/ski-out convenience (or very short walks) and mountain atmosphere:

  • Hotel Miletto (often listed as Hotel Miletto & Spa) — One of the most popular and frequently recommended. About 100 m from the slopes. It has a spa/wellness area (sauna, Turkish bath, heated pool with hydromassage), restaurant, and family-friendly amenities. Ratings typically 8.4–8.8/10 on Booking.com (strong on location ~9.4–9.5) and around 4.5/5 on Tripadvisor (1,000+ reviews). Guests praise the position, staff, spa, and views; some note rooms can feel basic relative to price or mention occasional service inconsistencies. Good for families and those wanting on-site relaxation after skiing.

  • Club Hotel Lo Sciatore (Hotel Lo Sciatore) — Excellent central location facing the square, roughly 50 m from the ski slopes and near the bobsled track/cross-country trails. Family-run feel with restaurant (local/Italian cuisine), bar, kids’ club/activities, and ski facilities. Ratings often 8.8–9.2/10 (very high on location, cleanliness, and staff). Praised for hospitality, convenience for skiers, and authentic mountain vibe. Ideal if you want to leave the car parked and walk everywhere in the resort area.

  • La Pinetina — Right by the slopes with a pine-forest setting and trails nearby. Features a well-regarded restaurant (strong on local food/pizza), garden, and straightforward mountain rooms. Ratings around 9.0–9.1/10 on Booking (excellent location scores near 9.8–9.9 and high marks for staff). Guests frequently highlight the position, friendly service, cleanliness, and food. A solid, more intimate/practical choice.

Local guides also mention Hotel Kristall & Spa (with recent management updates noted for 2026/27; spa and family options) as another on-plateau possibility worth checking for availability.

Strong nearby alternatives

If the plateau hotels are full or you prefer more space, lower prices, or extra amenities:

  • Casale Maginulfo (Roccamandolfi, a short drive) — Frequently high-rated (often 9.3+), charming mountain-style property with restaurant and garden. Good base for exploring the Matese area.
  • Fonte Del Benessere Resort (Castelpetroso, ~15–20 min drive) — Higher-end wellness focus with indoor/outdoor heated pools, spa, and restaurant. Ratings typically 9.2–9.3; popular for relaxation rather than pure ski-access convenience.
  • Boutique Hotel Palazzo Corso Umberto (Bojano) — Elegant option with strong ratings (~9.4–9.6), restaurant, and more town amenities. A bit further but well-reviewed.
  • Other solid mentions: Velum House B&B, Montis Country & Charme, LE CANONICHE NEL MATESE (albergo diffuso style), or apartments/residences like those near San Nicola for groups seeking self-catering.

Quick tips

Campitello Matese is a compact high-plateau ski area, so staying on-site maximizes convenience in winter (especially with snow/road conditions). San Massimo or nearby villages (Bojano, Roccamandolfi, Castelpetroso) add more dining/services but require driving up. Check current ski conditions, parking (often free but confirm with snow), meal plans (half-board is common and practical), and exact slope distance, as “near Campitello Matese” can vary. Ratings and availability fluctuate by season—winter weekends and holidays book up fast.

For the latest prices, availability, and photos, search Booking.com, Hotels.com, or the hotels’ direct sites with your dates. Preferences for spa, family rooms, or budget can refine the choice further.
