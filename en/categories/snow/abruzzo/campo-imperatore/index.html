When choosing a place to stay in L'Aquila , the biggest decision is whether you want to be right in the historic center—surrounded by winding stone alleys and local trattorias—or tucked away on the outskirts where parking is seamless and modern amenities are guaranteed.

The Top Picks

Best Overall Experience: Hotel Castello L'Aquila (4-star). This is an exceptionally managed hotel located right by the historic Spanish Fortress (Forte Spagnolo). It strikes the perfect balance between central convenience and a peaceful neighborhood vibe. The staff goes above and beyond, and the rooms feature beautiful linens and excellent small touches like dedicated herbal tea kits.

Best for Historic Charm: Palazzo dei Pavoni, Relais di Charme (4-star). Nestled right in the heart of town near historic landmarks, this beautifully restored palace functions as an upscale bed and breakfast. Staying here feels incredibly exclusive, and they even offer senior suites with fully equipped living spaces and standard in-room breakfast service.

Best Luxury B&B: Residenza San Flaviano, Relais di Charme (Unrated/B&B style). If you want a flawless, intimate boutique experience, this is the highest-rated spot in the city center. It’s perfect for couples looking for an elegant, quiet base to explore on foot.

Best Full-Service Classic Hotel: Hotel L'Aquila (4-star). Housed in a grand 18th-century building right in the city center, this hotel offers spacious rooms and an on-site restaurant. It provides that old-school European hotel atmosphere, though some areas like the carpets show a bit of wear.

Best for a Spa Break (Out of Center): Hotel La Dimora del Baco (4-star). Located about a 14-minute drive from the city center, this modern property is perfect if you are traveling by car and want free parking, a peaceful garden setting, an excellent traditional restaurant, and a full-service spa.

Other Solid Options

  • Smaller highly rated B&Bs or residences (e.g., Locanda di Posta or central apartments) can score even higher in guest feedback if you prefer a more intimate stay.

Quick Tips for Choosing

  • Best for historic center / walkability → Hotel Castello or Dimora Fortebraccio.
  • Best for relaxation + amenities (pool, space, quieter) → La Compagnia Del Viaggiatore or La Dimora del Baco.
  • Modern convenience + good value near transport → My Suite Hotel.
  • L’Aquila is relatively compact; many hotels offer free parking, which is useful given the historic center’s limited spots. The city has good access to Gran Sasso National Park for day trips.

Prices typically start around €70–150+ per night depending on season, room type, and how far ahead you book (book earlier for better rates).
