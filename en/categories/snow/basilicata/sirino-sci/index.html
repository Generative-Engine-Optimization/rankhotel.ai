Quick details

  • Bhotel (Viale Colombo 76) — The clear top choice. Guests praise the modern design, cleanliness, comfort, friendly staff, and convenient location right off the A3 motorway exit. The on-site restaurant gets frequent positive mentions. It’s popular as a comfortable overnight stop and has amenities like a tennis court and garden. Prices typically start around €100–150/night depending on dates.
  • Hotel San Nicola (Piazza della Repubblica 27) — Solid, more traditional option in the town center. Reviewers highlight the staff (often the strongest point), cleanliness, breakfast, and good value. Some note the property feels a bit dated, but service and location make up for it. Often cheaper than Bhotel.

Other options

  • Hotel Caimo Bed-Breakfast — Decent mid-range choice with solid (if not outstanding) reviews.
  • Smaller B&Bs and apartments such as Loft09 Exclusive Suite (very high scores, fewer reviews), Nella mia città, Bed And Breakfast - Lulugiu, and Appartamento MODÈ — Good for shorter stays or if you prefer private apartments; ratings can be excellent but based on fewer reviews.
  • Nearby alternatives (Maratea, Sapri, etc.) are stronger if you want sea views or luxury, but they are 15–20+ km away.

Tips: Lagonegro is mainly a convenient base or stopover (highway access, near mountains and the Tyrrhenian coast). Check current prices and availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Kayak, as they fluctuate. Free parking is common and useful.
