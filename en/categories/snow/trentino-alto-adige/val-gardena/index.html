If you’re looking for top-end hotels in Val Gardena, I’d focus on these. The valley’s strongest luxury options are concentrated in Ortisei and Selva di Val Gardena, with Santa Cristina offering a quieter middle ground.

My shortlist

  • Gardena Grödnerhof Hotel & Spa - Ortisei
    One of the valley’s standout luxury properties, especially if food and wellness matter. Its Anna Stuben restaurant retained a Michelin star in the 2026 guide.

  • Hotel Granbaita Dolomites - Selva di Val Gardena
    Excellent all-around luxury choice with a strong spa/wellness offering and mountain setting. Booking.com currently lists it at 9.3/10, while Tripadvisor lists it at 4.8/5.

  • Hotel Tyrol - Selva di Val Gardena
    Particularly appealing for food lovers: its Suinsom restaurant has a Michelin star in the 2026 guide.

  • Alpenroyal Hotel - Selva di Val Gardena
    A classic luxury option with an extensive spa and highly regarded restaurant; its restaurant also retains a Michelin star.

  • GRANVARA® Relais & SPA Hotel - Selva di Val Gardena
    A newer-generation luxury/spa choice. It is one of Val Gardena's 5-star hotels and is particularly suited to a relaxing, high-end stay.

  • Hotel Montchalet - Ortisei
    A smaller, more intimate luxury property. Booking.com currently gives it 9.6/10, with particularly strong comments about its facilities and service.

  • ADLER Dolomiti - Ortisei
    A very established resort-style choice with extensive spa/pool facilities and an exceptionally large volume of positive guest reviews.

Which town?

Ortisei: best if you want a beautiful town atmosphere, restaurants and shops, and easy access to the Seceda/Alpe di Siusi areas.

Selva: better suited if skiing and immediate access to the Sella Ronda are priorities; it also has a particularly strong concentration of luxury hotels.

Santa Cristina: quieter and more intimate, a good compromise if you don't want the busier feel of Ortisei or Selva.

⚠️ Trade-offs & Risks

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates rise sharply in winter ski season and summer hiking months. Booking early is essential.

  • Location differences: Ortisei hotels are closer to cultural attractions, while Selva and Santa Cristina are better for ski access.

Gemini

If you are planning a trip to the stunning Dolomites, Val Gardena offers some of the most luxurious, wellness-focused mountain lodging in Europe. To choose the best hotel, it helps to understand the valley’s three distinct villages: Ortisei (charming, pedestrian-friendly, great lift access), Santa Cristina (quiet, central, great views), and Selva (best for high-altitude ski-in/ski-out and intense hiking).

The absolute best hotels in the region excel at combining South Tyrolean alpine charm with world-class spa cultures ("wellness worlds") and fine dining.

The Top Picks in Val Gardena

The Vibe: Relais & Châteaux elegance steps from the Alpe di Siusi lift.

Why it's a top pick: Run beautifully by the Bernardi family, Gardena Grödnerhof is a masterpiece of upscale comfort. Its standout feature is an otherworldly spa zone complete with zero-gravity relaxation beds, multiple saunas, and cold plunges. It also boasts incredible dining and thoughtful touches like an extensive pillow menu.

The Vibe: An expansive, historic 5-star resort in the absolute center of town.

Why it's a top pick: If you want a resort that handles everything for you, ADLER Dolomiti is unmatched. They offer fantastic, complimentary daily outdoor programs like guided trekking and e-bike tours. The sprawling indoor/outdoor water world and park area make it feel like an oasis right in the middle of Ortisei.

Note: For an intimate, child-free alternative, their sister property next door, ADLER Spa Resort Balance , is an incredible adults-only wellness retreat.

The Vibe: Ultra-exclusive, sleek, and modern all-suite boutique luxury.

Why it's a top pick: A departure from traditional heavy wood alpine styling, Hotel Montchalet leans into glamorous, contemporary design and eclectic decor. It offers a quiet, highly personalized sanctuary where the staff anticipates your every need, making it a favorite for honeymooners or special occasions.

The Vibe: Grand alpine luxury surrounded by massive mountain lawns.

Why it's a top pick: As a member of the Leading Hotels of the World, Alpenroyal Hotel offers sweeping views of the Sassolungo peak. It is highly peaceful, featuring live piano music in the evenings, a gourmet Michelin-vibe culinary experience, and massive, beautifully manicured grounds.

The Vibe: High-end boutique charm with a deep focus on Ladin traditions.

Why it's a top pick: For a more intimate, rustic-chic atmosphere, Dorfhotel Beludei is exceptional. Located in the quieter village of Santa Cristina, it offers fantastic panoramic views, highly rated fine dining, and a free private ski shuttle to get you to the slopes smoothly.

Excellent 4-Star / High-Value Options

  • Hotel Angelo Engel (Ortisei) — Frequently ranks near the top on TripAdvisor for location (near lifts/town), indoor/outdoor pool, spa, dining, and guided activities. Strong recent reviews.
  • Alpenhotel Plaza (Santa Cristina) — Central, walkable to lifts, rooftop heated infinity pool with Dolomites views; often recommended for first-timers.
  • Hotel Sureghes (Ortisei) — Outstanding food and staff at a strong value; excellent base without the highest price.
  • Luna Mondschein (Ortisei) — Good spa (including hay sauna) and half-board value.
  • Hotel Laurin Small & Charming or Hotel Continental Dolomites / Freina Mountain Lifestyle Hotel (Selva) — High guest scores for spa, location near slopes, and service.

Quick Tips by Preference

  • First visit / walkability → Ortisei (easiest town access + lifts) — start with Alpenhotel Plaza, Sureghes, or Angelo Engel.
  • Ski focus / slopes → Selva (closest to Sella Ronda) or Santa Cristina (Saslong area); look for ski-in/ski-out or shuttle options like Granbaita, Continental, or Cendevaves.
  • Pure luxury/spa → Gardena Grödnerhof or ADLER Dolomiti.
  • Value → Sureghes, Luna Mondschein, or well-rated 3–4 star garnis (book early in peak ski/summer seasons).
  • Family → Properties like Cavallino Bianco are often suggested in guides.

Val Gardena hotels frequently include half-board (breakfast + dinner), excellent wellness areas (saunas, pools with mountain views), and alpine-modern design. Book well ahead for winter (especially Christmas–Easter) or peak summer hiking season, as the best places fill quickly. Check current availability and exact rates on Booking, the hotel sites, or TripAdvisor, and confirm ski/hike access for your dates. Enjoy the Dolomites!
