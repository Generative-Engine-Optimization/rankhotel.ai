We asked

«what are the best hotels in Passo Rolle?»

ChatGPT · gpt-5-6

If you mean staying right at Passo Rolle itself, rather than in nearby San Martino di Castrozza, the choice is fairly small. The official local tourism directory lists several accommodations at the pass.

My shortlist

  • Albergo Vezzana - my pick for staying directly at the pass. It sits at about 2,000 m, directly opposite the Pale di San Martino, with hiking trails starting from the hotel and ski access in winter. It also has an on-site restaurant, sauna, garage and Wi-Fi.
  • Hotel Venezia - Another 3-star option directly at Passo Rolle, with 26 rooms. Good if you want a conventional hotel rather than a mountain-refuge experience.
  • Ristorante Rifugio Capanna Cervino - More of a mountain-refuge experience than a traditional hotel. It's directly on the slopes and surrounded by the Pale di San Martino; the official directory lists 9 rooms/24 beds.
  • Agritur Malga Juribello - A particularly rustic option, open seasonally, with accommodation in a working mountain dairy farm. It's reached on foot in roughly 30 minutes from Passo Rolle.

If you're willing to stay 10-20 minutes down the road in San Martino di Castrozza, the selection becomes much better. For example, Romantik Hotel Regina, Hotel Colbricon Beauty & Relax, and G. Hotel Des Alpes (Classic since 1912) are established options along Via Passo Rolle.

For a trip focused on the scenery and hiking, I'd look first at Albergo Vezzana. Passo Rolle is exceptionally well positioned for the Pale di San Martino, Val Venegia and the Colbricon lakes.
