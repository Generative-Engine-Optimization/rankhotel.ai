If you mean Artesina in Piedmont, the accommodation choice is a little unusual: there are relatively few traditional hotels directly in the village, so some of the strongest options are in nearby Prato Nevoso, Frabosa Soprana, and the surrounding valleys.

Here are the places I’d look at first:

  • Hotel Marguareis Srl - In the Artesina area itself and convenient for skiing. A straightforward choice if being close to the slopes is your priority.
  • Grand Hotel Mondolè - In Prato Nevoso, part of the same Mondolè ski area. A good option if you want more hotel infrastructure and resort amenities.
  • Hotel Mondolè - One of the more ski-focused choices around Prato Nevoso; a ski-tour operator describes it as adjacent to a blue piste with doorstep skiing and recently refurbished rooms.
  • Chalet Everest Prato Nevoso - A chalet-style alternative in Prato Nevoso if you'd prefer something less like a conventional large hotel.
  • Duchessa Margherita Chateau & Hotel - In Vicoforte rather than up at the resort, but an attractive choice if you care more about a stylish hotel and are happy to drive to the slopes.
  • Albergo della Ceramica - In Villanova Mondovì; another well-reviewed valley option if you're combining skiing with sightseeing around Mondovì/Vicoforte.

For ski-in/ski-out convenience, I'd concentrate on Artesina/Prato Nevoso itself rather than the valley hotels. The Mondolè ski area encompasses Artesina, Prato Nevoso and Frabosa Soprana.

When choosing where to stay for a trip to Artesina, your best option depends heavily on whether you prioritize staying directly on the slopes or are willing to drive a short distance for more upscale amenities. Artesina itself is a dedicated, functional ski village within the Mondolè Ski district, meaning the accommodations right on the slopes lean toward basic, alpine-style lodging. Many winter travelers opt to stay in the livelier neighboring resort of Prato Nevoso (connected by the ski slopes) or boutique properties down in the valley.

🏔️ Staying Directly in Artesina

If your primary goal is stepping out of bed and straight onto the ski lifts, your main choice right in the village is the slope-side option.

The Vibe: A no-frills, traditional alpine lodge whose main selling point is its unbeatable ski-in/ski-out access. What to Expect: Reviewers frequently note that while the location is absolutely perfect for skiers, the interior amenities are dated and room maintenance can be inconsistent. It features an on-site restaurant, bar, small gym, and spa. Insider Tip: Request a room on a higher floor with a mountain view to enjoy the morning sun hitting the peaks.

🏂 Staying in Connected Prato Nevoso

Just a short drive or a ski lift ride away, the neighboring resort village of Prato Nevoso offers a wider variety of modern accommodations, restaurants, and apres-ski nightlife.

The Vibe: Modern, upscale alpine apartments perfect for families or groups wanting a high-quality self-catering setup. What to Expect: This is a highly-rated, brand-new facility offering clean, quiet, and beautifully decorated chalets with gorgeous mountain vistas. Insider Tip: Because these are apartments, pack a few essentials like dish towels and extra toiletries, as amenities are sparse upon arrival.
