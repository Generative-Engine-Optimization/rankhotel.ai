Monte Cimone (often just called Cimone) is the main ski resort in the Northern Apennines (Emilia-Romagna, Italy), centered around villages like Sestola, Fanano, Riolunato, Montecreto, and Fiumalbo. Accommodation is mostly mid-range hotels, agriturismi, residences, and apartments rather than large luxury chains. Options are concentrated at the base of the slopes or a short drive away.

Here are some of the consistently highly rated places based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, and similar sites (ratings and availability fluctuate seasonally, especially in winter):

Top recommendations near the slopes

  • Il Borgo dei Celti Agriturismo (Fiumalbo / Doccia del Cimone) — Frequently ranked among the closest and best-value options (roughly 2–3 km from Monte Cimone). Restored medieval-style mountain apartments with parquet floors, wooden beams, fully equipped kitchens, mountain views, free parking, restaurant serving local Tuscan-Emilian cuisine, garden with kids’ play area, outdoor sauna/wellness (wood-heated pool or similar), and panoramic terrace with BBQ. Pets often allowed. Strong praise for views, cleanliness, hospitality, and authentic atmosphere. Tripadvisor ~4.5/5.

  • Locanda Zita (Sestola / Vesale area) — Highly regarded small hotel/inn (~8–10 km from the main slopes, short drive). Excellent restaurant focused on authentic mountain cuisine (homemade pasta, porcini mushrooms, local specialties), spa elements (sauna, hot tub/jacuzzi), free parking, and friendly service. Guests frequently call it a “hidden gem” with outstanding food and rooms; Booking scores around 8.7–9+ and Tripadvisor ~4.5/5. Great for food-focused stays.

  • Hotel Gabriella (Fanano) — Solid mid-range choice with restaurant, garden, terrace, and spa facilities. Good location for the slopes, friendly staff, and solid value; often listed among top-rated nearby hotels.

  • Residence Cimone SuperSci Polle-life (Riolunato / Le Polle area) — One of the closest options to the lifts (around 2–3 km). Aparthotel-style with practical ski access; useful for self-catering and families.

  • Hotel San Marco Sestola — Popular in Sestola with good access to lifts/golf club area, friendly staff, and solid breakfast. Frequently booked for its convenient location.

Other strong nearby options

  • Val di Luce SPA Resort (Abetone / Val di Luce, ~8–9 km / short drive) — More upscale 4-star design hotel with full spa (heated pools with mountain views, sauna, Turkish bath, treatments), restaurant, ski storage/school access, and modern rooms (some with fireplaces). Ideal if you want wellness + skiing; Booking scores around 8.6 with strong location marks. Note it is technically on the Abetone/Val di Luce side of the broader area.
  • Rifugio Hotel Calvanella (Sestola) — Garden, terrace, restaurant/bar; convenient and well-reviewed for a more traditional mountain feel.
  • Hotel Le Polle (Riolunato) — Close to the slopes for easy ski access.

Practical tips

  • Closest to slopes: Look toward Riolunato/Le Polle, Fiumalbo/Doccia del Cimone, or Passo del Lupo/Pian del Falco areas for minimal transfer times. Sestola and Fanano are slightly farther but have more amenities and restaurants.
  • Style of stay: Expect family-run hotels, agriturismi, and residences more than big resorts. Many offer free parking, Wi-Fi, and mountain views. Spa/wellness and good food (local specialties like tigelle, porcini, tortellini) are common highlights.
  • When to book: Winter (ski season) fills up; shoulder seasons (late spring/early autumn) or summer for hiking are quieter and often cheaper. Check current snow/lift status and road conditions (mountain roads can be winding).
  • Alternatives: Apartments and B&Bs are plentiful if you prefer self-catering. For more luxury or spa focus, Val di Luce is the standout in the wider area.

For the latest prices, availability, exact distances, and reviews, check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the official Cimone tourism site, as conditions change with the seasons.
