Forni di Sopra (in the Friulian Dolomites / Carnic Alps, Province of Udine, Italy) is a small mountain village popular for hiking, skiing, and nature. It has a limited selection of hotels and accommodations, mostly 3-star or similar, family-run properties focused on mountain views, hospitality, and outdoor activities rather than luxury resorts.

Top-rated options (based on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Kayak, Trivago, and similar sites)

These stand out for guest ratings, review volume, and consistent praise for location, staff, cleanliness, and views:

  1. Hotel Posta (often ranked #1 on Tripadvisor)

    • ~4.7/5 (200+ Tripadvisor reviews); strong Booking scores (around 9.2–9.3 where listed).
    • Central location with mountain/valley views. Praised for friendly/helpful staff (especially owners), spacious clean rooms, excellent food (restaurant and breakfast), outdoor pool/hot tub/sauna, garden, and good value. Facilities include a playground and amenities suited to hikers/skiers.
    • Ideal for those wanting a classic mountain-hotel experience with on-site dining.

  2. Residence Dolomiti

    • Exceptional ratings: ~9.7/10 on Booking (30+ reviews) and similarly high on other platforms.
    • Apartment-style stay near the center with spa facilities (steam room, sauna, beauty services), garden, terrace/balcony, kitchenette, free parking, and mountain views. Guests highlight friendliness, comfort, and amenities.
    • Great for couples or small groups wanting more independence + wellness.

  3. Hotel Due Cime

    • Strong scores: ~8.7–9.1/10 across sites; ~4.5/5 on Tripadvisor.
    • Central location, free Wi-Fi, pet-friendly options. Frequently listed among top hotels for cleanliness, modern updates, and convenience.

  4. Villa Alpina Hotel

    • ~8.7–8.9 ratings.
    • Central, parking, free Wi-Fi, pet-friendly. Popular for solid value and mountain setting.

  5. Albergo Centrale / Albergo Meuble Tarandan / Albergo Nuoitas

    • Consistently 8.5–9.0 range (or ~4.5/5 on Tripadvisor).
    • Centrale: central, friendly staff, good breakfast/views.
    • Tarandan: rustic charm, panoramic views.
    • Nuoitas: chalet-style, family-friendly (slightly outside center), good for winter activities.

Hotel Davost is a popular, more budget-oriented choice (~8.0–8.1 with hundreds of reviews), known for panoramic views, traditional breakfast, parking, and central access—solid if you prioritize volume of feedback and value over the absolute highest scores.

Quick tips

  • Season matters: High season is typically winter (skiing) and summer/fall (hiking); shoulder months (e.g., June) can be cheaper. Average night prices often range from roughly €80–€250+ depending on dates and type.
  • Most properties emphasize mountain views, free parking/Wi-Fi, and proximity to trails or slopes. Spa/wellness is more limited (strongest at places like Residence Dolomiti or Hotel Posta).
  • Check recent reviews on Tripadvisor or Booking for your specific dates, as small hotels can vary with occupancy/season. Many are family-run and highly rated for hospitality.
  • Alternatives include apartments/private rentals (e.g., Borgo Nuovo Deluxe or similar high-rated options) if you prefer self-catering.

For the latest availability, prices, and exact reviews, search Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Kayak directly with your travel dates—Forni di Sopra is compact, so most good options are walkable or a short drive from the center and key outdoor spots.
