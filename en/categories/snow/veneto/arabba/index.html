🔑 Key Considerations

  • Location: Blizzard Apartments are right in Arabba, ideal for ski access. Hotels like Gardena Grödnerhof and Granbaita are in Val Gardena, a short drive but with more luxury options.
  • Atmosphere: Choose Blizzard Apartments for independence, Granbaita Dolomites for a classic alpine hotel feel, or Gardena Grödnerhof for refined luxury.

⚠️ Risks & Trade-offs

  • High-season demand: Arabba is part of the Sellaronda ski circuit, so hotels book out quickly in winter. Advance booking is essential.
  • Price differences: Luxury hotels (Ciasa Salares, Aman Rosa Alpina) are significantly more expensive than local B&Bs or apartments.
  • Location trade-off: Staying in Val Gardena or San Cassiano offers more amenities but requires driving to Arabba’s ski lifts.

Grok · grok-3

Arabba (in the Dolomites, Italy, part of the Arabba-Marmolada ski area and Sella Ronda circuit) is a compact, authentic alpine village with strong skiing and hiking appeal. The best hotels are mostly 3- and 4-star properties focused on location near lifts, good food, wellness facilities, and mountain views rather than ultra-luxury 5-star resorts (those are more common in nearby Corvara or Alta Badia).

Here are some of the consistently highest-rated and most recommended options based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites:

Top Picks

  • Hotel Evaldo (4-star)
    Frequently ranks among the highest-rated. Central/quiet location with easy access to lifts (shuttle often available), excellent food (local and regional cuisine), strong spa/wellness (indoor pool, multiple saunas), spacious clean rooms, and very friendly family-run service. Guest scores often around 9.4–9.5/10 on Booking and ~4.8/5 on Tripadvisor. Praised for comfort, cleanliness, and overall hospitality.

  • Sporthotel Arabba (4-star)
    Excellent ski-to-door / near-lift access (Burz slopes), panoramic views, good restaurant with local/Veneto cuisine and solid wine selection, hot tub/spa facilities, and spacious rooms. Strong ratings (~9.2/10 on Booking, high Tripadvisor scores). Guests highlight location for skiing/hiking, staff, food, and cleanliness. A popular choice for both winter sports and summer activities.

  • Hotel Alpenrose
    Very close to the Burz ski elevators (short walk), mountain views from rooms, strong dining (including a Michelin-listed Stube Ladina restaurant noted in some sources), wellness options, and family-run hospitality. High scores (~9.3–9.4/10 on Booking, ~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor with hundreds of reviews). Frequently praised for food quality/variety, staff, cleanliness, and location for skiing or cycling/hiking.

  • Grifone Dolomiti Resort (also called Hotel Grifone)
    Often tops Tripadvisor traveler rankings for the area. Good facilities including spa/indoor pool, restaurant with extensive wine list, and solid service. Convenient for the Sella Ronda and nearby lifts (near Campolongo Pass area). Strong overall reviews (~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor).

Other Strong Contenders

  • Dolomites Base Camp Hotel Olympia — Comfortable rooms with views, spa/wellness (sauna, steam room, outdoor bath), restaurant, and good location. Scores around 9.1/10.
  • Hotel Pordoi – Fam. Furgler — Central, family-run, well-regarded for hospitality and value.
  • Apartments such as Apartments Mirabell or residences like Residence Aspen score very highly for self-catering options (spacious, clean, well-equipped, great views/location), though they are not full-service hotels.

Tips

  • Location matters for skiing: Prioritize properties near the Burz lifts or with ski storage + shuttle for the easiest access to the Arabba-Marmolada and Sella Ronda terrain.
  • Many top hotels emphasize half-board (breakfast + dinner) with local Ladin/Italian cuisine, wellness areas, free parking, and mountain views.
  • Ratings can vary slightly by season and platform; always check recent reviews and current availability/prices on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the official Arabba tourism site, as Arabba is smaller and books up during peak ski season.
  • Nearby alternatives in Corvara or Alta Badia offer more luxury options if you’re open to a short transfer while still accessing the same ski area.

For the most up-to-date rates and availability, search directly on Booking.com or Tripadvisor with your travel dates, as prices fluctuate significantly between summer hiking and winter ski seasons.
