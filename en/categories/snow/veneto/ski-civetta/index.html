⚠️ Practical Considerations

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates rise significantly in winter (ski season) and summer (hiking season).

  • Ski access: Alleghe is part of the Civetta ski area, so proximity to lifts is key.
  • Booking early: Essential for December–March, especially around holidays.
