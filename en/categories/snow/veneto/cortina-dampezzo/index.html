Grok · grok-3

The best hotels in Cortina d’Ampezzo (the “Queen of the Dolomites”) mix historic grande-dame properties, newly renovated or recently opened luxury spots, and stylish spa-focused stays. The area is gearing up for the 2026 Winter Olympics, which has driven renovations and new openings, so options are strong across luxury, boutique, and spa categories. Prices are high, especially in peak ski season, and many properties emphasize views, wellness, and alpine design.

Here are standout recommendations based on recent guides (Telegraph, CN Traveller, Hotel Guru, GQ), guest ratings (Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Hotels.com often 9.0+), and expert coverage:

Top Luxury & Historic Standouts

  • Grand Hotel Savoia (Radisson Collection) — Frequently ranked among the very best. This 1912 belle-époque property sits near the center with elegant interiors, attentive service, a polished spa, indoor pool, and strong Dolomite views. It’s a classic “grande dame” with modern updates and good facilities for groups or events.
  • Hotel Ancora (Leading Hotels of the World) — One of Cortina’s oldest (founded 1826), fully reopened/renovated in 2025 under Renzo Rosso (OTB/Diesel group) with design by Vicky Charles (ex-Soho House). Intimate, characterful rooms (around 35–38, all different, many with mountain views), spa (~200 m²), restaurant focused on local/sustainable ingredients, terrace on Corso Italia, and the private Brave Club. Feels like a stylish alpine home rather than a formal hotel.
  • Cristallo (soon Mandarin Oriental Cristallo) — The original historic luxury landmark (Art Nouveau, 1901), long a celebrity haunt. Currently under major renovation (Herzog & de Meuron involved) and expected to reopen as Mandarin Oriental’s first alpine resort around early 2027 (timelines have shifted). Expect high-end rooms/suites with views, extensive spa with indoor-outdoor pool, dining, and full resort amenities once open. Check current status if planning near-term travel.

Excellent Newer or Spa-Focused Options

  • Hotel Tofana Cortina — New 5-star (opened for the 2024/25 season) in the Pocol area, a short distance from town toward the Falzarego Pass. Strong guest scores (often 9.5–10/10), ski-in/ski-out access or proximity, excellent spa (multiple saunas including Aufguss, pool, hot tub), high-quality half-board dining, and shuttle to the center. Quiet, modern alpine luxury with outstanding service and food feedback.
  • Rosapetra Spa Resort — Contemporary chalet-style with blonde wood, glass, and beams; standout spa, indoor pool, gym, and Dolomite views from rooms. Secluded yet convenient (shuttle available); popular for relaxing ski or summer stays and frequently recommended by Condé Nast Traveler and others.
  • Hotel de LEN — Modern, sustainable “Hotel of Wood” (Ladin name) in the town center (sister property vibe to Borgo Egnazia). Timber façade and warm Nordic-minimal interiors, rooftop spa with panoramic views, outdoor hot tub, emotional showers/steam, and good dining. Boutique scale (~22 rooms); excellent for central location + design-focused wellness.
  • Faloria Mountain Spa Resort — Updated historic property with contemporary design, large spa, impressive pool, and valley/Dolomite views. Good for wellness-focused trips; martinis and après atmosphere praised.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Eight Cortina (former Hotel Europa) — Recently renovated/reopened by the local family; contemporary luxury near the Faloria lift, strong breakfast with views, and central energy.
  • Grand Hotel Ampezzo — Historic property that has undergone renewal and soft openings; central location near Faloria.
  • Hotel de la Poste — Classic four-star with old-world elegance, wood-paneled bar, and rich history—great atmosphere in the heart of town.
  • Ambra Cortina or Camina Suite and Spa — Boutique/chalet-style options often praised for charm, location, or modern comfort and high guest scores.

Tips:
Cortina is walkable in the center (Corso Italia), with ski buses and shuttles common. For skiing, check proximity to lifts (Faloria, Tofana, etc.). Summer is excellent for hiking and milder rates. Book early for peak periods (Christmas, February, Olympics-related dates). Many top properties offer half-board, excellent breakfasts, and spas—worth prioritizing if you want the full experience. Ratings and availability shift, so cross-check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or official sites for your dates.
