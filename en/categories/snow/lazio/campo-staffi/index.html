If you mean Filettino, Lazio, the selection is fairly small-there are only a couple of conventional hotels, plus several highly rated mountain apartments/B&Bs. Current listings suggest these are the main options to consider:

In Filettino

  • Hotel Monte Viglio - the clearest traditional-hotel choice in town. It has a 4.3/5 rating from 35 reviews on the current local listing, and Tripadvisor currently lists it among Filettino's principal accommodations.
  • Casetta di montagna - technically an apartment rather than a hotel, but currently rated 9.9/10 from 11 reviews on Booking.com, with mountain views and a balcony.
  • A casa di Olly - another apartment-style stay near the center, rated 8.4/10 from 18 reviews; reviews particularly mention the convenient location and welcoming hosts.
  • Sorgente dell'Aniene - a more basic apartment option, currently rated 7.3/10.

If you're willing to stay nearby

For a more upscale hotel experience, Fiuggi is worth considering, roughly a drive away from Filettino. Options include Palazzo Fiuggi, Silva Hotel Splendid, and Ambasciatori Place Hotel. These offer considerably more in the way of spa/wellness facilities than the properties in Filettino itself.

For a stay actually in Filettino, I'd focus on Hotel Monte Viglio if you specifically want a hotel, or Casetta di montagna if you're open to a private accommodation. The latter has the strongest current guest score, although it's based on only 11 reviews.

Nearby alternatives (often recommended when searching “Filettino hotels”)

Because Filettino is tiny, many listings pull in places 10–15 km away:

  • Civitella Roveto area (closer, ~9–10 km): Albergo Rio (strong ratings), Hotel River, La dimora nelle Rue, Mariella Vernile (high 9+ scores).
  • Fiuggi (~14 km): More full-service options with spas and amenities, such as Hotel San Giorgio (Heaven Spa), Silva Hotel Splendid, Ambasciatori Place Hotel. These have hundreds/thousands of reviews and higher comfort levels if you prefer hotel amenities over pure mountain seclusion.

Quick tips

  • Best for pure Filettino experience — Prioritize the top apartments/chalets (especially Casetta di montagna or the exclusive chalet) for location, views, and guest scores. Ideal for hiking, skiing at Campo Staffi, or quiet nature stays.
  • Book ahead — Limited inventory, especially in peak ski or summer seasons. Check current availability and exact prices on Booking.com or similar, as they vary by dates.
  • What to expect — Mountain simplicity: free parking is common, many places are pet-friendly, and self-catering is the norm outside the hotel. No large luxury resorts right in town.
  • Getting there — Car is strongly recommended (Rome Ciampino is ~60–90 minutes away depending on traffic).
