ChatGPT · gpt-5-6

If by “best” you mean the strongest combination of guest reviews, location, and amenities, these are the places I’d look at around Monte Livata. Note that Monte Livata has relatively few traditional hotels; many of the highest-rated options are apartments, cabins, and mountain retreats.

My shortlist

  • Hotel Livata Mountain Wellness - best for a hotel + wellness experience. It’s a 4-star property in Livata, with restaurant, bar, garden and wellness facilities. Current Booking.com guest score is 9.0/10 from 263 reviews, and recent 2026 Tripadvisor reviews praise the renovated rooms, cleanliness, breakfast, restaurant and spa.
  • Casa nel Bosco - best for a private mountain stay. Located right in the Campo dell'Osso/Monte Livata area, it currently scores 9.6/10 from 52 reviews. It sounds particularly suitable if you want a forest setting and more space than a conventional hotel.
  • Livata - Campo dell'Osso…nel bosco a 1.700 metri - best for being immersed in nature. It has a 9.4/10 score from 90 reviews, and guest comments specifically mention the recent renovation, good equipment, fireplace/firewood and garage.
  • Rifugio La Veranda - good small, cozy option. It has a 9.3/10 score, although that's based on only 7 reviews, so the rating is less established than the larger properties.
  • Rifugio della Bandita - good rustic mountain-retreat choice. It's actually in Livata and currently scores 8.4/10, though based on only 5 reviews.
  • Campaegli Resort - worth considering if you don't mind staying a little farther away. It's about 6 km from Monte Livata and has an 8.6/10 score from 183 reviews, with a restaurant, bar, garden and fitness center.

For a couple's getaway, I'd particularly look at Hotel Livata Mountain Wellness if you want comfort and spa facilities, or Casa nel Bosco if you want a more secluded woodland experience.

Hotel Livata Mountain & Wellness (also called Hotel Livata Mountain & Wellness) is widely regarded as the top hotel in Monte Livata itself.

Hotel Livata Mountain & Wellness

  • Location: Viale dei Boschi 28, right in Livata (Monte Livata area, Subiaco).
  • Type: Modern 4-star hotel (recently renovated/reopened).
  • Ratings: Typically 9.0 on Booking.com (250+ reviews), 4.7–4.8 on Google, and strong Tripadvisor scores.
  • Highlights: Spa with steam room/hammam, massages and treatments; indoor pool; restaurant and bar; comfortable, spacious modern rooms (many with balconies or mountain views); excellent breakfast; friendly staff; free WiFi and parking.
  • Why it’s the best for most people: It’s the main full-service hotel on the mountain, convenient for skiing (near Piste Monna dell’Orso and other slopes), hiking, and outdoor activities. Guests consistently praise the cleanliness, comfort, food, and the “oasis” feel in the mountains. Ideal for couples, families, and wellness stays.

Quick tips

  • Monte Livata is a small mountain resort (ski in winter, hiking/nature in summer/fall) about 1 hour east of Rome in the Simbruini Mountains. On-mountain options are limited, so Hotel Livata Mountain & Wellness stands out clearly for full hotel services.
  • Book early in peak ski season or summer weekends.
  • Check current rates and availability on Booking.com, the hotel’s own site (livatahotel.it), or major platforms, as prices vary by season.
  • For a more authentic countryside experience with excellent food, choose Agriturismo Colle Tocci.
