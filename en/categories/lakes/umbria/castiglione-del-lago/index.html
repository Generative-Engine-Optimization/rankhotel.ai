Other Strong Contenders

  • Hotel Trasimeno (Bittarelli) — Solid mid-range option with outdoor pool, roughly 15-minute walk from the center; good value and practical amenities.
  • Hotel Aganoor and Hotel Duca della Corgna — Reliable choices in or near the center/garden settings; frequently appear in top lists with solid ratings (typically 8.2–8.7+).
  • Agriturismi and smaller properties (e.g., Morami Wine Agriturismo or various farm stays) score highly for those seeking a more rural, wine-country experience.

Quick Tips

  • Location preference: Stay in the historic center (Boutique, La Torre, Aganoor) for walkability to the Rocca del Leone fortress, Palazzo della Corgna, restaurants, and lake views. Choose lakeside/countryside spots (Le Macerine or Poggioleone) for pools and quieter surroundings.
  • Prices: Expect roughly €100–€200+ per night depending on season, room type, and dates (higher in summer). Always check current rates on Booking.com or similar, as they fluctuate.
  • Best overall for most visitors: Hotel Boutique Castiglione del Lago for its combination of high ratings, location, service, and character.
  • Book in advance for peak summer (especially July–August) around Lake Trasimeno. Many properties are pet-friendly and offer free Wi-Fi.
