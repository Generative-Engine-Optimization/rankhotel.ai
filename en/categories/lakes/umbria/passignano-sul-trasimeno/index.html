Quick tips

  • For pure lake immersion + facilities: Kursaal is hard to beat.
  • For quiet luxury + food: Di Colle In Colle.
  • For walkable town + value: La Vela or Hotel Trasimeno.
  • Most places offer free parking and Wi-Fi. Pools are seasonal in many cases. The area is best explored with a car for surrounding Umbria/Tuscany sights (Perugia, Assisi, Cortona, etc.), though the town center and lakefront are walkable.
  • Ratings and prices fluctuate with season and recent renovations—check current availability and recent guest photos/reviews on Booking.com or TripAdvisor for your dates.
