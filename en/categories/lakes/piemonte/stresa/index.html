For a classic, lakeside escape on Italy's Lake Maggiore, Grand Hotel Des Iles Borromees is the definitive top pick.

This legendary 5-star hotel is practically a living museum of Belle Époque opulence. It completely embodies old-world luxury with its Murano glass chandeliers, lush gardens, and a brand-new, world-class 3,000-square-meter spa.

If you prefer a sleek, contemporary atmosphere or a highly tailored boutique vibe over sprawling historic palaces, several excellent alternatives offer distinct advantages depending on your travel style.

👑 The Ultimate Grand Dame: Grand Hotel Des Iles Borromees

The Experience: Stepping inside feels like traveling back to 1863. The property features lavishly appointed rooms, formal attire requirements for dinner at Il Borromeo, and impeccably manicured grounds.

Insider Tip: Request a lake-view room to watch the sunrise over the Borromean Islands. Keep in mind that the hotel undergoes a winter closure from late November through mid-February.

🥂 The Best Views & Social Scene: Hotel la Palma

The Experience: Fully renovated to a chic, light-filled contemporary standard, this property is famous for hosting the only true 360-degree rooftop Sky Bar in Stresa. It's the absolute best vantage point in town for a sunset cocktail.

Insider Tip: You get two distinct swim environments here—the rooftop "Sky Pool" and a separate lakeside Beach Club down below.

🌹 The Romantic Hideaway: Hotel Villa e Palazzo Aminta

The Experience: Located just slightly outside the main walking core (though they offer a convenient, free town shuttle), this former mansion feels like an exclusive resort. It treats guests to a private beach, a subterranean cave spa, and traditional Italian elegance.

Insider Tip: Book a dinner table at I Mori, their intimate, 35-guest restaurant encased entirely in a wrought-iron and crystal gazebo.

Grok · grok-3

The best hotels in Stresa (on Lake Maggiore, Italy) are primarily luxurious lakeside properties with strong historic or boutique appeal, excellent views of the Borromean Islands, and high guest ratings.

Stresa is known for its grand Art Nouveau/Belle Époque hotels. Rankings draw from consistent mentions across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and specialist sites (as of 2025–2026 data). Top picks emphasize location, views, spa/pool facilities, service, and overall guest scores.

Top Luxury / 5-Star Hotels

  • Grand Hotel des Iles Borromées & Spa
    The classic icon of Stresa (opened 1863). Majestic Art Nouveau building right on the lakefront promenade, facing the Borromean Islands. Features indoor and outdoor pools, a full spa, elegant rooms/suites (many with lake views), gardens, and strong dining. High ratings (around 9.1 on Booking.com from 2,000+ reviews; 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor from 2,300+ reviews). Excellent central location near the ferry terminal and town. Noted for historic grandeur (including Hemingway associations) and attentive service.

  • Hotel Villa e Palazzo Aminta
    Opulent Belle Époque villa/hotel a short distance south of the center (shuttle available). Private lakeside garden and beach, outdoor pool with island views, luxurious spa/wellness center, and two gourmet restaurants. Rooms and suites feature classic elegance with modern comforts. Very strong ratings (4.5/5 on Tripadvisor from ~1,400 reviews; often 8.8–9+ elsewhere). Ideal for a more secluded, romantic luxury stay.

  • Boutique Hotel Stresa
    Newer high-end boutique option (opened/restored more recently) with modern luxury in a stylish setting. Outdoor pool, fitness center, restaurant, and excellent location near the center/promenade. Frequently scores very high (around 9.4–9.5 on platforms like Booking/Trip.com) for cleanliness, comfort, design, and personalized service.

Strong 4-Star Contenders

  • Hotel La Palma — Reliable lakeside favorite with excellent lake/mountain views (especially from balcony rooms), outdoor pool/terrace, modern comforts, and a good restaurant/bar. Consistently ranks near the top for value and quality (around 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor with 2,000+ reviews). Great central location.

  • Hotel Regina Palace — Historic Art Nouveau property on the lakefront with indoor/outdoor pools, spa facilities, gardens, and classic “old-school” grandeur. Solid ratings and convenient location.

Other Notable Mentions

  • Grand Hotel Bristol — Another well-regarded historic option with lake views, pool, and good service.
  • Hotel Belvedere or Hotel Verbano (on Isola dei Pescatori) — For a unique island experience with romantic atmosphere and strong food (especially Verbano). Smaller and more intimate; requires boat access.
  • Higher-rated boutique or smaller options like Sempione Boutique Hotel often appear in guest-score lists for modern comfort and location.

Quick Tips

  • Location — Most top hotels are along or near Corso Umberto I / the lakeside promenade for easy access to ferries to the Borromean Islands (Isola Bella, Isola Madre, Isola dei Pescatori), the town center, and promenades.
  • Best time & prices — Peak summer (June–August) is busiest and most expensive. Shoulder seasons (May–June or September–October) offer better value with pleasant weather. Luxury rooms commonly start from roughly €250–600+ per night depending on season, view, and dates (prices fluctuate significantly).
  • What to prioritize — Lake-view rooms with balcony, spa access, and proximity to the ferry pier. Many properties offer parking, pools, and good breakfasts.
  • Booking — Check current availability and recent reviews on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or the hotels’ sites, as ratings and amenities can shift slightly. Milan Malpensa Airport is the closest major airport (~45–60 minutes by car/train).

These properties dominate “best of” lists for their combination of setting, facilities, and guest feedback. For specific dates, budget, or preferences (e.g., pure luxury vs. better value, spa focus, or island stay), more tailored recommendations are possible.
