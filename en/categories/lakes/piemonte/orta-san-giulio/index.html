How to Choose

Gemini

For an unforgettable stay in Orta San Giulio , the absolute crown jewel is Villa Crespi , a magnificent 5-star Moorish palace featuring a renowned 3-Michelin-star restaurant run by celebrity chef Antonino Cannavacciuolo. If you prefer a more intimate lakeside boutique feel, a panoramic vineyard retreat, or a charming spot right in the historic center, the town offers several incredible alternatives that perfectly capture the tranquil magic of Lake Orta.

The Ultimate Splurge & Gastronomic Landmark

Villa Crespi (5-Star Hotel): This is one of Italy's most unique properties, built in 19th-century Moorish style complete with an authentic minaret tower and stunning arabesque interiors.

  • Why choose it: It is a bucket-list destination for food aficionados due to its legendary 3-Michelin-star cuisine. The service is highly formal and personalized.
  • Good to know: There is no on-site pool, but they provide shuttle access to a nearby private beach club. Rates easily exceed €1,000 per night.

Best Panoramic & Vineyard Retreats

La Darbia Resort (4-Star Hotel): Perched in the hills among vineyards just a few minutes drive from the center.

  • Why choose it: It features beautiful casual apartments, warm family-style hospitality, and a stunning infinity pool with sweeping views over Lake Orta and the mountains.
  • Good to know: Ideal if you are looking for a peaceful, nature-oriented base rather than being directly in the village square.

Best Lakeside & Waterfront Views

Hotel San Rocco (4-Star Hotel): A converted 16th-century monastery sitting right on the water’s edge.

  • Why choose it: It offers a premier waterfront location with an outdoor pool, a rooftop hot tub, and spectacular, direct views of the Isola San Giulio. It is only a 5-minute walk from the main piazza.
  • Good to know: The decor leans a bit traditional, but the immediate lake access and proximity to town are unmatched.

B&B Al Dom (4-Star B&B): A refined, adults-only bed and breakfast housed inside an 18th-century lakeside building.

  • Why choose it: It features a leafy waterfront garden right on the lake. The host, Massimo, is famous for his exceptional personal attention, customized breakfasts, and private boat tours.

Best Historic Center Vibe & Great Value

Locanda di Orta (4-Star Inn): A charming, upscale inn nestled right in the car-free historic heart of the village.

  • Why choose it: With just 9 relaxed rooms, it feels deeply local. The highlight is having breakfast or an evening drink on the rooftop balcony looking over the rooftops and the lake.

Hotel Ristorante la Bussola (3-Star Hotel): A relaxed hotel situated slightly elevated above the town.

  • Why choose it: Consistently rated as the best value option in the area. It features a beautiful outdoor swimming pool, easy private parking, and a massive panoramic terrace looking out to the lake. It is an easy 15-minute walk down into the historic center.

The top hotels in Orta San Giulio (on Lake Orta, Piedmont, Italy) are consistently led by luxury and lakeside properties, based on recent ratings from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Trip.com, Expedia, and similar sites (as of 2026).

Orta San Giulio is a small, charming medieval lakeside village, so options are limited (mostly smaller hotels, B&Bs, and agriturismi). Here are the standout choices:

1. Relais & Châteaux Villa Crespi (5-star luxury standout)

  • Ratings: Exceptional, typically 9.3–9.6/10 (Booking, Expedia, etc.); ~4.7/5 on Tripadvisor.
  • Highlights: Stunning 19th-century Moorish-style villa with private gardens leading to the lake. Spacious rooms/suites with antique furnishings and modern amenities. Excellent staff, spa/wellness options, and a highly regarded Michelin-starred restaurant (linked to chef Antonino Cannavacciuolo). Ideal for a romantic or special-occasion stay.
  • Drawbacks: Premium pricing (often $500–700+ per night); limited number of rooms (~12–14).
  • Best for: Luxury seekers and foodies.

2. Hotel San Rocco (4-star lakeside classic)

  • Ratings: Generally 7.9–8.2/10 on Booking/Expedia; mixed on Tripadvisor (around 3.5–3.6/5 in some tallies, higher on other sites). Strong on location.
  • Highlights: Converted 16th-century monastery right on the lakeshore with garden, outdoor pool, views of Isola San Giulio, restaurant, and easy access to the village. Some rooms have lake views/balconies; good breakfast noted by many guests.
  • Drawbacks: Facilities and rooms can feel dated or in need of updates for some visitors; value-for-money feedback varies.
  • Best for: Lakeside location and atmosphere without full 5-star prices (often $150–250+ per night).

3. Hotel Bocciolo (3-star solid mid-range)

  • Ratings: Strong, typically 8.7–9.2/10.
  • Highlights: Close to the lake and a short distance from Piazza Motta (historic center). Comfortable rooms, good breakfast, on-site restaurant with positive reviews, and convenient location.
  • Best for: Value and convenience (prices often around $150–200 per night).

4. Hotel La Bussola (3-star with views)

  • Ratings: Around 8.7–9.0/10.
  • Highlights: Peaceful promontory setting with panoramic views of the lake and Isola San Giulio. Garden with swimming pool, bright rooms (some with balconies), and friendly staff. Popular with tour groups and independent travelers.
  • Best for: Views and a quieter stay a short walk from the center.

Other strong options

  • Agriturismo Il Cucchiaio di Legno — High ratings (~9.3–9.4); rural/hillside setting with modern rooms, garden, and excellent local food focus (great for a more authentic/agriturismo experience).
  • Casa Longhi and similar highly rated apartments/B&Bs — Often score 9.7–9.9 for hospitality, location near the center/island ferry, and value (good alternatives if you prefer self-catering or boutique stays).
  • Central picks like Hotel Leon d’Oro or La Contrada dei Monti for historic-center convenience.

Tips:
Prices fluctuate seasonally (higher in summer); book lake-view rooms early. The historic center is largely pedestrianized, so check parking/access details. Many places emphasize lake views of Isola San Giulio and proximity to the Sacro Monte. For the absolute best experience, prioritize Villa Crespi for luxury or San Rocco/Bocciolo/La Bussola for balanced lakeside stays. Check current availability and recent reviews on Booking.com or Tripadvisor, as feedback can vary by room and season.
