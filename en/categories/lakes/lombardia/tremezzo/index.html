If you mean Tremezzo on Lake Como, these are the standout hotels I’d look at, depending on the kind of stay you want:

  • Grand Hotel Tremezzo - iconic luxury. The classic choice in Tremezzo: Art Nouveau grandeur, excellent lake/Bellagio views, multiple pools, gardens, restaurants, and direct access to the lake. The hotel dates to 1910 and sits right by Villa Carlotta.

  • Villa Sola Cabiati - extraordinary private splurge. This is not really a conventional hotel: it's a six-suite private villa operated by Grand Hotel Tremezzo, with its own gardens, pool, jetty, private chef and staff. It's designed for exclusive rental.

Tremezzo is one of the most stunning towns on Lake Como, offering front-row views of Bellagio and direct access to the central lake's highlights. Because Tremezzo is relatively small, your accommodation options range from one of the world's most legendary Grand Tour luxury properties to charming, intimate lakeside boutique stays.

The Absolute Top Pick for Luxury

If your budget allows for an ultra-luxury splurge, this is a legendary Belle Époque palace that truly lives up to the hype. The Vibe: Old-world Italian glamour combined with vibrant, modern hospitality. Standout Features: It is famous for its iconic "Water-on-the-Water" floating pool that sits directly on Lake Como, a beautiful rear garden pool, and the world-class T Spa. Dining: It hosts five restaurants and bars, including La Terrazza Gualtiero Marchesi, the only restaurant authorized to serve full menus by the founding father of modern Italian cuisine.

Concierge Tips for Your Stay

Ferry Convenience: Tremezzo is exceptionally well-connected. Staying anywhere near the main lakeside road puts you minutes from the ferry terminal, allowing you to easily hop across to Bellagio, Varenna, or Menaggio without needing a car.

Book Well in Advance: Given the limited number of hotels directly in Tremezzo, properties sell out months ahead for the peak summer season (June to September).

Pair with Local Highlights: If you visit, make sure to walk over to the neighboring Villa Carlotta to explore its massive botanical gardens and historic museum.

Grand Hotel Tremezzo is widely regarded as the top (and most iconic) hotel in Tremezzo on Lake Como.

Top Recommendation: Grand Hotel Tremezzo (5-star luxury)

  • Historic Art Nouveau property (opened 1910) right on the lake with spectacular views of Bellagio and the mountains.
  • Highlights: Three pools (including a famous floating “Water on the Water” pool on the lake), private beach/lido, large botanical gardens, spa (T Spa), tennis court, and multiple restaurants/bars.
  • Elegant rooms and suites (many with lake views, balconies, or rooftop terraces with hot tubs); high-end service and Belle Époque atmosphere with modern updates.
  • Consistently high ratings (around 9.5–9.8/10 or 4.8/5 on major sites) and frequently praised as one of the best resorts on Lake Como. It’s family-owned (De Santis family) and has hosted celebrities.
  • Note: It’s a premium/expensive option (often well over €1,000/night in high season).

Quick Tips

  • Luxury seekers: Grand Hotel Tremezzo is the clear standout for the classic Lake Como experience.
  • Better value / quieter: Hotel La Perla or La Darsena.
  • Tremezzo sits on the “Riviera of the Azaleas” shore, very close to Villa Carlotta gardens and with easy boat access to Bellagio and other spots.
  • Book early for peak season (summer); lake-view rooms are highly recommended.
