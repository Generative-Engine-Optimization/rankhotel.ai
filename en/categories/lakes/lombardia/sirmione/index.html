Sirmione is one of the most stunning towns on Lake Garda, completely split between its bustling medieval center and the tranquil, olive-grove-filled tip of the peninsula. When choosing a hotel here, the big decision is whether you want a grand thermal spa experience, a secluded historic estate, or a chic lakeside boutique close to the castle.

Here are the best hotels in Sirmione, depending on the kind of experience you are looking for:

The Top Pick for Ultra-Luxury & Romance

The Vibe: Pure, timeless Italian elegance. It feels like stepping straight onto the set of a classic cinematic masterpiece. Why choose it: This is the ultimate sanctuary in Sirmione. While the rest of the old town gets packed with day-trippers, Villa Cortine is secluded inside a massive five-hectare private park at the very tip of the peninsula. It offers absolute privacy, breathtaking sunset views over the lake, neoclassical architecture, a beautiful outdoor pool, and even a private pier. Insider Tip: Driving here requires navigating the strict restricted traffic zone (ZTL). Be sure to provide your license plate to the hotel ahead of time so they can register it with the local police at the castle.

The Top Pick for Natural Thermal Spas

The Vibe: Genteel, serene, and deeply restorative. Why choose it: Sirmione is famous for its healing sulfur hot springs, and this is the premier property to experience them. It is the only 5-star hotel in town featuring a private spa fed directly by natural thermal waters, complete with outdoor thermal pools that make you feel like you are floating right on Lake Garda. The lakefront views are magnificent, and the acclaimed on-site restaurant, L’Orangerie, serves top-tier regional cuisine. Insider Tip: If you want a similar thermal spa experience but at a slightly lower 4-star price point, look at its sister property, Hotel Sirmione Terme, located right in Piazza Castello.

Here are some of the best hotels in Sirmione (the charming peninsula town on Lake Garda), based on recent traveler ratings from TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and other sources (as of 2025–2026 data).

Sirmione is compact and popular, so the historic center (around the Scaliger Castle) is largely car-free—many hotels offer parking nearby or shuttle service. Thermal spa hotels are a big local draw thanks to the area’s natural hot springs.

Excellent Mid-to-Upper Range / Highly Rated Favorites

  • Casa Scaligeri (Hotel Casa Scaligeri) — Outstanding scores (9.6/10 on Booking from 1,300+ reviews; 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor). Boutique, family-run hotel right in the heart of the old town near the castle and beach. Praised for staff, cleanliness, location, and breakfast.
