Varenna is incredibly charming, but because it is a relatively small, authentic village, its high-end hotel inventory is intimate. While larger neighboring towns like Bellagio or Menaggio boast massive 5-star grand resorts, Varenna excels at historic character, romantic villas, and boutique guesthouses.

Here is a curated guide to the absolute best places to stay right in Varenna, tailored by the type of experience you are looking for.

💡 Local Concierge Advice for Your Stay:

Arriving by Train: If you take the scenic rail line from Milan Centrale to the Varenna-Esino station , keep in mind it sits at the top of a steep hill. It is about a 10-to-15 minute walk down to the lakefront. If you have heavy luggage, check if your hotel (like the Hotel Royal Victoria) offers a shuttle or arrange a quick taxi ahead of time.

Dining Reservations: Varenna's best tables—especially the lakeside decks at Casa du Lac or the fine-dining Visteria inside the Royal Victoria—book out days in advance during peak season. Secure your dinner slots at the same time you book your room.

Varenna is a small, charming village on the eastern shore of Lake Como, known for its scenic beauty, ferry connections, and walkable historic center. Hotels here are mostly boutique or historic properties rather than large resorts, with strong emphasis on lake views. Book well in advance, especially for lake-view rooms in peak season (summer).

Other Strong Options

  • Villa Varenna — Historic (1895), garden and terrace, solid ratings (~9.0 on Booking.com).
  • High-rated B&Bs/apartments — Bed & Breakfast il Bolentino (often 9.5+), Divina Vita Apartments, or smaller places like I Tre Oleandri for more intimate or budget-friendly stays with high guest scores.

Tips for Choosing

  • Location — Prioritize properties near the ferry pier or historic center for easy access to boats (to Bellagio, Menaggio, etc.) and the train station (Varenna-Esino). Many top hotels are walkable to both.
  • Views & Amenities — Lake-view rooms or terraces are highly prized and book out fastest. Royal Victoria stands out for a pool and spa.
  • Price Range — Expect mid-to-high pricing (often €200–600+ per night depending on season and view). Smaller hotels and B&Bs offer better value.
  • Practical Notes — Varenna has limited parking (often public garages or hotel arrangements a short walk away). Many properties involve stairs. Check recent reviews for updates on renovations or service.
