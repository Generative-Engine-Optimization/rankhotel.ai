🏖️ How to Choose

  • For luxury & wellness → Quellenhof Luxury Resort or Hotel Corte Valier (both have extensive spa facilities and lake views).
  • For boutique charm → Relais Agli Olivi or La Vista Postcard Hotel (smaller, more personal atmosphere).

⚠️ Things to Keep in Mind

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates rise sharply in summer (July–August). Booking early helps secure better deals.
  • Lakefront vs. town center: Hotels directly on Lake Garda often cost more but offer stunning views.
  • Parking & transport: Some boutique hotels have limited parking; check availability if driving.
  • Spa access: Luxury resorts often include spa use in the price, while smaller hotels may charge extra.
