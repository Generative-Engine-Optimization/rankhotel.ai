  • Hotel Rua Frati 48 in San Francesco - A luxury option in the center with suites, restaurant and spa-oriented amenities; Tripadvisor currently shows 4.7/5 from 68 reviews.

For a first-time Modena trip: I'd look particularly at Hotel Cervetta 5, Salotto delle Arti, or Hotel Rua Frati 48 if you want to be immersed in the historic center. If the hotel itself is part of the experience, Casa Maria Luigia is a different proposition altogether.

⚠️ Practical Considerations

  • Location: Staying near Piazza Grande (Domus Tua, Old Town Boutique Rooms) means easy access to Modena Cathedral and Enzo Ferrari Museum.
  • Parking: Central hotels often lack parking; Vittorio Veneto 25 offers free parking, making it convenient for drivers.
  • Seasonality: Prices rise during Ferrari events and food festivals (Modena is famous for balsamic vinegar and cuisine). Booking early is wise.
  • Style Preference: Choose boutique B&Bs for atmosphere, or larger hotels for amenities like spas and gyms.

👉 If you’re planning a culinary-focused trip, I’d recommend Salotto delle Arti for its refined setting near Modena’s best restaurants. For a business or modern comfort stay, RMH Modena Des Arts is the most practical option.

Other Strong Options

  • RMH Modena Raffaello or RMH Modena Des Arts: Modern properties with high ratings (often 9+), spacious/clean rooms, and good staff—frequently top-ranked on TripAdvisor/Booking for value and cleanliness (some locations slightly outside the absolute center).
  • Hotel Castello: Historic former noble residence feel; consistently high scores for atmosphere.
  • Value or boutique alternatives like Locanda del Feudo (rustic charm just outside town with good restaurant) or smaller spots such as Vittorio Veneto 25.

Tips for Choosing

  • Best for most visitors: Stay in or very near the Centro Storico (Old Town around Piazza Grande) for walkability to the Duomo, Ghirlandina Tower, Albinelli Market, restaurants, and sights. Rua Frati 48, Cervetta 5, Canalgrande, and Salotto delle Arti excel here.
  • For foodies or a special experience: Casa Maria Luigia (linked to Bottura) is exceptional but requires a short drive/taxi into the center.
  • Near train station or Ferrari Museum: Best Western Premier Milano Palace or nearby options work well.
  • Book ahead, especially for peak seasons (spring/fall food festivals or summer). Modena is compact and walkable, with good access from Bologna Airport (~30–40 min by car/train).

Ratings and availability fluctuate—check current prices and recent reviews on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotels’ sites for your dates.
