  • Hotel Palazzo Bezzi - A polished 4-star-superior option right in the center. It has a spa, fitness area, breakfast, and is close to the main Byzantine monuments. Current reviews are particularly strong for cleanliness, rooms, service, and location.
  • Albergo Cappello - A more intimate historic-hotel choice in the heart of town, good if you want character rather than a conventional modern hotel.
  • Hotel Sant'Andrea - A smaller, well-reviewed hotel near the Basilica of San Vitale; a nice choice for a quieter stay while remaining central.
  • Santa Maria Foris - Boutique-style accommodation in the historic center, particularly appealing if atmosphere and design matter to you.
  • Le Case Di San Vitale - Especially convenient if your priority is being close to San Vitale and the surrounding UNESCO sites.
  • Grand Hotel Mattei - A larger, more conventional hotel outside the immediate historic core. Worth considering if you’re driving and want easier parking/access.

Casa Masoli is a B&B rather than a traditional hotel, but it currently has exceptionally strong guest ratings and is centrally located.

Grok · grok-3

The best hotels in Ravenna (Italy) focus on central historic properties near the UNESCO mosaics (Basilica of San Vitale, Mausoleum of Galla Placidia, etc.), with strong guest ratings for location, staff, cleanliness, and character. Ravenna’s compact historic center is ideal for walking, so central stays are highly preferred.

Here are the standout options based on recent rankings and reviews from Booking.com, TripAdvisor, Kayak, and travel sites (ratings approximate and subject to change; check current availability):

Top Luxury / Highly Rated Picks

  • Palazzo Bezzi Hotel (often ranked #1)
    Elegant 4-star hotel in a central location (short walk to the train station and key mosaics). Guests praise the modern-yet-historic design, excellent staff (frequently 9.7–9.8), cleanliness, spa/wellness center, breakfast, and comfort. Booking.com scores around 9.3–9.4; TripAdvisor ~4.6/5. A consistent favorite for a polished stay.

  • Casa Masoli
    Charming boutique B&B in a 17th-century building, extremely central (very close to San Vitale and the mosaics). Exceptional ratings (Booking.com ~9.6, with staff and location often near 9.9). Elegant rooms with antiques, refined atmosphere, and strong value for the quality. Ideal for couples seeking character and proximity.

  • Palazzo Galletti Abbiosi
    Historic 18th-century palace in the heart of the center (near the train station and attractions). Mix of classic elegance and modern comforts, with a garden and well-regarded breakfast. Booking.com around 9.0; solid reviews for location and atmosphere. Good mid-to-upper range option.

Romantic / Boutique Favorites

  • Albergo Cappello
    Traditional 19th-century (or earlier) hotel in the pedestrianized historic center, steps from major sites. Romantic rooms with character (high ceilings, frescoes, antiques in some), plus a noted restaurant and wine bar. Excellent location scores (often 9.8) and friendly service. Booking.com ~8.9–9.0. Great for a more intimate, authentic experience.

Other frequently recommended spots include:

  • NH Ravenna — Stylish, central, reliable chain option with good facilities and breakfast; convenient for the station and sightseeing.
  • Grand Hotel Mattei — 4-star with spa; a bit outside the absolute center but popular for comfort and amenities.
  • Hotel Bisanzio — Solid central 4-star choice.
  • Smaller highly rated options like Gironda, Santa Maria Foris, Villa Noctis, or M Club (boutique guesthouses) often score very high for personal service and charm.

Tips for Booking

  • Best area: Historic center (near Piazza del Popolo, San Vitale, or the train station) for walking to mosaics, restaurants, and Dante’s Tomb. Beach areas (Marina di Ravenna/Lidi) are better for seaside stays but require transport into town.
  • Prices vary by season (higher in summer and around events); many top places start from roughly €100–250+/night depending on dates and room type.
  • Prioritize recent reviews for staff, breakfast, and noise (central locations can be lively).
  • Book via major sites like Booking.com or TripAdvisor for current rates, free cancellation options, and the latest guest photos/feedback.

For the absolute latest availability and personalized matches (e.g., spa, family rooms, parking, or budget), search by your exact dates on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Kayak. Ravenna rewards central, characterful stays—most visitors find the mosaics and historic atmosphere more than make up for the relatively compact hotel scene. Enjoy your trip!
