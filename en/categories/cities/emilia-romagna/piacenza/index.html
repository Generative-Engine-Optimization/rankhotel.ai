Grok · grok-3

Here are some of the consistently highest-rated and most recommended places to stay in Piacenza (Emilia-Romagna, Italy), based on recent guest reviews from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Trip.com, and similar sites.

Piacenza is a compact, walkable historic city, so central locations near Piazza Cavalli, the Duomo, or the train station are especially convenient. Options range from classic 4-star hotels to stylish boutique suites and a popular agriturismo just outside town.

Top Central Picks

  • Grande Albergo Roma (4-star, Via Cittadella 14)
    One of the strongest traditional hotel options right in the historic center (very close to Piazza Cavalli). Guests frequently praise the excellent location, rooftop terrace/views, helpful staff, cleanliness, and comfortable rooms. Ratings typically sit around 8.5–9.1 (Booking/Trip.com) and ~4.0/5 on Tripadvisor. It has parking, a restaurant/bar, and solid amenities for both leisure and business stays.

  • Palazzo Giacometti Suites (Via Scalabrini 7)
    A standout boutique/historic option in a beautifully restored 18th-century building. Reviewers describe it as feeling like staying in a private art collection or museum, with exceptional restoration, art, comfort, and outstanding hosts (especially noted for help with restaurants). Ratings are very high (often 9.5–9.6). It’s smaller/more exclusive and adults-oriented.

  • Domus San Martino GuestHouse (Via Roma 51)
    Highly rated central guesthouse (around 9.1) with soundproof rooms, strong cleanliness and comfort scores, and a great location a short walk from Piazza Cavalli and main sights. Good value, friendly atmosphere, and convenient for exploring on foot.

  • B&B HOTEL Piacenza (Via Alessandro Bolzoni 1)
    Modern 3-star property (relatively new/renovated) near the train station and with easy access to the center. Guests like the cleanliness, comfortable rooms, good breakfast, parking options, and friendly staff. Solid ratings in the high 8s to low 9s; excellent practical choice for value and convenience.

Strong Alternative Just Outside the Center

  • Corte La Volta (Strada della Mussina 65)
    Popular agriturismo/farm stay about 4–6 km from the historic center (easy drive or short taxi). Set in a peaceful rural courtyard with garden views. High ratings (~9.2) for hospitality, quiet setting, breakfast with local products, and overall experience. Ideal if you want countryside calm while still being close to the city and highways.

Other Noteworthy Mentions

  • Best Western Park Hotel — Reliable 4-star a bit further out (good for drivers, with parking, sauna, and business facilities).
  • High-rated smaller properties and apartments often appear at the top of guest lists (e.g., various “City House,” “Maggie House,” or boutique B&Bs), frequently scoring 9.5+ for personal service and style.

Tips:

  • Central hotels and guesthouses are best for walking to sights, restaurants, and piazzas.
  • If you have a car, the agriturismo or Park Hotel options add parking convenience and quieter surroundings.
  • Ratings and availability fluctuate—check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the hotel sites for your dates, recent photos, and current prices (many good options start in a reasonable mid-range for Italy).
  • Piacenza is known for excellent food (especially cured meats, cheeses, and local wines), so prioritising a place with strong local recommendations for restaurants is worthwhile.
