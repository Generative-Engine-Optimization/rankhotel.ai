Gemini

Staying in Matera means experiencing its famous Sassi (ancient cave dwellings) firsthand. The absolute best hotels here are grotto-style boutiques that seamlessly blend thousand-year-old carved limestone with striking modern luxury.

The Top Pick

If you want the ultimate, quintessential Matera experience, choose Aquatio Cave Luxury Hotel & Spa . Located in the heart of the Sasso Caveoso, this 5-star property manages to make ancient, rugged caves feel incredibly bright, sleek, and minimalist.

Why it wins: The crown jewel is its 500m² underground spa built entirely inside ancient water cisterns, featuring a heated pool carved straight into the tufa rock. It offers the perfect, tranquil contrast to a dusty afternoon of hiking through the historic stone city.

Strong Alternatives Based on Your Style

Every top-tier hotel in Matera has a distinct personality. Here is how they stack up depending on what you value most:

Hotel Star Class Vibe & Standout Feature When to Choose It
Sextantio Le Grotte Della Civita 4-Star Hyper-Authentic Romance. Minimal tech, stone floors, candlelit rooms, and classical music filling the historic cavern air. Choose this for pure historic immersion and a deeply romantic, candlelit escape away from the modern world.
Palazzo Gattini Luxury Hotel 5-Star Palatial Opulence. Set in a 15th-century palace on the highest ridge next to the Duomo. Offers panoramic views and a great cocktail terrace. Choose this if you prefer a grand, aristocratic palace feel over sleeping inside a deep underground cave, but still want easy trail access.
Vetera Matera 5-Star Sophisticated Design. A newer property receiving immense praise for its "white-glove treatment" and dramatic interplay of shadows and architectural lighting. Choose this if you appreciate high-concept interior design and want flawless service on a slightly quieter hillside.
Corte San Pietro 4-Star Warm & Intimate. A beautifully run boutique property with an internal courtyard and a lovely herb garden. Choose this if you prefer smaller, family-run hospitality and a genuinely cozy, rustic-elegant atmosphere.
Palazzo del Duca 4-Star Private Pool Suites. A mix of historic rooms and suites, some of which feature their own private indoor cave plunge pools. Choose this if you want to splurge on a specific suite that has a personal pool carved right into your room.

Practical Concierge Tips for Matera

Pack Light & Wear Sneakers: The Sassi are a maze of steep, uneven stone steps and slippery tufa cobblestones. Walking to your hotel with heavy luggage can be incredibly brutal.

Accessibility: If anyone in your group has mobility issues, avoid deep cave properties. Palazzo Gattini or Lo Stemma Luxury sit closer to the upper, flatter roads of the town, making them much easier to access by car or shuttle.

Book the Spa in Advance: If you choose a hotel primarily for its cave pool or thermal spa (like Aquatio or Palazzo Gattini), check if you need to reserve a private time slot before you arrive.

Grok · grok-3

The best hotels in Matera (Italy) emphasize unique cave (Sassi) stays in the UNESCO World Heritage site, blending ancient rock-hewn architecture with modern luxury. Top options consistently rank highly on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and travel guides for atmosphere, location in the historic center, service, and amenities like spas.

Here are standout recommendations (focusing on highly rated luxury and boutique properties; rates vary by season and are often €200–600+ per night for top-tier rooms):

Top Luxury Cave & Historic Hotels

  • Aquatio Cave Luxury Hotel & Spa (5-star): Frequently tops lists. Rooms and suites carved into the rock in the Sassi, with a spa, rock-carved pool, and strong modern design. Excellent location for exploring on foot; highly praised for comfort, service, and the unique cave experience. Guest ratings often 9.4–9.5+.

  • Vetera Matera (Relais & Châteaux, 5-star): Boutique “albergo diffuso” (spread across historic buildings) with cave suites and noble suites offering views. Features an underground spa (Jacuzzi, steam room, sauna, outdoor area), contemporary art, and strong sunset views. Recent Travel + Leisure review highlights it as among the plushest in town. Ratings around 9.6.

  • Palazzo Gattini Luxury Hotel (5-star, Leading Hotels of the World / VRetreats): Elegant 15th-century palace in a prime elevated spot overlooking the Sassi (near the cathedral). Mix of historic and modern design, rooftop views/drinks, underground spa, and refined restaurant. Intimate (around 20 rooms). Strong ratings and recent affiliation boost.

  • Sant’Angelo, Matera (Small Luxury Hotels / Luxury Resort): Interconnected historic cave dwellings in a central historic square (Piazza San Pietro Caveoso). Atmospheric rooms with views, outdoor seating areas, and helpful staff. Great for couples and central exploration. Ratings around 9.0–9.1.

  • Sextantio Le Grotte della Civita (Design Hotels member): Iconic, atmospheric restoration of prehistoric caves in the Sassi (Civita area). Very authentic feel with candlelit elements, high-quality bedding/baths, excellent breakfast in a historic space, and outstanding service. Often called a once-in-a-lifetime stay; ratings frequently 4.9/5 or 9.5–9.8. Note: Some rooms can feel more spartan/humid due to the authentic cave nature.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Locanda di San Martino Hotel & Thermae Romanae: Cave hotel with Roman-style thermal baths/spa options. Solid mid-to-upscale choice in a central Sassi location; good breakfast and atmosphere.
  • Pianelle Resort: High ratings (often near 9.6–5.0 in some rankings) with excellent views and a convenient location near key sites.
  • Il Palazzotto Residence & Winery or BV Quarry Matera: Stylish options with strong guest feedback for location, design, and service.

Tips: Book cave rooms for the signature Matera experience (many have modern amenities like excellent bedding and bathrooms while retaining stone walls). The historic Sassi area involves stairs and limited car access—hotels often help with luggage/transfers. Parking can be a challenge; confirm options. For the best rates and availability, check current reviews on Tripadvisor/Booking.com, as preferences (spa focus, views, budget, couples vs. families) matter. Newer or mid-range options also exist outside the core Sassi if you prefer easier access.
