Gemini

When choosing a place to stay in Imperia , the biggest decision is whether you want to be right in the historic center, steps from the marina, or nestled in the olive groves overlooking the Ligurian coast. Since Imperia is divided into two main coastal districts—Oneglia and Porto Maurizio—staying centrally gives you easy access to both, while neighboring towns like Diano Marina offer fantastic full-service resort alternatives just minutes away.

Here is a curated shortlist of the best hotels in and around Imperia based on style and location preference:

🏛️ Top Picks in Central Imperia

Hotel Rossini al Teatro ( 4-star hotel | ~$98/night): Located in Oneglia’s center, this hotel is built into a former theater. It is exceptionally clean, boasts large parking spaces, and sits within walking distance of the historic commercial streets and the sea.

Hotel Croce di Malta ( 3-star hotel | ~$122/night): Situated right by the Porto Maurizio marina. It offers direct sea views, straightforward but comfortable rooms, and puts you right next to excellent waterfront dining.

Arcadia Marina (B&B/Boutique Stay | ~$104/night): A charming option featuring a magical garden and great tranquility. It is highly praised for fresh, custom-prepared breakfasts and its close proximity to the old town, beaches, and local gelaterias.

Uliveto Saglietto ( 4-star agriturismo/hotel | ~$152/night): Tucked away in the hills of Poggi (Imperia), this is ideal if you want a rustic yet elegant retreat. It features an outdoor pool, clean rooms, and offers tours of an old olive oil factory.

🏖️ Premium Alternatives in Neighboring Diano Marina (10 mins away)

Hotel Torino Wellness & Spa ( 4-star hotel | ~$196/night): The ultimate pick for relaxation. Tucked away on a quiet side street, it offers an incredible ambience, a sophisticated spa, an outdoor pool, and top-tier dining.

B&B HOTEL Diano Marina Palace ( 4-star hotel | ~$99/night): A stellar beachfront option. Guests highly recommend it for its modern rooms and the incredible experience of falling asleep to the sound of crashing waves.

Other strong mentions

  • Hotel Kristina — Solid sea views and location near the water; more budget-friendly.
  • Hotel Miramare — Historic 19th-century property with park, pool, and Gulf of Imperia views (ratings around 8.0; some note it feels dated in places).
  • Smaller highly rated B&Bs/apartments (e.g., Il Mirto B&B, Il Sole di Poggi) often score extremely well for atmosphere, breakfast, and personal touches but have fewer reviews.

Quick tips

  • Location preference: Stay in Porto Maurizio for the historic center, cathedral, and more scenic waterfront; Oneglia for the harbor, museums, and slightly more commercial vibe.
  • Seasonality: Summer (especially July–August) books up and prices rise; shoulder seasons (May–June, September) offer better value and milder weather.
  • Prices: Expect roughly €100–250+ per night depending on season, room type, and whether breakfast/parking is included. Many properties offer free or paid parking (useful in the historic areas).
  • Ratings can vary slightly by platform and date range—check current reviews and photos for your travel dates, as smaller properties sometimes have limited recent feedback.
