If you're using La Spezia as your launchpad for the Cinque Terre, you're making a smart move. It's much easier to navigate with luggage, less crowded, and has a fantastic local restaurant scene. When staying here, your main decision is choosing between a sleek boutique hotel right near the train station or a peaceful getaway tucked up in the hills.

🏨 The Best Hotels & Guest Houses in La Spezia

Vibe: Chic, industrial-style boutique hotel with a lively atmosphere. Why I love it: If you want a traditional hotel experience rather than a small guest house, The Poet Hotel is the gold standard in town. It's exceptionally well-designed with a trendy lounge and a stylish bar. Location & Logistics: Located right in the heart of downtown, it's a very straightforward 5-minute walk to the central train station. Paid parking is available on-site if you are driving.

Despite being right in the middle of town, the rooms are whisper-quiet thanks to heavy acoustic sealing. Location & Logistics: Practically sitting on top of the train station (a 4-minute walk), making it unbeatable for catching early morning trains to Cinque Terre. Location & Logistics: It is tucked up in the hills, so getting to the city center requires a 20-minute walk down a long, steep flight of stairs. It's a fantastic pick if you're driving or don't mind a little exercise, but skip it if you have mobility constraints or heavy luggage.

Location & Logistics: It sits in a quiet courtyard directly off the main walking strip, neatly split about 10 minutes from both the central station and the ferry harbor.

Location & Logistics: Situated right on Piazza Saint Bon, putting you less than a 5-minute walk from the tracks.

💡 Local Tips for Your Stay

Train vs. Ferry: If your primary plan is riding the trains into the Cinque Terre villages, book a spot near Piazza Saint Bon or the station (like Glam Resort or Affittacamere Superior). If you prefer taking the scenic morning ferry boats, staying closer to the Via del Prione shopping strip or the harbor will save you daily footsteps.

The "Hotel" Reality: Many of the best-rated accommodations in La Spezia are technically affittacamere (boutique guest houses or room rentals) rather than full-scale hotels with 24/7 bellhops. They offer much higher quality and more personal service for the price, but make sure to coordinate your check-in time ahead of arrival.

Quick Tips

  • For Cinque Terre access: Prioritize anything within a 5-minute walk of La Spezia Centrale (Firenze e Continentale, Casa Danè, The Poet Hotel, Hotel Venezia).
  • For views/atmosphere: Le Ville Relais or NH La Spezia.
  • Budget vs. comfort: Casa Danè and similar affittacamere (room rentals) offer excellent value; the 4-star options provide more facilities and consistency.
  • Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer and peak Cinque Terre months). Always check current rates and availability on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Expedia, and read the most recent reviews for noise, parking, or elevator notes.
