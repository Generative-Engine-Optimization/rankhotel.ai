Gemini

Taranto has a fascinating layout, split between the historic Borgo Antico (the old island city) and the bustling Borgo Nuovo (new town), with a few stunning luxury retreats tucked just outside the center. Deciding where to stay comes down to whether you want full resort relaxation, historic boutique charm, or sweeping sea views within walking distance of the sights.

The Luxury Countryside Escape

If budget isn’t a primary constraint and you want a true Puglia countryside experience, this is hands-down the top choice. Converted from an ancient farmhouse and surrounded by rolling hills and olive groves near the shore, it’s an incredibly quiet, peaceful oasis. What to expect: It features a world-class spa, an excellent on-site fine dining restaurant, and a beautiful outdoor pool area tucked among fig trees. The tradeoff: It sits about a 10-to-15-minute drive outside the city center, so you will need a car or taxi to go sightseeing.

The Best of Borgo Nuovo (Modern & Sea Views)

Located right in the center of town along Via Roma, this is arguably the most practical and well-rounded hotel for first-time visitors. It bridges the gap between the new town shopping streets and the historic center, making everything walkable. What to expect: Ask for a room facing the harbor for fantastic port views. Locals and past travelers highly praise their morning spread—it is routinely cited as one of the best breakfasts in the region. Good to know: Paid parking is available, which is a massive plus in central Taranto.

If you want a waterfront resort feel without leaving the city, the Delfino sits right on the edge of the Gulf of Taranto along Viale Virgilio. What to expect: Sweeping panoramic ocean views and a large outdoor pool right on the water. If you stay here, make sure to book one of the updated rooms in the renovated tower; they are sharp, minimalist, and modern. The tradeoff: Some of the standard rooms in the older wings can feel a bit dated.

Boutique Historic Stays (Borgo Antico)

Staying in the Old Town offers incredible character, but keep in mind that the historic core has a very raw, gritty, and authentic feel to it. Streets are narrow, and you'll be navigating older staircases rather than large elevators.

Here are some of the best hotels in Taranto (Puglia, Italy), based on consistent high ratings, guest reviews, and mentions across Booking.com, TripAdvisor, and other sources (as of recent 2025–2026 data).

Taranto offers a mix of boutique stays in the historic center (Città Vecchia/Borgo Antico), modern options with sea views or amenities, and a luxury masseria-style property a bit outside town.

Top Recommendations

Hotel L’Arcangelo - Boutique Hotel (often ranked #1 or near the top)

  • Historic-center boutique hotel in a restored building with Apulian stone, frescoes, wood beams, and terraces/rooftop views (some rooms overlook the cathedral or city).
  • Excellent staff (frequently praised as friendly and helpful), strong breakfast, clean and stylish rooms.
  • Ratings: ~8.9–9.1 on Booking, 4.8/5 on TripAdvisor (1,000+ reviews). Very central for exploring the old town, castle, and waterfront.
  • Good mid-range option (often from around €100–150/night depending on dates).

Salina Hotel (4-star, modern favorite)

  • Newer property with modern rooms, rooftop terrace/restaurant, infinity-style pool with views, spa, and good breakfast.
  • Highly rated for cleanliness, comfort, and facilities; staff often described as friendly.
  • Ratings: ~9.0 on Booking, 4.7/5 on TripAdvisor. Located a few kilometers from the absolute center (taxi or car convenient; some note it as slightly outlying).
  • Strong choice for a more resort-like stay with amenities.

Hotel Delfino Taranto (also known as Delfino Hotel Taranto – HO Collection)

  • Seafront 4-star hotel with panoramic sea views, modern rooms, and solid facilities.
  • Popular for location on the waterfront and comfort; frequently appears in “best of” lists.
  • Ratings typically around 8.9–9.0. Convenient for both the city and sea.

Relais Histò San Pietro Sul Mar Piccolo (luxury 5-star standout)

  • Historic masseria/estate overlooking the Mar Piccolo, set in olive groves with a restored 11th-century basilica elements, large spa (~1,750 m² with Roman baths-inspired features), outdoor pool, restaurant, and refined rooms/suites (natural stone and wood accents).
  • Ideal for a luxurious, peaceful escape rather than a city-center base (about 7–10 km from central Taranto).
  • Higher-end pricing (often €300–450+). Praised for history, spa, and serene setting.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Hotel Europa — Central location near the Swing Bridge, Aragonese Castle, and marina; solid ratings (~9.1) and convenient for sightseeing.
  • Boutique or suite-style options such as Bacã Suites, Restaurant & Bar, Sirenuse Suites, or Palazzo Matà — Often score very high (9.3–9.6 range on some platforms) for modern design, location, and service; good for shorter or more independent stays.

Quick Tips

  • For history & walkability → Prioritize L’Arcangelo or Hotel Europa in/near the old town.
  • For modern comfort + pool/spa → Salina Hotel.
  • For luxury & relaxation → Relais Histò.
  • For sea views → Delfino or similar waterfront properties.
    Prices fluctuate with season (higher in summer); always check current availability and reviews on Booking.com or TripAdvisor, as smaller properties can sell out. Parking can be limited in the historic center, so confirm if you have a car.

Taranto itself mixes industrial heritage with a fascinating old town, castle, and nearby Ionian Sea beaches—pair your hotel choice with those interests. If you have preferences (budget, dates, pool must-have, family-friendly, etc.), I can refine the suggestions further!
