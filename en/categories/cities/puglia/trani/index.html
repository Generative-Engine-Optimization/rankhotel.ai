Practical Tips for Choosing

  • Location priority — Stay near the harbor/cathedral for walkable access to the main sights, restaurants, and seafront. Many top places are in or very near the historic center.
  • Style of stay — Trani favors boutique hotels and high-quality B&Bs over big chains. Expect characterful historic buildings with modern comforts.
  • Common guest feedback — Staff helpfulness, cleanliness, and location score very highly across the leaders. Occasional notes mention dated elements in older buildings (e.g., some rooms at San Paolo) or parking challenges in the restricted historic zone (ZTL)—many hotels offer valet, nearby arrangements, or guidance.
  • Prices & booking — Rates vary by season (higher in summer); expect roughly mid-range to upper-mid for the top properties (check current availability as they fluctuate). Sites like Booking.com and Tripadvisor are good for recent verified reviews and photos.
  • Alternatives — If you prefer a pool or more space, look at villa-style options a bit outside the densest center. For pure value, the ibis styles is a solid, no-fuss choice.

Trani is known for its beautiful waterfront cathedral, castle, and relaxed Apulian atmosphere—staying centrally lets you enjoy it on foot.
