For a stay in Trapani, the best options are boutique guest houses and historic palazzo conversions clustered within the pedestrianized old town or tucked away in peaceful neighborhood oases. Choosing the right spot depends primarily on whether you want a seamless base for island-hopping from the port, a historic atmosphere, or a resort feel with a pool.

Here is a curated breakdown of the top places to stay in Trapani:

✨ My Top Pick: The Peaceful Oasis

Giardini Mon Plaisir (4-star)If you want an exceptional, highly personalized boutique experience, this is the absolute best property in town. It feels like a hidden sanctuary just slightly removed from the old town noise.

The Vibe: A beautifully restored historic villa surrounded by lush, private citrus gardens.

Standout Features: A quiet outdoor pool and an incredible daily breakfast featuring fresh local Trapanese delicacies and citrus juices picked straight from their own trees.

Best For: Travelers seeking a relaxing, high-end retreat with superb local hospitality.

⚓ Best for Island Hopping & Rooftop Views

Azzoli Trapani (4-star)Located in the heart of the historic center, this sleek hotel features premium apartment-style suites that offer the perfect mix of independent living and hotel amenities.

The Vibe: Chic, responsive service, and beautifully maintained historic architecture.

Standout Features: A stunning rooftop terrace and pool that offers panoramic views of the city rooftops while you eat breakfast. It is situated right next to the port, making early-morning hydrofoils to the Egadi Islands effortless.

Best For: Couples or families who want a central base with a rooftop pool and easy ferry access.

Residence la Gancia (4-star)Perched beautifully near the Piazza Mercato del Pesce, this property offers self-catering flexibility without sacrificing hotel comfort.

The Vibe: Mediterranean elegance meets modern comfort.

Standout Features: Elegant apartments equipped with convenient kitchenettes, paired with a rooftop terrace overlooking the sea.

Best For: Longer stays or anyone who wants sweeping sea views with their morning espresso.

🏛️ Best for Historic Old Town Character

Dimora Botteghelle (4-star)Tucked right onto Corso Vittorio Emanuele, this boutique gem places you directly into Trapani's vibrant street life.

The Vibe: Artfully designed interiors that emphasize high-quality finishes, local character, and intimate charm.

Standout Features: Outstanding personalized concierge care and an exceptional breakfast. It is the perfect tactical base if you are renting a car to explore Erice, Segesta, or Castellammare del Golfo.

Best For: Foodies and sightseers who want high-end design in a hyper-central location.

Hotel San Michele (3-star)A reliable, classic choice that delivers upper-tier comfort inside an authentic, historic atmosphere.

The Vibe: Warm, secure, and old-world, built directly around a traditional open-air courtyard patio.

Standout Features: Extremely walkable to every major cathedral, restaurant, and alleyway downtown. It offers spacious, refined rooms (some with private balconies) at a very fair value.

Best For: Travelers wanting a traditional hotel layout right in the historic center.

🏖️ Best for Beachfront & Relaxation

I Mulini Resort (4-star)If you prefer a beach-forward resort experience over a tight historic street grid, look just slightly north toward the coastline bordering Erice.

The Vibe: Airy, open, and classic seaside relaxation centered around historic salt-mill architecture.

Standout Features: Direct private beach access, a great outdoor pool, a hot tub, and a highly-regarded regional Sicilian restaurant on-site.

Best For: Sun-seekers who want a resort property with easy parking and open sea views.

Grok · grok-3

Top hotels in Trapani (Sicily) consistently praised across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, and travel sites include Palazzo Gatto Art Hotel & Spa, Room of Andrea, Hotel San Michele, and Residence La Gancia. These stand out for high guest ratings (typically 4.5–4.7/5), central locations in or near the historic center/old town, strong service, and amenities suited to exploring Trapani’s streets, port (for Egadi Islands ferries), beaches, and nearby spots like Erice or the salt pans.

Here’s a breakdown of some of the strongest options:

Palazzo Gatto Art Hotel & Spa (often ranked among the very best)

  • Restored 18th-century building in the historic center with a modern, design-focused interior featuring local ceramics.
  • Highlights: Spa (sauna, Turkish bath, hydromassage pool, salt room), rooftop terrace/jacuzzi with city views, wine lounge, and solid breakfast.
  • Ratings frequently around 4.7/5 (Tripadvisor) or 9.0+ on Booking; praised for cleanliness, staff, and location near restaurants and attractions.
  • Ideal for a stylish, wellness-oriented stay.

Room of Andrea (Room Of Andrea Hotel)

  • Elegant hotel in a historic building near the old town, park, beach, and port area (Viale Regina Margherita).
  • Highlights: Rooftop pool and bar with views, spacious rooms, excellent and varied Sicilian breakfast, helpful staff, and convenient parking nearby.
  • Strong Tripadvisor best-value ranking and high scores (~4.5/5 or 9.0 on Booking) for comfort, location, and service. Guests often call the breakfast one of the best in Sicily.

Hotel San Michele

  • Boutique hotel in the historic center, converted from parts of an ancient church with charming, renovated rooms.
  • Highlights: Central location (walkable to town and sea), attentive staff, good breakfast, and convenient services like valet parking.
  • Consistently high ratings (~4.6/5) and frequent mentions as a jewel-like, characterful stay.

Residence La Gancia

  • Centrally located in the old town near restaurants, attractions, and the ferry terminal.
  • Highlights: Spacious rooms (some with sea views or unique features), rooftop terrace/solarium, solid breakfast, and high overall scores (~4.7/5 with many reviews).
  • Popular for its convenient base and value.

Other strong contenders

  • BADIA NUOVA Apart Hotel — Chic option with rooftop lounge/bar offering sea and historic-center views; good for a more apartment-style stay.
  • Hotel Vittoria or Hotel Trapani In — Reliable central or near-port choices with solid reviews, useful if you need easy ferry access to the Egadi Islands.
  • Relais Antiche Saline — A bit outside the center near the salt pans (a scenic area); higher-end/unique rural feel with strong reviews for a more peaceful, atmospheric stay.

Quick tips

  • Best areas: Historic center/old town for walkability to sights, dining, and atmosphere; near the port for ferries; or slightly outside for quieter resorts/pools with views of the salt flats or countryside.
  • Luxury or wine-focused stays (e.g., Baglio Soria or similar baglios) are more common a short drive toward Marsala or other nearby towns.
  • Ratings and rankings fluctuate with recent reviews and availability—always check current prices, photos, and cancellation policies on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or the hotel sites. Peak summer (especially July–August) books up and costs more; shoulder seasons are often better value.
  • Trapani works well as a base for western Sicily (Erice, Segesta, beaches like San Vito Lo Capo, salt pans, and island day trips).

Preferences (budget, pool/spa priority, car access, family vs. couples) can narrow it further—let me know if you have specific needs!
