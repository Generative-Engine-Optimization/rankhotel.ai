If you mean Catania city itself, rather than the wider area around Mount Etna, I’d focus on these:

  • Palazzo Sangiorgio - luxury / historic center. A stylish 5-star option near Piazza Duomo, with spacious rooms and a strong sense of place. It’s also currently among the better-reviewed luxury hotels in Catania.
  • Liberty Hotel - best boutique feel. An elegant, intimate property in a historic building. Tripadvisor currently lists it as its top-value boutique hotel in Catania, with a 4.7/5 rating.
  • Habitat Boutique Hotel - best for modern boutique style + location. Right in the center, with a garden and contemporary rooms; Booking.com currently gives it 9.1/10 from more than 1,200 reviews.
  • Asmundo Gisira Art of Living Boutique Market - best for character/design. An art-focused boutique property close to the Pescheria market, particularly interesting if you want something distinctly Sicilian rather than a conventional hotel.
  • Duomo Suites & SPA - best for central location + spa. Contemporary rooms in a historic building, right in the old city, with a rooftop terrace and hammam.
  • Hotel Bastiò Suites | Suite Lusso Catania - best for a romantic splurge. High-end suites near the historic center; it currently has a 4.8/5 rating on the local business listing and is among Tripadvisor's highly rated 5-star properties.
  • Palace Catania | UNA Esperienze - best full-service traditional hotel. On Via Etnea, making it convenient for exploring the center; Booking.com currently lists it at 8.8/10.

If I were narrowing it down

For a first visit: Habitat or Palazzo Sangiorgio
For romance/luxury: Bastiò or Palazzo Sangiorgio
For boutique character: Liberty or Asmundo Gisira
For spa + sightseeing: Duomo Suites & SPA
For a more conventional upscale hotel: Palace Catania

One important distinction: Taormina has some much more extravagant resort-style hotels, but they're not in Catania and are quite different as a base for exploring the city.

🔑 Key Considerations

  • Location: Most top hotels are in or near the historic center (Via Etnea, Piazza Duomo), ideal for sightseeing.
  • Style: Choose between historic elegance (Liberty Hotel, Palazzo Marletta) or modern boutique (Zoe Luxury Suites, Zahara).
  • Budget: Luxury hotels range US$380–400 per night; boutique stays are US$150–225.
  • Amenities: Rooftop terraces with Etna views (Palace Catania, Duomo Suites), spa services (Duomo Suites), and historic charm (Liberty Hotel, Palazzo Marletta).

⚠️ Risks & Trade-offs

  • Seasonal pricing: Rates can spike in summer; booking early is recommended.
  • Boutique hotels: While charming, they may lack full-service amenities like gyms or large restaurants.
  • Historic buildings: Elegant but sometimes less soundproof than modern hotels.

✅ Recommendation

Gemini

If you want the absolute best experience in Catania, I highly recommend booking the Palace Catania | UNA Esperienze. It strikes the perfect balance of historic Sicilian character, premium 4-star comfort, and a central location right on Via Etnea that makes walking to everything effortless.

However, Catania has a diverse hotel scene. The right choice depends on whether you are looking for absolute five-star luxury, a chic boutique vibe, or a coastal resort.

Here is my curated shortlist of the best hotels in Catania, categorized by travel style:

🌟 The Ultimate Luxury: Palazzo Sangiorgio

Vibe: Ultra-modern sophistication hidden behind a historic facade.

Why Choose It: This brand-new 5-star hotel is located in a beautifully renovated pocket of the historic center. The spacious rooms are packed with high-tech amenities, and the on-site dining is widely considered some of the best in the city.

Key Highlights: Exceptional modern spa services, private courtyard spaces, and brilliant a la carte breakfasts.

Location: Piazza Cardinale Pappalardo, 1 — a quick walk to the Duomo.

🏛️ Classic Baroque Elegance: Palace Catania | UNA Esperienze

Vibe: Grand, historic, and distinctly Catanese.

Why Choose It: Set inside a stunning 1915 baroque palace , this hotel uses local volcanic black stone and bright Sicilian ceramics to create a beautiful sense of place. It’s the ultimate safe, central base for first-time visitors.

Key Highlights: The Etnea Roof bar and restaurant offers legendary, panoramic views of Mount Etna over breakfast or sunset cocktails.

Location: Via Etnea, 218 — right in the heart of the main shopping and dining artery.

🎨 Art & Heritage Charm: Liberty Hotel

Vibe: Intimate, romantic, and old-world aristocratic.

Why Choose It: A refined 4-star gem built inside an early 1900s Liberty-style mansion . The rooms feature soaring, double-height ceilings, and some are designed as grand duplexes.

Key Highlights: Renowned for its flawless, personalized concierge service and a peaceful stone courtyard perfect for morning espresso.

Location: Via S. Vito, 40 — tucked away on a quiet side street just minutes from the bustling Teatro Massimo Bellini area.

🏖️ Beach Resort & Transit: Romano Palace Luxury Hotel

Vibe: Mediterranean resort getaway.

Why Choose It: If you want a pool, massive gardens, and private beach access while staying close to the city, this is the main 5-star option. It is also strategically positioned just a 5-minute drive from Catania Fontanarossa Airport .

Key Highlights: A massive 25,000 m² private garden, themed luxury suites (like Moroccan and Gold Baroque), and a free shuttle that runs into the historic center.

Location: Viale Presidente Kennedy, 28 — along the La Playa coastline.

💎 Boutique Value: Eh13 Luxury Accommodation

Vibe: Sleek, intimate, and modern.

Why Choose It: A highly rated boutique guest house that offers luxury-level comfort and design without the steep price tag.

Key Highlights: Exceptionally clean, minimalist rooms featuring private terraces that offer beautiful, direct window views of Mount Etna.

Location: Via Sant'Euplio, 13 — right next to the lush Villa Bellini park.

  • Historic center is ideal for first-time visitors (walkable to major sights, markets, and nightlife). Airport hotels (e.g., Catania International Airport Hotel) suit early/late flights but are less atmospheric.
  • Prices vary widely by season (higher in summer); expect boutique/luxury options roughly €150–400+ per night depending on dates and room type. Always check current availability and reviews.
  • Many properties emphasize Sicilian design (lava stone, ceramics, local art) and strong breakfasts.
