Gemini

Choosing where to stay in Ragusa, Sicily, depends entirely on whether you want to immerse yourself in the historic, labyrinthine alleyways of Ragusa Ibla (the lower, older section) or prefer the grander, more accessible layout of Ragusa Superiore (the upper town built post-1693 earthquake).

If you are looking for absolute romantic immersion, historic atmosphere, and spectacular views, I'd highly recommend sticking to Ragusa Ibla. If you are driving a rental car and prefer easier parking, modern conveniences, and less intense stair-climbing, Ragusa Superiore is a much smoother base.

A Quick Insider Tip on Navigating Ragusa

If you choose to stay in Ragusa Ibla , note that traffic zones (ZTL) are heavily restricted, and driving down the narrow stone alleys can be stressful. Always message your hotel ahead of arrival to get specific coordinates for their designated parking or drop-off zones. If you stay in Superiore , you can easily walk down the famous panoramic staircases to explore Ibla, but plan to take a local taxi or bus back up to avoid a brutal uphill climb at the end of the night!

Luxury / Countryside Options

  • Eremo della Giubiliana: 5-star historic hermitage/monastery turned resort a short drive south of Ragusa (near Marina di Ragusa). Features outdoor pool, gardens, on-site archaeological site (ancient necropolis), restaurant, and even a private airstrip. Peaceful countryside setting with sea views on clear days; ideal if you want a resort feel rather than staying in the old town.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Antico Convento dei Cappuccini — Former monastery in Giardino Ibleo with cloister gardens and a peaceful atmosphere.
  • Terra del Sole Ibla (B&B) — Frequently praised for panoramic private terraces and value.
  • Relais Chiaramonte or Artemisia Resort — Good countryside options with pools, a bit further out.
  • Itria Palace or La Casa del Gelsomino — Solid mid-range/higher-rated choices with good locations and guest feedback.

Quick Tips

  • Best area: Stay in Ragusa Ibla for the full Baroque experience (walkable to the Duomo, piazzas, and restaurants). Ragusa Superiore is more modern/practical with easier parking and access.
  • When to book: Spring (April–June) and autumn (September–October) are ideal; summer is hotter and busier.
  • Prices: Mid-range boutique options often start around €80–150/night; luxury properties higher, especially in peak season. Always check current rates and availability on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites.
  • Ratings and availability can fluctuate, so verify recent reviews for your travel dates.

San Giorgio Palace is the most frequently recommended all-rounder for location + quality + value in the historic center. For pure luxury or a quiet countryside escape, consider Relais Antica Badia or Eremo della Giubiliana. Enjoy Ragusa—it’s one of Sicily’s most beautiful Baroque towns!
