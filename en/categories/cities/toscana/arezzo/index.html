Grok · grok-3

Here are some of the best hotels in Arezzo (Tuscany, Italy), based on consistent high ratings from TripAdvisor, Booking.com, Hotels.com, and other review platforms (as of 2025–2026 data).

Arezzo is a charming historic city known for its medieval center, Piazza Grande, and Piero della Francesca frescoes. Options range from luxury hillside retreats to well-located city-center properties. Ratings and popularity can vary slightly by site and dates, so check current availability and reviews for your travel period.

Luxury / Standout Options

  • Badia di Pomaio (Small Luxury Hotels of the World) — Frequently tops luxury lists. A restored 17th-century abbey on a hillside about 15 minutes from the center. Highlights include an infinity pool with valley views, beautiful gardens, farm-to-table Tuscan cuisine (often using on-site ingredients), and serene, stylish rooms. Exceptional guest scores (around 9.6–9.7/10 on Booking; strong TripAdvisor feedback). Ideal for a peaceful, romantic, or special-occasion stay. Adults-oriented (typically 14+).

  • Chimera Tuscany Resort — Modern resort-style property a short drive outside the center with spa, outdoor pool, fitness facilities, and stylish rooms/gardens. High ratings (often 8.9–9.8 range depending on the site) and praised for comfort, staff, and facilities. Good if you want amenities beyond a pure city hotel.

Excellent City-Center / Highly Rated Choices

  • Hotel Continentale — Classic 4-star hotel right in the historic center (near the train station and a short walk to Piazza Grande and key sights). Features a rooftop terrace with city views, renovated rooms/suites, fitness center, bar, and strong breakfast. Consistently high scores (around 8.5–8.6/10) with praise for location, staff, and convenience. Pet-friendly.

  • La Corte del Re Suite & Rooms — Historic building overlooking Piazza Grande itself, with original Etruscan/medieval elements, uniquely decorated rooms (some with kitchenettes and square views), and an antique feel. Very strong location ratings and good overall scores (TripAdvisor often ranks it highly; Booking around 8.3+). Great for immersing in the old town atmosphere.

  • Sugar Rooms Residenza d’Epoca — Stylish, modern rooms in a renovated historic palace (Corso Italia, very central, near Piazza Grande). High scores (often 9.5/10 on Booking), praised for design, cleanliness, staff (especially host service), breakfast, and location. Boutique feel.

Reliable Mid-Range / Popular Picks

  • Hotel Minerva — Solid 4-star option slightly outside the absolute center (walkable or short bus/taxi). Spacious rooms, restaurant with Tuscan food, fitness/wellness facilities (including Turkish bath), free parking, and good breakfast. Strong ratings (around 8.5–8.8) and frequently recommended for value and amenities.

  • Etrusco Arezzo Hotel (Sure Hotel Collection by Best Western) — Comfortable 4-star with spacious rooms, free Wi-Fi/parking, restaurant, and convenient access (near highway but usable for the city). Good value ratings and consistent positive feedback.

  • Vogue Hotel — Central 4-star with solid scores (often 8.8 range), modern comforts, and good location feedback.

Other Notables

  • B&B Antiche Mura or similar historic B&Bs near the Duomo for a more intimate, characterful stay (high guest scores for location and charm).
  • Agriturismi or countryside options (e.g., properties with pools/jacuzzis a short drive away) if you prefer a rural Tuscan vibe while still near Arezzo.

Tips:

  • For the historic center and walkability, prioritize Continentale, La Corte del Re, Sugar Rooms, or similar.
  • For luxury and views/peace, choose Badia di Pomaio (car or taxi recommended).
  • Many properties offer free Wi-Fi, air-conditioning, and breakfast; parking can be limited in the center.
  • Arezzo is compact—most central hotels put you within easy walking distance of the main sights. Book ahead for peak seasons or events like the Giostra del Saracino or antique fair.

Prices vary widely by season (expect higher in summer and for luxury properties). Compare current rates and recent reviews on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or Hotels.com for the latest details. Enjoy Arezzo!
