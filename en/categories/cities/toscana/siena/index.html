The best hotels in Siena (Tuscany, Italy) combine historic charm, strong guest ratings (typically 9.0+/10 on Booking.com or 4.5+/5 on TripAdvisor), prime locations near Piazza del Campo or the Duomo, and excellent service. Top picks draw from sources like The Hotel Guru, U.S. News, TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and Hotels.com (as of 2026 data).

Siena’s compact historic center favors walkable properties; countryside options offer pools and quiet. Prices vary widely by season (higher during Palio races in July/August); book ahead and check current rates/reviews.

Luxury / Special Occasion

  • Grand Hotel Continental Siena – Starhotels Collezione (5-star): Siena’s standout five-star in a 17th-century palazzo near Piazza del Campo. Features frescoed ceilings, Murano chandeliers, period furnishings, and strong service (e.g., arranging experiences). Consistently high ratings (~9.2/10). Ideal for grandeur in the heart of the city.
  • Hotel Certosa di Maggiano (often rated ~9.6–9.7/10): Converted 14th-century Carthusian monastery just outside the walls (~1–2 km / short drive or taxi from the center). Peaceful gardens, outdoor pool, excellent restaurant, attentive staff, and historic interiors. Frequently praised as one of the most special stays near Siena.

Boutique / Central Historic

  • Il Battistero (Residenza d’Epoca): Intimate restored historic palazzo (just 7 rooms/suites) directly opposite the Baptistery of San Giovanni, steps from the Duomo and Piazza del Campo. Elegant rooms with original details, luxurious bathrooms, views, and on-site bistrot/enoteca. Highly recommended for atmosphere and location.
  • Palazzo Ravizza: Family-run historic pensione/hotel in the center with old-world elegance (tiled floors, beams, vaulted ceilings), a lovely garden with valley views, and good parking. Strong ratings (~9.0–9.4/10) and frequent top-list mentions for charm and convenience.
  • Campo Regio Relais: Romantic B&B-style with antique-filled rooms and excellent views (including of the Duomo from some terraces). Small and atmospheric.

Other Strong Contenders (Often Highly Rated)

  • Hotel Athena (4-star): Reliable central option near the historic core with solid facilities, breakfast, and frequent high scores (~9.0–9.4/10) across platforms. Good for practicality.
  • Sangallo Park Hotel: Popular for location, pool/views, breakfast, and value; strong TripAdvisor presence.
  • Smaller or modern options like Torre del Fuggisole also earn excellent guest praise for friendliness and cleanliness.

Tips

  • Location matters: Stay inside or very near the walls for easy walking to the Duomo, Piazza del Campo, and Torre del Mangia. Countryside hotels (like Certosa) require a short trip but reward with space and calm.
  • Best times: Shoulder seasons (May–June, September–October) for milder weather and fewer crowds. Avoid peak summer heat if possible; book early for Palio dates.
  • Practical notes: Many historic properties lack elevators. Parking can be limited/restricted in the center—confirm options. Florence or Pisa airports are the main gateways (roughly 1–1.5 hours by car).
  • Always verify latest reviews, availability, and any current renovations on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotel sites, as ratings and offerings can shift.
