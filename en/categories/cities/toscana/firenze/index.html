«what are the best hotels in Firenze?»

ChatGPT · gpt-5-6

If by “best” you mean excellent luxury, location, service, and atmosphere, Florence (Firenze) has several standout choices. Current 2026 guides particularly highlight the Four Seasons, St. Regis, Villa San Michele, and several historic palazzi.

My shortlist

  • Four Seasons Hotel Firenze - The grandest option: two Renaissance palazzi, a huge private garden, pool, spa, and Michelin-starred dining. It's also Florence's only current Three-Key MICHELIN hotel.
  • The St. Regis Florence - Classic, ultra-luxurious Florentine palazzo on the Arno, with a particularly grand old-world atmosphere.
  • Hotel Lungarno - Excellent if you want Ponte Vecchio/Arno views and a more intimate luxury experience.
  • Antica Torre di Via Tornabuoni 1 - Boutique luxury right in the historic center, with a rooftop terrace and excellent shopping location.
  • Brunelleschi Hotel - Fantastic for being immersed in Florence's historic center, just steps from the Duomo and tucked into historic buildings.
  • Hotel Savoy - Right on Piazza della Repubblica; glamorous, central, and one of Florence's classic luxury addresses. Time Out currently identifies it as its “most iconic” choice.
  • Hotel Palazzo Guadagni - A more characterful choice in Santo Spirito, especially appealing if you want a neighborhood feel and rooftop views.
  • Palazzo Firenze by Baglioni Hotels & Resorts - Historic-palazzo atmosphere with a very high-end feel.

If you want something outside the center

Belmond Villa San Michele is particularly special: it's in the hills below Fiesole rather than in central Florence, with sweeping city views, gardens, and a historic monastery setting. It underwent a major renovation and reopened in spring 2026.

For a first trip to Florence, I'd narrow it to:

  • Four Seasons → maximum luxury + gardens
  • St. Regis → classic grand-hotel experience
  • Hotel Lungarno → Arno/Ponte Vecchio + intimate luxury
  • Brunelleschi → best for being right in the historic action
  • Villa San Michele → romantic, tranquil, spectacular views

Four Seasons Hotel Firenze is the most consistently top-ranked luxury hotel in Florence (Firenze), followed closely by other high-end properties like Portrait Firenze, Hotel Savoy, Hotel Lungarno, The St. Regis Florence, and Helvetia & Bristol. Newer standouts include Collegio alla Querce.

Florence is compact, so central locations near the Duomo, Uffizi, Ponte Vecchio, or Arno River put you within easy walking distance of major sights. Many top hotels occupy restored Renaissance palazzi or historic buildings.

Other strong luxury or near-luxury options include Villa La Massa (slightly outside the center, high guest scores), Il Salviatino (gardens and views), Bernini Palace (excellent guest ratings and central location), and Hotel Calimala (modern, well-located four-star with strong reviews).

Boutique / Stylish Alternatives

  • SoprArno Suites, Riva Lofts, Hotel La Gemma, Antica Torre di Via Tornabuoni, and The James — characterful, design-focused, or historic tower stays with good locations and high praise for atmosphere.

Practical Tips

  • Budget/mid-range high performers (still well-reviewed): Look at places like Hotel Accademia, IQ Hotel Firenze, or Social Hub properties if you want solid quality without five-star prices. Guest scores on major sites often run 9+ for well-located mid-tier options.
  • Areas: Historic center (Duomo/Signoria) for maximum walkability (noisy and pricey); Oltrarno or riverside for charm and views; slightly out (Fiesole hills or northeast) for space, gardens, and quieter nights.
  • When to book: Rates at top hotels frequently start €700–1,100+ in peak season and can climb higher for suites or high-demand dates. Shoulder seasons (spring/fall) or midweek are better value.
  • Rankings draw from recent guides (U.S. News, Time Out, Condé Nast Traveler, Michelin Keys, Lonely Planet, specialist luxury roundups) and guest review aggregates. Preferences vary by priorities (views vs. gardens vs. absolute centrality vs. newness).

For the absolute “best,” most expert and luxury-focused sources currently point to the Four Seasons as the benchmark, with Portrait Firenze and Hotel Lungarno as top alternatives for location and style. Check current availability and reviews on major platforms, as openings and renovations (e.g., Collegio alla Querce, Villa San Michele updates) continue to refresh the scene.
