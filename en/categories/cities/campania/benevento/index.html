Grok · grok-3

B&B Domus Traiani, Hotel Villa Traiano, and Antum Hotel stand out among the top options in Benevento (Campania, Italy), based on recent guest ratings from TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites (as of 2025–2026 data). Benevento is a compact historic city, so many highly rated stays are small B&Bs or boutique hotels near landmarks like the Arch of Trajan, rather than large international chains.

Top Recommendations

Here’s a summary of the strongest performers:

  • B&B Domus Traiani (often ranked #1 on TripAdvisor)
    Near the Arch of Trajan (short walk), quiet yet central location. Guests consistently praise the warm hospitality (hosts like Adele are frequently mentioned), spotless and comfortable rooms, excellent breakfast (often with a view/terrace), and value. Ratings typically 4.9/5 on TripAdvisor (hundreds of reviews) and ~9.2–9.6/10 on Booking.com. Ideal for a personal, well-located stay focused on the historic center.

  • Hotel Villa Traiano (4-star boutique)
    Elegant early-20th-century villa right by the Arch of Trajan (about 50 meters away), with gardens, rooftop views/SPA, restaurant, and modern rooms (some with design furnishings like Poltrona Frau and amenities such as BVLGARI). Strong for location, atmosphere, and facilities; ratings often around 4.1/5 on TripAdvisor and 8.0–8.6/10 elsewhere. Good choice if you want a more hotel-like experience with spa/restaurant options and private parking (fee applies).

  • Antum Hotel (4-star)
    Modern/renovated property with seasonal outdoor pool, garden, restaurant (Ama Experience, often praised), bar, and free private parking. Convenient (walkable to the center, near the station area). Guests highlight cleanliness, staff, pool area, and overall comfort. Ratings commonly 8.6–8.9/10 on Booking.com and around 4.1/5 on TripAdvisor. Excellent for relaxation or if you want on-site dining and a pool.

Other Strong Contenders

  • Piazza Roma Rooms and various highly rated B&Bs (e.g., SOTTARCO B&Benevento, B&B Comfort Room 93, La Botte Piccola, or B&B gigi) — Often score 9.4–9.8/10 on Booking.com for location, cleanliness, and host friendliness. These suit travelers preferring apartments or smaller, characterful stays.
  • Agriturismo Parva Vinea (or similar countryside options nearby) — Perfect 5/5 scores in some rankings for a more rural, peaceful experience if you’re okay being slightly outside the center.

Quick Tips

  • Best for historic center access: Domus Traiani or Villa Traiano.
  • Best amenities (pool/restaurant/SPA): Antum or Villa Traiano.
  • Budget/value: Many well-reviewed B&Bs start lower than the 4-star hotels.
  • Prices vary by season and dates (roughly from ~€50–150+ per night for good options); check Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or hotel sites for current rates, availability, and exact reviews. Parking is available at several (sometimes free or for a fee).
  • Benevento’s center is walkable, so proximity to the Arch of Trajan, Roman Theatre, and Duomo is a big plus for most visitors.

Ratings and rankings can shift with new reviews—always verify the latest on major booking platforms for your travel dates. Enjoy Benevento!
