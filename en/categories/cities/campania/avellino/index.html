Gemini

The best hotels and boutique accommodations in Avellino offer a mix of full-service luxury, convenient city-center locations, and charming local bed-and-breakfasts. Depending on whether you prefer a traditional full-amenity hotel, an elegant modern suite, or a peaceful countryside retreat, here are the top options in and around the city.

🏨 Premium Full-Service Hotels

Hotel de la Ville – The Top Full-Service Pick. If you want classic upscale amenities, this is the city's signature 4-star hotel . It features elegant rooms, mountain views, a highly rated refined restaurant, and a distinct heart-shaped outdoor pool. It sits just a short drive from the historic core, offering free parking and a quieter, spacious atmosphere.

Viva! Hotel Avellino – Best for City-Center Convenience. Located right on Via Circumvallazione, this modern 4-star hotel puts you within comfortable walking distance of Avellino’s central shops and restaurants. Some rooms feature private Jacuzzis and kitchenettes. It is highly practical if you want to explore the town on foot, though parking is handled via a cheap public lot nearby.

⚜️ Elegant Boutique & Luxury Guest Houses

Le Suite Borboniche Luxury House – Best for a High-End Boutique Experience. Tucked into the city center on Via Giuseppe Verdi, this property boasts flawless traveler reviews for its immaculate design and excellent location. It provides premium room service, air conditioning, and a more intimate, exclusive feel than the larger hotels.

Lungofiume Suite – Best Modern Design. This highly rated guest suite offers stylish, clean, and contemporary design elements within walking distance of the main local attractions. It is an excellent middle-ground if you want hotel-level cleanliness with the privacy of a luxury apartment.

🌳 Charming B&Bs and Countryside Escapes

Liguorini House B&B – Best Hospitality & Mountain Views. Located just slightly outside the bustling core, this exceptionally rated bed and breakfast is famous for its panoramic mountain vistas and warm, personalized hospitality. It is a perfect base if you have a rental car and intend to tour the surrounding Irpinia wine region.

Hotel BelSito Avellino Est – Best for Highway Access & Regional Exploring. Located slightly east of town in nearby Manocalzati, this 4-star property features classic rooms, an outdoor pool, a hot tub, and a dedicated business center. Because it sits right off the main transit corridors, it serves as a great launchpad for day trips across Campania.

Quick Tips

  • Best overall / central: Hotel De La Ville or Viva Hotel.
  • Best with pool/spa & parking: Bel Sito Due Torri or Grand Hotel Irpinia.
  • Budget / boutique feel: Look at the highly rated B&Bs (many under €100–150/night depending on season).
  • Prices typically start from ~€70–150+ per night (varies by season and dates). Free Wi-Fi and parking are common.
  • Avellino is a good base for exploring Irpinia (wine, countryside, Montevergine) or as a stop between Naples and the east.
