Quick Tips

  • Location priorities: Stay near the station/historic center (Cavour area) for walking access to sights, or slightly outside for parking and quiet.
  • Prices: Expect roughly €50–120+ per night depending on season, dates, and room type (higher near events or weekends). Always check current availability.
  • Nearby alternatives: For more upscale or lakeside options, look toward Lake Orta, Lake Maggiore, or Malpensa Airport hotels (e.g., in Cameri or nearby towns), as pure luxury is limited inside Novara itself.
  • Ratings and availability change, so cross-check Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Google for your exact dates and recent reviews.
