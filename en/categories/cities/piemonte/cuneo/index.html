🔑 Key Considerations

  • Location: Hotels like Royal Superga and Phi Principe are right on Piazza Galimberti, perfect for exploring Cuneo’s historic center.
  • Style: Choose Phi Hotel Principe for upscale elegance, or Cuneo Hotel for simplicity and value.
  • Budget: B&B Hotel Cristal offers the lowest rates while maintaining modern comfort.
  • Boutique Charm: Contrada dei Giardini is ideal if you prefer a small, intimate stay with personalized service.

⚠️ Risks & Trade-offs

  • Parking: Central hotels may have limited parking; Cristal and Victoria are better for drivers.
  • Atmosphere: Larger chains (Best Western, B&B Hotel) provide consistency but less local character compared to boutique options.
  • Availability: Smaller B&Bs like Contrada dei Giardini can book out quickly due to limited rooms.

  • Hotel Victoria (3★) — Solid ~8.8–9.2 ratings, comfortable, convenient for shopping/downtown, easy parking.
  • Highly rated B&Bs and smaller properties (often 9.4–9.8 with fewer reviews): Casa Torre Allera, B&B Contrada dei Giardini, Tetto Nuovo B&B, La Loggia dei Mercanti, and similar central or near-centre options. These can feel more personal but may have limited services compared with full hotels.

Quick tips

  • Location — Most top-rated hotels sit in or very near the historic centre (Piazza Galimberti / Via Roma area), ideal for walking to restaurants, shops, and sights. A few (e.g., B&B Hotel Cuneo Cristal) are slightly farther with free parking.
  • Prices — Expect roughly €80–150+ per night for a double (varies by season, dates, and inclusions). Rates fluctuate; check current availability.
  • What guests praise most — Central location, cleanliness, staff friendliness, and breakfast quality. Noise from streets can occasionally be an issue in some rooms—request a quieter one if sensitive.
  • Practical notes — Parking is available at many (sometimes paid or limited in the historic centre). Cuneo is a good base for exploring the surrounding Alps, Langhe wine region, and Piedmont countryside.
