Other Notable Options

Quick Tips

  • Central Biella (Piano or near Piazzo) is ideal for walking to the cathedral, shops, restaurants, and the funicular up to the historic upper town.
  • Parking is a big plus in the top hotels (many offer free or private options).
  • Prices typically start around €70–150+ per night depending on season and dates; check current rates on Booking.com or Tripadvisor.
  • Biella is known for textiles/fashion heritage, local cheeses and Piedmont wines, and proximity to the Alps, the Sacred Mount of Oropa (UNESCO), and outdoor areas.
