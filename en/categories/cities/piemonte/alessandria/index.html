Quick Tips

  • Best overall central choice: Alli Due Buoi Rossi stands out for location + consistent positive feedback on staff, cleanliness, and comfort. Parking is usually nearby (often paid and worth booking ahead).
  • Best if you have a car: Al Mulino or Diamante — easier access, free/ample parking, and quieter.
  • Higher-rated alternatives nearby (province of Alessandria): Places like Capriccio Art Hotel (Serravalle Scrivia) or various country-house/relais options often score 9.0+ and are popular if you’re combining the city with the Serravalle Designer Outlet or wine areas.
