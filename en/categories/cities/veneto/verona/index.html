Here are some of the consistently top-rated hotels in Verona, based on recent guest reviews from Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Trip.com, and travel guides (ratings and popularity as of 2026 data).

Verona’s historic center (Città Antica) is the best area to stay—walkable to the Arena, Juliet’s House, Piazza delle Erbe, and most sights. Book early for summer opera season or peak periods, as central hotels fill up.

Luxury / 5-Star Highlights

  • Due Torri Hotel — Elegant historic 5-star (part of Leading Hotels of the World) in a great central location near Sant’Anastasia. Frequently praised for charm, service, and atmosphere. High ratings (around 9.2–9.3).
  • Vista Verona — Boutique 5-star in the heart of the old town (near Juliet’s balcony). Notable for design, spa/indoor pool, rooftop restaurant with views, and Michelin Key recognition. Strong guest scores (around 9.4).
  • Hotel Gabbia d’Oro — Opulent 5-star in an 18th-century palazzo on/near Piazza delle Erbe. Known for romantic maximalist style, original frescoes, and excellent breakfast in a winter garden/sun room.
  • NH Collection Palazzo Verona — 5-star built around Roman ruins with frescoes and historic details. Popular luxury choice with solid reviews.

Excellent Mid-Range / Highly Rated Favorites

  • Hotel Accademia (4-star) — Frequently called one of the best overall or mid-range picks. Excellent central location near the Arena and main sights, classic style in a historic building, strong service and breakfast. Consistently high scores (often 9.2+ with thousands of reviews).
  • Hotel Milano & SPA — Central (near Arena), rooftop views of the amphitheater, spa facilities, and good value for the location. Very popular on Tripadvisor.
  • Hotel Giulietta e Romeo — Steps from the Arena, modern/clean rooms (some for families), convenient and well-reviewed (around 9.1–9.4).
  • Escalus Luxury Suites — Intimate all-suite option near Juliet’s House with private terraces and romantic feel; high ratings.
  • Opera Relais de Charme (or similar Opera properties) — Often ranks very high for quiet, charming stays with excellent scores (frequently 4.9/5 on Tripadvisor).

Unique / Romantic Options

  • Relais Balcone di Giulietta — Inside Juliet’s House courtyard; some rooms overlook the famous balcony, with after-hours courtyard access. Highly rated for the unique experience.
  • Relais Ponte Pietra or Relais Villa dei Gelsi & Spa — Charming, high-scoring properties (some with spa/pool elements); good for a quieter or more secluded feel while still accessible.

Practical Tips

  • Best location — Stay in the historic center for walking everywhere. Near Porta Nuova station is more practical for trains/cars but requires a short walk or transport to the main sights.
  • Budget note — Central mid-range hotels often start in the mid-to-high €100s–€200s per night (varies widely by season); true luxury can be significantly higher.
  • Other strong mentions — Relais Fra’ Lorenzo (hillside with pool/views), Hotel Indigo Verona – Grand Hotel Des Arts, and various well-reviewed boutique apartments or guesthouses.

Prices, availability, and exact ratings fluctuate, so check current reviews and deals on Tripadvisor, Booking.com, or the hotel sites. For the absolute “best,” prioritize recent guest feedback on location, cleanliness, and staff, as Verona’s top properties excel in those areas.
