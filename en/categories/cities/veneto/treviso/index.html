🏨 Key Considerations

  • Boutique charm vs. full-service: Treviso is rich in boutique B&Bs with historic character (Locanda San Tomaso, Trevisi27), while larger hotels like Best Western Premier BHR offer spa and dining facilities.
  • Budget range: Options start around US$88 (B&B HOTEL Treviso) and go up to US$181 (Little Garden).
  • Location: Most B&Bs are in the historic center, ideal for walking tours. The Best Western is slightly outside Treviso, better for those with a car.
  • Atmosphere: Choose boutique B&Bs for intimacy and design, or larger hotels for convenience and amenities.

⚠️ Practical Tips

  • Seasonality: Prices rise in summer; autumn and spring offer better deals.
  • Availability: Boutique B&Bs often have limited rooms—book early.
  • Transport: If you plan to explore Veneto (Venice, Padua), staying near Treviso’s train station or choosing a hotel with parking is wise.
  • Hidden costs: Check for city tax (usually €1–€2 per night per person).

  • For luxury boutique: B&B Barberia or Little Garden.
