The top-rated hotels in Vicenza (Veneto, Italy) emphasize central locations near Palladian architecture and the historic center, strong guest scores (typically 8.8–9.4/10 on Booking.com or 4.4–4.7/5 on Tripadvisor), cleanliness, staff service, and breakfast. Ratings and popularity draw from Booking.com, Tripadvisor, Trivago, and similar sites as of recent data.

Vicenza lacks many large luxury resorts but excels in boutique properties, historic palazzi, and charming relais in or near the center. Prices vary by season (often €100–200+ per night for mid-to-upscale doubles); check current rates on Booking.com or similar. Here are standout options:

Top Central/Boutique Picks

  • Relais Santa Corona (Contrà Santa Corona 19): Frequently tops lists with ~9.2–9.3/10 (Booking) and 4.7/5 (Tripadvisor). Restored 18th-century building in a quiet central spot near Corso Palladio and attractions like Teatro Olimpico. Praised for modern rooms with high ceilings, excellent staff, cleanliness, and solid breakfast. Ideal for a boutique feel between a B&B and hotel.

  • Palazzo Scamozzi (near Piazza Castello / Corso Palladio): High scores around 8.8–9.4/10 and 4.7/5. Historic palazzo in a prime pedestrian-center location, walkable to the train station and sights. Guests highlight stylish renovated rooms, helpful multilingual staff, good breakfast, and quiet atmosphere. Great for immersion in the historic core.

  • The Glam Boutique Hotel & Apt (Viale Antonio Giuriolo 10): ~9.1/10 and 4.7/5. Stylish boutique near Teatro Olimpico and Palazzo Chiericati with unique modern/artsy decor, comfortable rooms, strong staff service, and solid breakfast. Design-focused and central.

  • Antica Pusterla Relais de Charme (Contrà San Francesco area): Often 9.3–9.4/10. Luxury-feeling relais in a historic setting with elegant rooms, spa/wellness options (sauna, steam, etc.), garden/terrace, and high praise for service and breakfast. Slightly quieter edge-of-center vibe with a refined, romantic atmosphere.

Reliable Mid-to-Upscale Favorites

  • Hotel Campo Marzio (Viale Roma 21): Popular 4-star (~8.7–8.8/10, 4.4/5 on Tripadvisor with many reviews). Very central (near train station and pedestrian zone), spacious rooms (some with garden/city views), good breakfast, free bike hire, and helpful staff. Convenient base with parking options; often ranked highly for value and accessibility.

  • Antico Hotel Vicenza: Solid 4-star scores (~8.6–9.0/10). Characterful period property in the center with good location near main sights, efficient staff, and comfortable rooms. Frequent top-list mention.

Other Notables

  • High-scoring smaller or apartment-style options like Antica Pusterla variants, Residenza Vescovado, Country Home B&B Il Melo, or Villa Ghislanzoni (often 9+ scores) for more intimate or countryside-adjacent stays.
  • Nearby villa-style: Hotel Villa Michelangelo or similar for pool/garden settings outside the tight historic core.
  • Practical 4-stars like SHG Hotel De La Ville or Hotel Victoria for business/value travelers (slightly lower scores but reliable amenities).

Tips: Prioritize central locations for walking to Basilica Palladiana, Piazza dei Signori, and Palladio sites. Book ahead for peak seasons or events. Many offer parking (paid/private) or are near the train station. Read recent reviews for specifics on noise, breakfast variety, or accessibility, as smaller properties vary. For the absolute latest availability and prices, search Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Google Hotels with your dates. Enjoy Vicenza’s architecture and cuisine!
