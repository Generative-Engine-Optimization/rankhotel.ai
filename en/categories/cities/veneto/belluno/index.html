Belluno (in Veneto’s Dolomites foothills) has a relatively small selection of hotels. Higher-rated stays often mix classic hotels with agriturismi (farm stays) and B&Bs. Ratings and popularity can vary by platform and date, but these stand out for strong overall scores, location, amenities, and guest feedback (as of recent 2025–2026 data).

Top Recommendations

  • Park Hotel Villa Carpenada (Via Mier 158) — Frequently tops lists. A converted historic villa (often rated ~9.0–9.4/10 on Booking and ~4.4/5 on Tripadvisor) with mountain/Dolomites views, gardens, free parking, restaurant (praised for meals on the terrace), and spa elements. Guests highlight friendly multilingual staff, spacious characterful rooms with wooden beams, excellent breakfast, and a peaceful setting a short drive from the center. Ideal for a more upscale, relaxing stay.

  • Antico Fienile (Via Pittanzella 119) — Often one of the highest-rated (~9.6/10 Exceptional on Booking with hundreds of reviews). A charming farm stay/agriturismo in a historic building with mountain views, garden, terrace, free parking, and restaurant featuring local products. Guests love the warm hospitality (hosts + friendly dog Mimmo), cleanliness, comfort, breakfast, and tranquil countryside setting (a short drive from town). Great for nature lovers and those seeking authenticity after Dolomites activities.

  • Albergo Delle Alpi (Via Jacopo Tasso 13) — Solid central 3-star option (~8.6–8.7/10 Excellent; strong location scores ~9.4). Near the train station and city center, with renovated rooms, free Wi-Fi, air conditioning, and good breakfast. Praised for cleanliness, helpful staff, convenience for walkers/hikers, and value. Pet-friendly in some reports. Practical base for exploring Belluno and nearby ski/hike areas.

  • Suites Hotel Astor (Piazza dei Martiri 26/E) — Modern 4-star central hotel (~8.7–8.9/10; ~4.5/5 on Tripadvisor). Spacious suites (some with mountain views or office areas), spa/wellness (hot tub, sauna, massage), bar/restaurant with views, free Wi-Fi, and breakfast. Guests note good location near the cathedral/historic center, clean modern rooms, and amenities. Convenient for city stays with some luxury touches.

  • Albergo Cappello e Cadore (Via Sebastiano Ricci 8) — Historic central 3-star (~8.3–8.5/10 Very Good; popular for hikers). Near the main square, theater, and station. Comfortable rooms, breakfast, and luggage storage noted positively. Family-run feel; good for short stays or post-hike. Some reviews mention it as a reliable, characterful budget-to-midrange choice.

Other Notable Mentions

  • Vera Park & Wellness — Wellness-focused with good reviews for location and facilities.
  • Agriturismo Casa de Bertoldi or Agriturismo Modolo — Rustic farm stays with high ratings for authenticity, food, and countryside setting (pet-friendly options available).
  • Smaller/high-rated B&Bs or apartments (e.g., Casa 1885, various Corte Varola-style places) often score 9+ for personal service and location if you prefer non-hotel stays.

Tips

Prices typically start around €70–150+/night depending on season, room type, and dates (higher in peak summer or ski season; check current rates on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Kayak). Many offer free parking or are walkable to the center/station. Belluno is a gateway to the Dolomiti Bellunesi National Park, so properties with mountain views or easy access to trails/parking are popular. Book ahead for peak periods, and verify pet policies, spa access, or restaurant hours if important.
