Here are some of the consistently highest-rated hotels in Bergamo, Italy, based on recent traveler reviews from sources like Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Hotels.com, and specialist guides (as of 2025–2026 data). Bergamo splits into the atmospheric medieval Città Alta (Upper Town) and the more modern Città Bassa (Lower Town). Many top picks favor the Upper Town for charm or convenient spots near the train station/funicular.

Top Boutique & Historic Picks (especially in or near Città Alta)

  • GombitHotel (Città Alta) — Frequently tops “best of” lists. A stylish 13th-century property (around 13 rooms) in a medieval square near Piazza Vecchia, blending historic stone/exposed brick with modern design, quirky art, and excellent service. Ratings often 9.3–9.8/10. Great for atmosphere and walkability; breakfast is well-regarded.

  • Relais San Vigilio al Castello — Elegant 9-room boutique hotel high on San Vigilio hill (above Città Alta, near the castle). Historic buildings (from ~1630) with gardens, panoramic views over the Upper Town and plains, refined rooms, terrace, and on-site restaurant. Ratings around 9.4–9.8/10. Ideal for romance or peaceful stays with views.

  • Fuori Porta House (Città Alta) — Small (6 rooms), stylish boutique near Porta Sant’Alessandro/Piazza Vecchia area. Contemporary-historic mix, rooftop terrace, fusion restaurant/bar. Strong reviews for location, design, and service (often 9.3–9.4+).

  • Petronilla – Hotel in Bergamo (Città Bassa, historic San Lazzaro area) — Boutique favorite often ranking #1 or very high on Tripadvisor (around 4.9/5 or 9.4/10 on Booking). Unique art-filled rooms, outstanding staff, excellent homemade breakfast (courtyard in summer), plus sauna/hot tub/gym. Walkable to lower-town sights and transport; highly praised for hospitality.

Strong Central / Convenient Options

  • Hotel Excelsior San Marco (Città Bassa, Piazza della Repubblica) — Popular 4-star with strong overall scores (around 8.9–9.2/10 and high volume of reviews). Rooftop restaurant with views, spacious rooms, solid breakfast, excellent location near funicular to Città Alta, shops, and transport. Reliable and well-placed for exploring both parts of the city.

  • Mercure Bergamo Centro Palazzo Dolci — Modern 4-star near the train station. Clean rooms, good breakfast, convenient for arrivals/departures and lower-town access (ratings often ~8.4–9.2).

Other Notable Mentions

  • Radisson Blu Hotel, Bergamo Choruslife — Newer/modern option with high scores (often 9+), comfortable rooms, and good facilities.
  • Smaller highly rated spots or B&Bs in Città Alta (e.g., Palazzo Porta Picta, Al San Lorenzo, or various guest houses) frequently score 9.5–10 for intimacy and location, though inventory is limited.
  • For food-focused stays, look into places linked to strong restaurants (e.g., options near Relais da Vittorio outside the center).

Tips:
Città Alta is car-restricted and atmospheric (best reached by funicular from the lower town)—book early for peak season or weekends. Prices vary widely by date (mid-range often €150–300+/night); check current availability on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or hotel sites. Bergamo is compact and walkable, with the airport (Orio al Serio) well-connected by bus.
