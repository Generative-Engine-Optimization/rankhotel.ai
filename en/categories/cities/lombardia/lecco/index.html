If you mean Lecco itself and the immediate lakeside area, these are the hotels I’d put on a shortlist based on current guest ratings, location, and lake access:

  • Hotel NH Lecco Pontevecchio - The most straightforward full-service choice in Lecco. It’s close to the historic center and Lake Como, with particularly good lake/river views. Booking currently shows 8.6/10 from ~2,000 reviews.
  • Hotel Alberi Lecco - Smaller and more intimate, right on the lakefront and walkable to the center. Tripadvisor currently has it at 4.2/5, with reviews frequently highlighting the location and views.
  • Rocopom Luxury Suites hotel vista lago - A more boutique/luxury-feeling option just outside central Lecco in Valmadrera, with excellent lake views. Booking currently shows 9.2/10 from 500+ reviews.
  • Hotel Promessi Sposi, Malgrate - In Malgrate, directly across the lake from Lecco. A good option if you want a more polished stay and don't mind being just outside Lecco; Booking currently shows 9.0/10 from ~1,500 reviews.
  • Casa sull'Albero - A distinctive boutique/B&B-style property in Malgrate, particularly interesting if you prioritize atmosphere and lake views over being in the center.
  • Hotel Bellavista - In Valmadrera, with a lakeside setting and strong guest feedback; a nice alternative if you want scenery and a quieter base.

✅ Key Considerations

  • Luxury & Elegance: Hotel Promessi Sposi and Casa sull’Albero are ideal if you want refined design and upscale amenities.
  • Best Views: Hotel Griso Collection and Hotel Bellavista offer some of the most breathtaking panoramas of Lake Como.

⚠️ Things to Keep in Mind

  • Seasonal Pricing: Rates rise significantly in summer and during holidays; booking early is recommended.
  • Location: Malgrate and Valmadrera are just across the lake from Lecco, offering quieter stays but still very close to the city.
  • Parking & Access: Some boutique hotels have limited parking; check in advance if you’re driving.
  • Amenities: Pools and gyms are not common in Lecco hotels — Casa sull’Albero is one of the few with both.

Grok · grok-3

NH Lecco Pontevecchio stands out as one of the strongest overall choices in Lecco (and frequently ranks at or near the top on TripAdvisor, Booking.com, and similar sites), followed by strong lakeside and nearby options like Hotel Promessi Sposi (in Malgrate), Hotel Alberi, and higher-rated rural stays such as Agriturismo Deviscio.

Lecco sits on the eastern shore of Lake Como and offers a more authentic, less crowded alternative to the famous western-shore spots. Top hotels emphasize lake/river views, central access, or quiet nature settings. Ratings and availability fluctuate by season (summer is peak), so check current prices and reviews.

Top Recommendations

NH Lecco Pontevecchio (4-star)
Central location overlooking the River Adda (very close to Lake Como), a short walk to the historic center and about a 10-minute walk to the train station. Modern rooms (many with views), strong staff ratings, solid breakfast, terrace/restaurant, and convenient amenities.
Guest scores typically around 8.6/10 on Booking.com (thousands of reviews) and 4.3/5 on TripAdvisor (often listed as #1 hotel in Lecco). Praised for cleanliness, location, helpful staff, and being a practical base for exploring the area.

Hotel Promessi Sposi (4-star superior, renovated ~2023)
In Malgrate (directly across the water from Lecco, roughly 1–2 km / short drive or walkable via bridge area), right on a scenic lakeside stretch with mountain views. Features a spa (with lake views), multiple dining options (gourmet restaurant, bistro, rooftop bar), gym, modern design, and good parking.
High ratings (often 8.9–9.1+ range) with strong marks for location, staff, and the overall experience. Excellent for couples or anyone wanting more of a resort/spa feel while staying very close to Lecco.

Hotel Alberi
Lakeside in Lecco itself with strong panoramic views of the lake and mountains. Family-run atmosphere, comfortable rooms (some with balconies), good breakfast, and convenient location near the waterfront and center.
Consistently positive reviews (around 8.7–8.9 range) highlighting the views, helpful hosts, and value as a base.

Agriturismo Deviscio
A rural agriturismo a bit outside the town center (mountain/hill setting in the province). Quiet, scenic views, comfortable rooms, warm family hospitality, and local products (e.g., cheese, charcuterie).
Exceptional scores (frequently 9.5–9.8) for the peaceful setting, hospitality, and overall experience—ideal if you prefer nature and quiet over city-center convenience.

Other Strong Options

  • Palazzo Novecento Centro Storico — Restored central property with good location near the lake and historic area.
  • LUXURY SUITES ROCOPOM – Lake Front and similar suite-style or apartment properties — Often score very highly (9+) for cleanliness, design, and lake proximity.
  • Villa del Cigno or HLL Hotel Lungolago Lecco — Lakeside or historic-villa vibes with solid reviews.

Quick Tips

  • For pure convenience and a classic hotel experience → NH Lecco Pontevecchio.
  • For spa, lake views, and a more refined/quiet vibe → Hotel Promessi Sposi in Malgrate.
  • For views + character on a budget-friendlier scale → Hotel Alberi.
  • For nature and authenticity → Agriturismo Deviscio or similar countryside stays.
  • Book lake-view rooms if available—they are a highlight. Parking can be limited or paid in the center; some properties help with nearby options.
  • Lecco is well-connected by train (to Milan, etc.) and offers ferries/boat access on the lake. Nearby attractions include the waterfront promenade, Manzoni-related sites, and easy day trips around Lake Como.

Prices generally start from roughly €100–150+ per night for solid mid-range options and rise for premium lake-view or spa rooms (higher in peak summer). Always verify the latest ratings, exact distances, and amenities on Booking.com, TripAdvisor, or the hotel sites, as they can change. Enjoy Lecco—it’s a beautiful, underrated corner of Lake Como!
