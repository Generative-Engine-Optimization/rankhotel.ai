Practical tips

  • Style of stays: Expect personal, often family-run places with free parking, Wi-Fi, and mountain or garden views. Many suit nature lovers, hikers, or those exploring the Dolomites and Belluno area. Spas or jacuzzis appear at a few (e.g., GIAMI, some Casas).
  • Ratings context: Scores come from Booking, Tripadvisor, Kayak, and similar sites. Properties with fewer reviews can still score highly due to strong individual feedback on cleanliness, hosts, and location.
  • Booking advice: Check current availability and exact prices on Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or Kayak, as they vary by dates. Free cancellation is often available. The area is quieter than major Dolomites resorts.
  • Other Santa Giustinas: There is a Santa Giustina area near Rimini (beach hotels) and Santa Giustina in Colle (near Padua), but searches for “best hotels in Santa Giustina” overwhelmingly return the Belluno location.
