Savoca (a small medieval hilltop village in eastern Sicily near Taormina, known for The Godfather filming locations) has limited traditional hotels. Most options are boutique properties, a small resort, or high-end B&Bs/villas with strong guest reviews for atmosphere, views, hospitality, and location. Here are the standout choices based on traveler ratings and mentions across Tripadvisor, Booking.com, Kayak, Fodor’s, and similar sources:

Top Picks in Savoca

  • Resort Borgo San Rocco (most frequently recommended as the village’s main “destination” hotel)
    A 4-star resort created from restored medieval houses in the Borgo San Rocco hamlet. Highlights include panoramic sea/mountain/Etna views, a swimming pool on a grassy terrace, an on-site restaurant serving local Sicilian food, warm family-style hospitality (owner Vittorio and staff are frequently praised), and a peaceful setting.
    Ratings are consistently high (around 4.8/5 on Tripadvisor with 580+ reviews; ~9.2–9.4/10 elsewhere). Guests love the unique character, views, and staff; some note the hillside access involves a steep/winding drive or walk to the pool. Pet-friendly in many cases. Ideal for a special or romantic stay.

  • Bar Vitelli Charming Suites
    Boutique property in a structure dating to the 1400s (right by the famous Bar Vitelli from The Godfather). Fully updated with modern luxury touches (marble bathrooms, high-end toiletries, hydromassage showers/soaking tubs), antiques, private balconies in some rooms, a pool, and wine tastings in a historic cellar.
    Fodor’s highlights it as a top choice in Savoca. Ratings are excellent (often 9.7–9.9/10 or 5/5), though with fewer reviews due to its smaller size. Great for atmosphere and movie fans.

  • Lemon House
    Highly praised B&B/villa-style accommodation with a pool, garden, and excellent hosts (Christel is frequently mentioned for helpful local advice and hospitality). Peaceful setting, strong breakfasts, and near-perfect scores (often 5/5 or 10/10 with dozens of reviews). A strong all-around option for comfort and relaxation.

Other Well-Rated Options

  • Dolce Casa n41 — Peaceful garden retreat with homemade Sicilian breakfast and outstanding hospitality (high ratings around 9.4–9.7/10 or 5/5).
  • Pentefur / Pentefur Suite Savoca — Solid reviews (around 9.5), parking, and pet-friendly options.
  • B&B Il Padrino, Verdello Home, Il Belvedere, and various villas/apartments (e.g., Villa Laura, La Casina di Venere) — Smaller properties with strong guest feedback for location, cleanliness, and views; many score 4.8–5/5.

Practical Tips

Savoca is compact and scenic but hilly with limited services, so a car is highly recommended for arriving and exploring (narrow roads; some properties have parking). It’s a short drive from Taormina and coastal beaches. Many visitors stay in Savoca for 1–2 nights for the authentic village experience, then base themselves in Taormina for more hotel variety and amenities.

Prices and availability vary seasonally (higher in summer); check recent reviews and book via Booking.com, Tripadvisor, or the property sites for current rates, exact amenities, and cancellation policies. For luxury with more facilities, consider nearby Taormina options (e.g., Belmond Grand Hotel Timeo or similar) and day-trip to Savoca.
